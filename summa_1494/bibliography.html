Bibliography & Cited Works¶

Primary Incunable, Classical, Medieval & Early Modern Sources Cited in the 1494 Summa

Authoritative register of primary treatises and editions referenced across Fra Luca Pacioli’s Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita (Venice, 1494) and the GEOMETOR critical commentary.

Al-Kitāb al-mukhtaṣar fī ḥisāb al-jabr wa’l-muqābala — al-Khwārizmī, Muḥammad ibn Mūsā (c. 780 – c. 850)¶

Author: al-Khwārizmī, Muḥammad ibn Mūsā (c. 780 – c. 850)

Locus / Imprint: Latin translation of Robert of Chester / Gerard of Cremona

De Re Aedificatoria Libri Decem — Alberti, Leon Battista (1404 – 1472)¶

Author: Alberti, Leon Battista (1404 – 1472)

Locus / Imprint: Florence, 1485

Author: Archimedes of Syracuse (c. 287 – 212 BC)

Locus / Imprint: Book I

Dimensio Circuli (On the Measurement of the Circle) — Archimedes of Syracuse (c. 287 – 212 BC)¶

Author: Archimedes of Syracuse (c. 287 – 212 BC)

Locus / Imprint: Propositions 1–3

De Institutione Arithmetica — Boethius, Anicius Manlius Severinus (c. 480 – 524 AD)¶

Author: Boethius, Anicius Manlius Severinus (c. 480 – 524 AD)

Locus / Imprint: Books I–II

De Institutione Musica — Boethius, Anicius Manlius Severinus (c. 480 – 524 AD)¶

Author: Boethius, Anicius Manlius Severinus (c. 480 – 524 AD)

Locus / Imprint: Books I–V

Author: Campanus of Novara (c. 1220 – 1296)

Locus / Imprint: Venice: Erhard Ratdolt, May 25, 1482

Locus / Imprint: Nuremberg: Johannes Petreius, 1545, Cap. I

Author: Euclid of Alexandria (fl. c. 300 BC)

Locus / Imprint: Books I–XIII (with Campanus Books XIV–XV)

De elementis arismetice artis — Jordanus Nemorarius (fl. early 13th century)¶

Author: Jordanus Nemorarius (fl. early 13th century)

Locus / Imprint: Books I–X

Liber Abaci — Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci, c. 1170 – c. 1250)¶

Author: Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci, c. 1170 – c. 1250)

Locus / Imprint: Pisa, 1202 / rev. 1228

Practica Geometriae — Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci, c. 1170 – c. 1250)¶

Author: Leonardo Pisano (Fibonacci, c. 1170 – c. 1250)

Locus / Imprint: Pisa, 1220
