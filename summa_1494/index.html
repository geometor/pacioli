Summa de Arithmetica, Geometria, Proportioni et Proportionalita¶

Fra Luca Pacioli (Venice, 1494) — Critical Multi-Track Digital Codex & Symbolic Edition

Author: Fra Luca Pacioli, O.F.M. (c. 1447–1517)
Printer: Paganino de' Paganini (Venice, November 1494)
Patrons: Guidobaldo da Montefeltro, Duke of Urbino & Marco Sanudo
First Edition: Venice: Paganino de’ Paganini (1494)
GEOMETOR Edition: 616-folio critical reconstruction · 9 interactive vector diagrams · Synchronized English, Italian, Summary, & Notes tracks

A folio-verified digital edition of Fra Luca Pacioli's encyclopedic Renaissance synthesis, Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita (Venice, November 10–20, 1494), uniting speculative arithmetic, Hindu–Arabic algorism, Cossic algebra (Regola dela Cosa), Boethian and Euclidean proportion theory, Venetian double-entry bookkeeping (Particularis de computis et scripturis), early probability (Problem of Points), and stereometry of the regular and Archimedean solids across four synchronized tracks: English, Italiano (1494), Summary, and Notes.

Table of Contents¶

  • Unit I — Preliminaries & Speculative Arithmetic (Prelim. fols. 1r–8v; Part I, Dist. I, fols. 1r–18v)

    • Continentia de tutta lopera, Dedications to Marco Sanudo & Duke Guidobaldo da Montefeltro, and Humanist Epigrams

    • Quadrivium Hierarchy & Debt to Leonardo Pisano

    • Polygonal Figurate Numbers & Generation of Perfect Numbers (Euclid IX.36, fol. 8r)

  • Unit II — Algorismus, Surds & Cossic Algebra (Arte Maggiore / Regola dela Cosa) (Part I, Dist. II–V & VIII, fols. 19r–67v, 112r–150r)

    • Eight Modes of Multiplication (scacchieri, gelosia), Galea Division, Regola del Tre & El Cataym (Double False Position)

    • Syncopated Cossic Powers (.co., .ce., .cu.), Operational Signs & Euclid Book X Binomials / Apotomes (X.36, X.73)

    • The Six Canonical Quadratic Equations (II.4–II.6, fols. 145r–146r) & Verdict on General Cubics (fol. 150r)

  • Unit III — Tractatus de Proportionibus et Proportionalitatibus (Part I, Dist. VI, fols. 68r–98r)

    • Praise of Proportion in Philosophy, Perspective (Piero della Francesca) & Architecture (Alberti, Vitruvius)

    • Five Boethian Genera, Semicircle Construction of the Three Means (VI.13, fol. 73v) & Six Euclidean Modi Arguendi (V.16–V.22)

    • Division of $10$ in Extreme and Mean Ratio ($\sqrt{125}-5$ and $15-\sqrt{125}$, Euclid II.11, XIII.1)

  • Unit IV — Mercantile Arithmetic, Problem of Points & Particularis de Computis et Scripturis (Part I, Dist. IX, fols. 150v–224v)

    • Partnerships (compagnie), Barter, Exchange & the Problem of Points (Balla & Balestrare, fol. 197r–197v)

    • Venetian Double-Entry Bookkeeping (fols. 198v–210v): Inventario, Memoriale, Giornale (Per // A) & Quaderno Grande

    • Cassa Non-Negativity, Skew-Symmetric Conservation ($F^T = -F$), Summa Summarum & Bilancio del Libro

  • Unit V — Tractatus Geometriae: Mensuration, Surface Division & Inscribed Polyhedra (Part II, Dist. I–VIII, fols. 1r–76r)

    • Heron's $(13, 14, 15)$ Triangle (Area $= 84$), Archimedes's $96$-Gon Circle Bounds & De divisione superficierum (VI.19)

    • Wine-Barrel Stereometry (botte) & Malfatti-Type Three-Circle Algebraic Packing (fol. 57r)

    • Regular & Archimedean Solids in the Sphere of Diameter $6$ (fols. 68v–70r) & Venetian Colophon (Nov. 10, 1494, fol. 76r)
