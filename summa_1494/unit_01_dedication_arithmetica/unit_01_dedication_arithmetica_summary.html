UNIT I — MATHEMATICAL & ARCHITECTURAL SUMMARY¶

Structural Digest of the Preliminaries & Part I, Distinctio I

1. Bibliographic & Dedicatory Architecture¶

  • Imprint: Venice, Paganino de' Paganini, November 10, 1494 (Part II colophon) and November 20, 1494 (Part I register), folio ([8], 224, 76 leaves).

  • Dual Patronage: - Courtly & Humanist: Guidobaldo da Montefeltro, Duke of Urbino ([prelim. fols. 2r–3r]). - Venetian Patrician & Scientific: Marco Sanudo ([prelim. fol. 1v]), whose financial support made the presswork and woodcut marginalia possible.

  • Default Source Convention (`Part I, fol. 1v`): Unattributed propositions and algorithms throughout the Summa derive from Leonardo Pisano's Liber Abaci and Practica Geometriae.

3. Figurate & Perfect Number Identities (fols. 6r–18v)¶

  • Polygonal Numbers: For $s$-gonal numbers of side rank $k \ge 1$,

    $$P(s, k) = \frac{(s - 2)k^2 - (s - 4)k}{2}, \qquad T_k = P(3, k) = \frac{k(k+1)}{2}$$

  • Euclid’s Even Perfect Number Formula (:just:`IX.36`, `fol. 8r`):

    $$M_p = 2^p - 1 \text{ prime} \implies P_p = 2^{p-1}(2^p - 1) \text{ satisfies } \sigma(P_p) = 2 P_p$$

    For $p \in \{2, 3, 5, 7, 13\}$, this yields $6$, $28$, $496$, $8128$, and $33{,}550{,}336$ ($p = 9$ and $p = 11$ fail because $2^9 - 1 = 511 = 7 \cdot 73$ and $2^{11} - 1 = 2047 = 23 \cdot 89$ are composite).
