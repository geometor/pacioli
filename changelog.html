0.4.4 (2026-11-15)¶

  • Restructured the Table of Contents and part overviews across De Divina Proportione (docsrc/de_divina_proportione/{index,compendio/index,architectura/index,plates/index}.rst) and Summa de Arithmetica (docsrc/summa_1494/index.rst) into clean hierarchical outlines (.. container:: toc-outline), resolving collapsed nested sub-lists, inline floating :just: badge fragmentation, and duplicate visible .. toctree:: lists while preserving Prev / Next book navigation.

0.4.3 (2026-11-14)¶

  • Standardized top-level About page (docsrc/about/index.rst promoted to top header :order: 10) with the three-pillar GEOMETOR semantic-extraction mission and archival provenance; retired docsrc/mission/ stubs cleanly into Home and About.

0.4.2 (2026-11-14)¶

  • Standardized Book Title Page, top-level header navigation (Book, People, Bibliography, Usage), Reader’s Guide (docsrc/usage/), featured Author and Translator profiles in People, and English structural terminology.

0.4.1 (2026-11-13)¶

  • Added track-aware primary-source folio map (resources/summa_1494/folio_map.json) resolving 517/517 folio directives and 400/400 header badges across De Divina Proportione (1509) and Summa de Arithmetica (1494).

0.4.0 (2026-11-12)¶

  • Added the Summa de Arithmetica, Geometria, Proportioni et Proportionalita (Venice, 1494) Book Codex (docsrc/summa_1494/) alongside De Divina Proportione (1509):

    • Visually verified 32 folios of the November 10–20, 1494 Paganino de’ Paganini editio princeps across 5 canonical units with 4 synchronized companion tracks (English, Italiano 1494, Summary, and Notes) and dedicated apparatus registries (resources/summa_1494/{sources_manifest,concordance,persons,citations,glossary}.json).

    • Generated 9 interactive .euclid-graphic vector SVGs (svg_woodcut.py) wired to :g:, :eq:, and :just: roles across polygonal/perfect numbers, Cossic quadratic gnomons, Euclid Book X binomials/apotomes, semicircle three means, golden division of 10, Venetian Quaderno conservation, Heron’s (13, 14, 15) triangle, De divisione superficierum, and inscribed polyhedra in the sphere of diameter 6.

    • Added exact SymPy and geometor.model symbolic modules (proportions.py, tractatus_geometriae.py, ledger_conservation.py, svg_woodcut.py), comprehensive pytest unit tests (tests/), and runnable CLI demonstrations (demos/summa_*.py).

0.3.2 (2026-10-29)¶

  • Enabled intersphinx cross-referencing to geometor/euclid across all Euclid proposition, definition, postulate, and common-notion citations (:just: and :ref:): * Added resources/de_divina_proportione/enunciations.json combining all 642 Euclid Books I–XIII enunciations with Campanus/Hypsicles Books XIV and XV propositions (XIV.1–XIV.10, XV.1–XV.12) for rich hover tooltips. * Expanded rubric-style .. assertion:: step headings in scripts/build_docsrc.py so inline :just: roles render as individual interactive intersphinx links to https://geometor.github.io/euclid/.

0.3.1 (2026-10-29)¶

  • Integrated Constantin Winterberg’s 1889 critical commentary (Quellenschriften für Kunstgeschichte, Vienna, 1889) across the Book Codex: * Added Winterberg’s Vorwort & Einleitung (Preface and Historical-Mathematical Introduction, pp. III–V, 1–15) in synchronized English and Deutsch (1889) companion tracks (docsrc/de_divina_proportione/intro/intro_en.rst, intro_de.rst). * Added Appendix IV: Winterberg’s Substantive Mathematical Notes (*Sachliche Anmerkungen* 1–17, pp. 338–352) (docsrc/de_divina_proportione/plates/anmerkungen_en.rst, anmerkungen_de.rst), including restored algebraic proofs of the Thirteen Effects, the refutation of Pacioli’s Chapter XLVI claim on inscribing the octahedron in the icosahedron, and the polar-reciprocity treatise classifying all 21 mutual inscriptions of the five Platonic solids. * Injected Winterberg’s 17 Sachliche Anmerkungen and philological chapter footnotes directly into the corresponding _notes.rst tracks across Part I (Compendio) and Part II (Architectura), plus Alphabet plate footnotes on Letters H, N, and S.

0.3.0 (2026-10-29)¶

  • Restructured docsrc/de_divina_proportione/ as a photon_platform.publish Book Codex (:layout: book) with synchronized companion language tracks (English, Italiano 1509, Deutsch 1889, Latin / Greek 1509, and Critical Notes) and track-preserving Prev / Next navigation.

  • Built scholarly apparatus registries and RST index pages (bibliography.rst with 113 cited loci, persons.rst with 98 historical persons, and glossary.rst with 160 technical terms) with interactive :cite:, :person:, :term:, :just:, and :g: cross-references across all 318 folios and chapters.

  • Upgraded clean_image() in geometor.pacioli.graphics to normalize aged paper and gutter shadows into pure neutral white while preserving woodcut ink and red rubrication on Folios 0005 and 0311.

  • Recalibrated all 482 woodcut graphics across the 1509 Paganini edition (214 illuminated initial caps, 180 marginal geometric diagrams, and 88 full-page alphabet, architectural, and Leonardo polyhedra plates), removing 28 false-positive detections and mapping initials and diagrams to their exact chapters.

0.2.0 (2026-10-16)¶

  • Added high-resolution folio extraction for the 1509 Paganini edition of De Divina Proportione (pacioli-extract).

  • Implemented automated woodcut diagram and polyhedron detection, bounding-box calibration, and image cropping via OpenCV (pacioli-graphics).

  • Extracted all 202 folios and 374 graphics with structured JSON manifests (<folio>.json and centralized graphics_index.json).

  • Removed legacy inverted graphics in favor of standardized light-mode digital assets.

  • Added CLI commands pacioli-extract and pacioli-graphics.

0.1.0 (2025-12-10)¶

  • Synthesized English translations of De Divina Proportione (Book I) Chapters I-LXXI.

  • Implemented winterberg-parser to process the 1896 Winterberg bilingual edition.

  • Established file structure for parsed chapters (Italian, German, English).

  • Added winterberg_en/divina_en_2 directory for synthesized “Euclid-like” translations.

  • Added PacioliParser class for text extraction and cleaning.
