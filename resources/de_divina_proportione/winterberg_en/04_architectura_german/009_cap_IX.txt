Cap. IX.

Of the angular Columns.

After I with short Discussion to your Sufficiency of
the round Columns spoken, seemed it to me appropriate, also
of the angular Something to say, so that their Manufacture beside
the others not useless to be appear, so far they besides the
Support of the Weight at the Buildings very great Beauty
for the Sight grant, regarding whose I in Truth
nothing Else say will, than that, what we of the round
until now said have, whilst I me on your versed
Genie rely; and at the same time on that especially for each
Worker especially necessary Part, which you at me with
Attention heard have, namely of the Numbers and
Measures with the Application of their Proportions, regarding
whose I me sure rely, that you them always with your thereto
her-directed Instruments Compass and Level, i.e. by means of
the straight and curved Line will proportion can;
by means of whose, as above said become, every Operation to the
worthy Conclusion brought becomes, as man at the antique
Letters which in this Our Book before-placed are, open-
ly sees, which always with Circles and Quadrangles made are,
even if neither Pen nor Brush present. And although
man says, it be heavy, the Circle the Four-side to propor-
tion, with the Knowledge of the Quadrature of the Circle after
all Philosophers may she learnable and representable be, although
she not yet known and represented become is. Perhaps
is in these Days the-one born, who her give will, as
I me also my-sides offer, it Each, whi it deny
should, hand-grasp-ly to-prove. Thus point I on nothing
Else hence, than on that, what I over them before in this
Tractate at the regular Bodies and thereof dependent
said have. Therefore refer I you to that Place and you
become it open find.
