Cap. X.

Of the round and angular Pyramids.

The Pyramids further become to you through their Columns,
as well round as angular easy to learn be, so far each always
a Third of her Column is, as our Euclid proves; and there-
fore become I likewise regarding her their Arrangements
overgo, which not possible is to miss, since they, as
said, as well according to Weight as Dimension under all Cir-
cumstances always a Third of their Cylinder are. And their Arrange-
ment and Figures become you above in this Book at the same time
with all other Bodies have, also from the Hand of our
aforementioned Countryman Leonardo da Vinci from Florence,
regarding whose Drawings and Figures in Truth never
a Man been, who to him oppose could. Therefore etc.
