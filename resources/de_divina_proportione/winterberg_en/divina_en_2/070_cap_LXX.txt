LXX. Guide to the Illustrations

**Instruction**
To find the visual representation of the bodies described:
1.  Note the Roman numeral of the Chapter (e.g., Cap. IV).
2.  Refer to the Perspective Table using the same number (e.g., Plate IV).
3.  Refer to the Material Form Table using the same number.

**Note**
The illustrations in perspective are designed by Leonardo da Vinci.
