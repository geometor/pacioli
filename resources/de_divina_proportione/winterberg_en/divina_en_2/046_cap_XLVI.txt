XLVI. Summary of Inscriptions

**Proposition**
There are exactly 12 possible inscriptions of one regular body within another, not 20.
Not every body can contain every other body.

**List of Inscriptions**
1.  **In Tetrahedron**: Octahedron.
2.  **In Cube**: Tetrahedron, Octahedron.
3.  **In Octahedron**: Cube, Tetrahedron.
4.  **In Dodecahedron**: Icosahedron, Cube, Octahedron, Tetrahedron.
5.  **In Icosahedron**: Dodecahedron, Cube, Tetrahedron.

**Exclusions**
*   Tetrahedron cannot contain Cube, Icosahedron, or Dodecahedron.
*   Cube and Octahedron cannot contain Icosahedron or Dodecahedron.
*   Icosahedron cannot contain Octahedron (due to structural conflict involving perpendicular diameters).
