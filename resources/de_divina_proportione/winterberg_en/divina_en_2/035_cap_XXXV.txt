XXXV. Inclusion of the Tetrahedron in the Cube

**Proposition**
The Tetrahedron can be inscribed in the Cube.

**Construction**
1.  Construct the Cube.
2.  Draw the diagonal on each of its 6 square faces.
3.  These 6 diagonals form the 6 edges of the inscribed Tetrahedron.
4.  The 4 vertices of the Tetrahedron coincide with 4 of the 8 vertices of the Cube.
    (Euclid XV.1).
