XXXI. Determining the Sides of the Five Bodies from the Sphere Diameter

**Proposition**
Given the diameter of a sphere, one can determine the sides of all five inscribed regular bodies.

**Construction**
Let $AB$ be the diameter of the sphere.
1.  Divide $AB$ at $C$ such that $AC = 2 \cdot CB$.
2.  Desribe a semicircle on $AB$. Erect perpendiculars $CE$ and $DF$ (where $D$ is the midpoint of $AB$).
3.  $AE$ is the side of the **Tetrahedron/Pyramid** ($S_4$).
4.  $EB$ is the side of the **Cube** ($S_6$).
5.  $FB$ is the side of the **Octahedron** ($S_8$).
6.  $NB$ (derived from hex/decagon construction on radius) is the side of the **Icosahedron** ($S_{20}$).
7.  $PB$ (greater segment of $EB$ divided in Divine Proportion) is the side of the **Dodecahedron** ($S_{12}$).

**Ordering of Magnitude**
$S_4 > S_8 > S_6 > S_{20} > S_{12}$
(Tetrahedron > Octahedron > Cube > Icosahedron > Dodecahedron).

**Note**
The Cube precedes the Octahedron in construction, but the Octahedron exceeds the Cube in side length.
