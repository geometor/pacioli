IX. Definition of Root

**Definition**
The Root of a quantity is that quantity which, multiplied by itself, produces the given quantity.
The product is called the Square.

**Types of Roots**
1.  **Discrete (Rational)**: Can be expressed by a number. (e.g., $\sqrt{9} = 3$).
2.  **Surd (Irrational)**: Cannot be expressed precisely by a number. (e.g., $\sqrt{10}$).
