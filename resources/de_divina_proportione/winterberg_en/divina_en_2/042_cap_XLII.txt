XLII. Inclusion of the Octahedron in the Dodecahedron

**Proposition**
The Octahedron can be inscribed in the Dodecahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Inscribe a Cube in the Dodecahedron (as in XLI).
2.  Inscribe an Octahedron in that Cube (as in XXXVI) by connecting the centers of the Cube's faces.
3.  Therefore, the Octahedron is inscribed in the Dodecahedron.
    The 6 vertices of the Octahedron correspond to the centers of the 6 faces of the inscribed Cube, which align with the orientation of the Dodecahedron's structure.
    (Euclid XV.9).
