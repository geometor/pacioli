LIII. The Body of 26 Bases (Small Rhombicuboctahedron)

**I. Plane/Solid 26-Base Body**
Derived from the Cube by cutting/expansion (Cantellation).
*   Faces: 26 (18 Squares, 8 Triangles).
    *   18 Squares = 6 from original cube faces + 12 from edges.
    *   8 Triangles = from the 8 corners.
*   Vertices: 24.
*   Edges: 48.
*   Solid Angles: 24 (formed by 1 Triangle and 3 Squares).

**II. Elevated 26-Base Body**
Created by constructing pyramids on all 26 faces.
*   Pyramids: 26 (18 Quadrangular, 8 Triangular).
*   Edges: 144.
*   Faces: 96? (Dimensions become complex).
