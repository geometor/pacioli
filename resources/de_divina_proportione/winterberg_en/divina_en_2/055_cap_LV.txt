LV. Infinite Production of Forms and Platonic Correspondences

**Infinite Progression**
From the regular bodies, an infinite number of derived forms can be created by successive abscission (cutting) and elevation (adding pyramids).

**Platonic Correspondences (Timaeus)**
Plato assignment of the 5 Regular Bodies to the 5 Elemental Forms:
1.  **Earth = Cube**: Stable, hard to move.
2.  **Fire = Tetrahedron**: Mobile, sharp, ascending flame points.
3.  **Air = Octahedron**: Mobile, akin to fire.
4.  **Water = Icosahedron**: Mobile, rolling (many faces/spherical nature).
5.  **Heaven/Universe = Dodecahedron**: The receptacle of all, encompassing the others.
    *   Symbolism: 12 Faces correspond to 12 Zodiac signs.
    *   360 degrees: 12 Faces x 5 Triangles x 6 Scalenes = 360 sub-triangles (referencing Alcinous/Calcidius).

**Uniqueness of the Five**
There can be no more than 5 regular bodies. To posit a sixth would imply a lack of prudence in the Creator/Nature.
The Sphere involves all.
