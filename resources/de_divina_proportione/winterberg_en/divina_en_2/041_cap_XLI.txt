XLI. Inclusion of the Cube in the Dodecahedron

**Proposition**
The Cube can be inscribed in the Dodecahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Construct the Dodecahedron.
2.  Select 8 of the 20 vertices of the Dodecahedron appropriately.
3.  Connect these vertices to form a Cube.
    The 12 Pentagonal faces of the Dodecahedron are built upon the 12 edges of the inscribed Cube (Euclid XIII.17).
    Each face of the Cube corresponds to a "roof" formed by the Pentagons.
