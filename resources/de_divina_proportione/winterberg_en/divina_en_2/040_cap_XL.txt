XL. Inclusion of the Icosahedron in the Dodecahedron

**Proposition**
The Icosahedron can be inscribed in the Dodecahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Construct the Dodecahedron.
2.  Find the center of each of its 12 pentagonal faces (Euclid IV.14).
3.  Connect these centers with 30 straight lines.
4.  These lines cross the sides of the Dodecahedron perpendicularly.
5.  The result is an Icosahedron, with 20 triangular faces and 12 vertices resting on the centers of the Dodecahedron's faces.
    (Euclid XV.7).
