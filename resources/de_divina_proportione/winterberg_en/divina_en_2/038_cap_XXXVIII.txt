XXXVIII. Inclusion of the Tetrahedron in the Octahedron

**Proposition**
The Tetrahedron can be inscribed in the Octahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Inscribe a Cube in the Octahedron (as in XXXVII).
2.  Inscribe a Tetrahedron in that Cube (as in XXXV).
3.  Therefore, the Tetrahedron is inscribed in the Octahedron.
