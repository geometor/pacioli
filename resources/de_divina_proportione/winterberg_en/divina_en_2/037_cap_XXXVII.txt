XXXVII. Inclusion of the Cube in the Octahedron

**Proposition**
The Cube can be inscribed in the Octahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Construct the Octahedron.
2.  Find the center of each of its 8 triangular faces (Euclid IV.5).
3.  Connect these 8 centers with 12 straight lines.
4.  The result is a Cube, with its vertices resting on the centers of the Octahedron's faces.
    (Euclid XV.4).
