XXXIV. Inclusion of the Octahedron in the Tetrahedron

**Proposition**
The Tetrahedron cannot receive (contain) the Cube, Icosahedron, or Dodecahedron in a regular inscription.
It can only receive the Octahedron.

**Construction**
To inscribe the Octahedron in the Tetrahedron:
1.  Construct the Tetrahedron.
2.  Find the midpoint of each of its 6 edges.
3.  Connect these midpoints with straight lines.
4.  The resulting figure is an Octahedron, with its 6 vertices resting on the 6 edges of the Tetrahedron.
    (Euclid XV.1, XV.2).
