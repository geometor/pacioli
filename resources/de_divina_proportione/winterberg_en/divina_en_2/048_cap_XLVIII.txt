XLVIII. Variations of the Tetrahedron

**I. Plane/Solid Tetrahedron**
The standard body. 4 faces, 4 vertices, 6 edges.

**II. Abscinded (Truncated) Tetrahedron**
Created by cutting the vertices of the Tetrahedron at 1/3 of the edge length.
*   Faces: 8 (4 Hexagons, 4 Triangles).
*   Vertices: 12.
*   Edges: 18.
*   Solid Angles: 12.

**III. Elevated (Cumulated) Tetrahedron**
Created by constructing pyramids on the faces of the Tetrahedron.
*   Faces: 12 (Triangular).
*   Vertices: 8 (4 original, 4 new apices).
*   Edges: 18.
