LXXI. Mathematical Definitions

**Hypothesis**: A presupposition admitted by both author and adversary as a basis for argument.

**Hypothumissa (Hypotenuse)**: The side opposite the greatest angle in a rectilinear figure. Specifically, the side opposite the right angle in a right-angled triangle (Euclid I.47).

**Corausto**: A straight line joining two elevated points (Edges of elevation?).

**Cone**: The apex or supreme point of a pyramid where the lateral lines meet.

**Pentagonal Chord**: The straight line connecting two non-adjacent vertices of a pentagon (Diagonal).

**Perpendicular (Cathetus)**: A straight line standing at right angles to another line or plane. In a triangle, the altitude (often called "Arrow" by vulgars).

**Diameter**:
1.  **Circle**: A straight line through the center, terminating at the circumference.
2.  **Square**: The diagonal.

**Parallelogram**: A four-sided figure with opposite sides parallel (Square, Rectangle, Rhombus, Rhomboid).

**Diagonal**: A straight line joining opposite angles in a quadrilateral.

**Center**:
1.  **Circle**: The point from which all lines to the circumference are equal.
2.  **Polygon**: The point from which lines to all vertices (or sides) are equal.

**Arrow (Sagitta)**: The line segment from the midpoint of a chord to the midpoint of the arc subtended by the chord (Versine).
