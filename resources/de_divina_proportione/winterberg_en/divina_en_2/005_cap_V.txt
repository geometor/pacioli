V. The Title "Divine Proportion"

The title "Divine Proportion" is chosen for five properties corresponding to divine attributes:

1.  **Uniqueness**: It is one and only one, allowing no other species. (Corresponds to the Unity of God).
2.  **Trinity**: It always involves three terms. (Corresponds to the Holy Trinity).
3.  **Irrationality**: It cannot be expressed by a rational number or understandable quantity. (Corresponds to the Incomprehensibility of God).
4.  **Invariance**: It is the same in every continuous and discrete quantity, large or small. (Corresponds to the Immutability of God).
5.  **Quintessence**: Just as God confers being to the four elements (Earth, Water, Air, Fire) via the Quintessence (Heaven/Dodecahedron), the Divine Proportion is necessary to form the Dodecahedron (Heaven) and relates the other regular bodies (Tetrahedron/Fire, Hexahedron/Earth, Octahedron/Air, Icosahedron/Water). The regular bodies cannot be circumscribed by a sphere without this proportion.
