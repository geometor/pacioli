IV. Citations and Notation

Citations of Euclid:
- "The 5th of the 1st" refers to the 5th Proposition of Euclid's Book I.
- "The 4th of the 2nd" refers to the 4th Proposition of Euclid's Book II.
This convention applies generally to Euclid's Elements. Citations of other works will be explicit.

Algebraic Terms (Modernized):
- "Cosa" (Thing) = $x$ (unknown linear quantity)
- "Censo" (Square) = $x^2$ (square of the unknown)
- "Cubo" (Cube) = $x^3$ (cube of the unknown)
- "Multiplication", "Product", "Rectangle" are equivalent terms.
- "Square of a quantity" and "Power of a quantity" are equivalent terms.
- The "Power of a line" refers to its square (Euclid X).
