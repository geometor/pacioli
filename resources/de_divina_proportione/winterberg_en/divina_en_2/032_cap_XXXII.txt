XXXII. Proportions of the Bodies (Volume)

**Statement**
The proportions of the volumes of the regular bodies to each other are generally irrational, due to the intervention of the Divine Proportion (especially in the Dodecahedron and Icosahedron).
Exceptions: Ratios between Tetrahedron, Cube, and Octahedron may be rational depending on the sphere diameter.

**Key Relationships**
1.  **Dodecahedron : Icosahedron** (in same sphere).
    The ratio of their volumes is equal to the ratio of their total surface areas (Euclid XIV.10).
2.  **Octahedron Volume**.
    The Octahedron is divisible into two square-based pyramids with height equal to the sphere radius (Euclid XIV.16).
