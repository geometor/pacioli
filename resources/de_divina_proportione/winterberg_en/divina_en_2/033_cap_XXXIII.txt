XXXIII. Proportions of the Surfaces

**Statement**
Proportions of surfaces are generally irrational, except for those composed of squares and triangles (Tetrahedron, Cube, Octahedron), which are commensurable with the square of the sphere diameter.

**Key Relationships**
1.  **Dodecahedron : Icosahedron** (Surfaces).
    Ratio equals Side of Cube : Side of Icosahedron Triangle (Euclid XIV.8).
    Both bodies can have their faces circumscribed by the same circle (Euclid XIV.5).
2.  **Tetrahedron : Octahedron** (Surfaces).
    Single Face Ratio: $Face_4 : Face_8 = 4 : 3$ (Sesquitertia).
    Total Surface Ratio: $Surface_8 : Surface_4 = 6 : 4 = 3 : 2$ (Sesquialtera).
3.  **Cube Surface**.
    Total Surface Area = $2 \cdot D^2$ (Double the square of the sphere diameter).
    Distance from Center to Face = $1/2 \cdot S_6$.
