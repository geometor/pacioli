II. Preface

Knowledge begins with sensation. Sight is the noblest sense and the primary door to the intellect.
Mathematical sciences are of the first degree of certainty, followed by natural sciences.
Wisdom states that everything consists of Number, Weight, and Measure.
Mathematical disciplines are the foundation for all other sciences, including:
- Art of War (ballistics, fortifications, machines)
- Architecture
- Theology
- Astronomy
- Law (e.g., measuring land/floods)

This compendium presents the material bodies in their proper forms, derived from these mathematical principles.
