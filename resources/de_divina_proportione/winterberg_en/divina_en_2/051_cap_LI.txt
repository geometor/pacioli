LI. Variations of the Icosahedron

**I. Plane/Solid Icosahedron**
The standard body. 20 faces, 12 vertices, 30 edges.

**II. Abscinded (Truncated) Icosahedron**
Created by cutting the vertices of the Icosahedron at 1/3 of the edge length.
*   Faces: 32 (20 Hexagons, 12 Pentagons).
*   Vertices: 60.
*   Edges: 90.
*   Solid Angles: 60.
*   Note: The Hexagons are derived from the original triangular faces, the Pentagons from the cut vertices.

**III. Elevated Icosahedron**
Created by constructing pyramids on the 20 faces of the Icosahedron.
*   Faces: 60 (Triangular).
*   Vertices: 32 (12 original, 20 new apices).
*   Edges: 90.
*   Structure: Composed of 20 triangular pyramids (tetrahedra) on the faces.
