III. Definition of Mathematics

The term "Mathematics" is derived from Greek, meaning "disciplinable" (teachable/learnable).
Mathematical disciplines commonly include:
1. Arithmetic
2. Geometry
3. Astronomy
4. Music
(Subalternate: Perspective, Architecture, Cosmography, etc.)

Our classification prioritizes three or five:
1. Arithmetic
2. Geometry
3. Astronomy
(Optional: Music, Perspective)

Perspective is emphasized as equal to Music in worth, relating to the sense of sight (natural number and visual line) as Music relates to hearing (sonorous number and time).
Painting, derived from perspective, imitates nature.
