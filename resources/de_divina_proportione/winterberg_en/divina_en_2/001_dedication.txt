Dedication

To Ludovico Maria Sforza, Duke of Milan.
From Luca Pacioli, Professor of Sacred Theology.
Milan, February 9, 1498.

This work was presented in the presence of the Duke and numerous scholars and officials, including:
- Gometio (Theologian)
- Domenico Ponzone (Theologian)
- Francesco Busti (Order of Minors)
- Galeazzo Sforza San Severino (Captain General)
- Ambrogio Rosa (Medicine/Astronomy)
- Aluisi Marliano (Medicine)
- Gabriel Pirovano (Medicine)
- Nicolo Cusano (Doctor/Councilor)
- Andrea Novarese (Doctor/Councilor)
- Leonardo da Vinci (Architect/Painter/Sculptor)
- Giacomo Andrea (Architect)

Special mention is made of Leonardo da Vinci, citing his equestrian statue (12 braccia high, mass approx 200,000 pounds) and his painting of the Last Supper in the Temple of Graces.

The work is titled "Divine Proportion" and treats the mathematical disciplines necessary for architecture, perspective, and other sciences. It is dedicated to the Duke and his library.
