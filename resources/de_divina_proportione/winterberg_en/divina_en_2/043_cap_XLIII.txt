XLIII. Inclusion of the Tetrahedron in the Dodecahedron

**Proposition**
The Tetrahedron can be inscribed in the Dodecahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Inscribe a Cube in the Dodecahedron (as in XLI).
2.  Inscribe a Tetrahedron in that Cube (as in XXXV).
3.  Therefore, the Tetrahedron is inscribed in the Dodecahedron.
    The vertices of the Tetrahedron are a subset of the vertices of the Dodecahedron (via the Cube).
    (Euclid XV.10).
