X. Verification of the First Effect

**Demonstration**
Recall the proposition from Chapter VII: $(AC + AD)^2 = 5 \cdot AD^2$.
Let the line be 10. Half of the line ($AD$) is 5.
The greater segment ($AC$) is $\sqrt{125} - 5$.
Sum of greater segment and half line: $(\sqrt{125} - 5) + 5 = \sqrt{125}$.
Square of the sum: $(\sqrt{125})^2 = 125$.
Square of the half line: $5^2 = 25$.
$125 = 5 \times 25$.

**Conclusion**
The square of the sum of the greater segment and the half line is exactly five times the square of the half line.
(Euclid XIII.1)
