LXVIII. Measurement of the Truncated Pyramid

**Method (Restoration)**
To measure a truncated (cut/short) pyramid:
1.  **Extend**: Imagine the pyramid is complete, extending the lateral edges until they meet at the apex.
2.  **Measure Total**: Calculate the volume of the entire integral pyramid.
3.  **Measure Tip**: Calculate the volume of the small pyramid that was removed (the defining "defect").
4.  **Subtract**: Subtract the volume of the small pyramid from the volume of the total pyramid.
    $$V_{truncated} = V_{total} - V_{tip}$$
