LXIII. Measurement of the Laterated Column (Prism)

**Rule**
Volume = Area of Base × Height

**Example (Square Prism)**
*   Height ($h$) = 10.
*   Base Side ($s$) = 6.
*   Area of Base ($s^2$) = 36.
*   Volume = $36 \times 10 = 360$.

**General Application**
This rule applies to all laterated columns (Triangular, Pentagonal, Hexagonal, etc.), provided the area of the base is calculated according to the rules for that specific polygon.
