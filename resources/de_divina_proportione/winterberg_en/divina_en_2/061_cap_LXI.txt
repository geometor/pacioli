LXI. The Pentagonal and Multilateral Column

**Definition**
A pentagonal column is a laterated body contained by two parallel pentagonal bases and five lateral parallelogram faces.

**Generalization**
Columns can be formed with any rectilinear polygonal base (Hexagonal, Heptagonal, etc.).
*   **Structure**: 2 parallel Bases (n-sided polygons) + n Lateral Faces (Rectangles).
*   **Naming**: Derived from the shape of the base.
*   **Condition**: The lateral faces are always rectangular parallelograms (in a right prism).
