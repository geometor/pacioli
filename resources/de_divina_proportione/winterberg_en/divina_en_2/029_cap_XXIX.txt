XXIX. Construction of the Icosahedron

**Construction**
From a given sphere with diameter $AB$:
1.  Divide $AB$ at $C$ such that $AC = 4 \cdot CB$.
2.  Construct perpendicular $CD$. Draw $DB$.
3.  Construct a circle with radius $DB$.
4.  Inscribe an equilateral Pentagon and Decagon in this circle.
5.  Construct the icosahedron using vertices derived from these polygons and perpendiculars.
    (Detailed geometric construction omitted for brevity, involves pentagonal tiers).

**Properties**
The side of the Icosahedron is the "Minor Line" (irrational) if the sphere diameter is rational.
The side of the Icosahedron equals the side of the Pentagon inscribed in the circle with radius $DB$.

**Ratio**
The ratio involves the Divine Proportion and is derived from Euclid XIII.16.
$D^2 = 5 \cdot (Radius \ of \ Construction \ Circle)^2$.

**Reference**
(Euclid XIII.16)
