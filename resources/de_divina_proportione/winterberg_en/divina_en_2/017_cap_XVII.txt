XVII. Eighth Effect

**Proposition (Converse of Effect VII)**
If a line is divided according to the Divine Proportion:
The greater segment is the side of a Hexagon inscribed in a circle.
The lesser segment is the side of a Decagon inscribed in the *same* circle.

**Example**
Whole Line = 10.
Greater Segment = $\sqrt{125} - 5$ = Hexagon Side (Radius).
Circle Diameter = $2 \times (\sqrt{125} - 5) = \sqrt{500} - 10$.
Lesser Segment = $15 - \sqrt{125}$ = Decagon Side.

**Reference**
(Euclid XIII.9; Ptolemy Almagest I.9)
