LXIX. Measurement of Abscinded and Elevated Bodies

**Method regarding Abscinded (Truncated) Bodies**
Treat the abscinded body as a regular body with "defects" (missing parts).
1.  Calculate the volume of the full regular body.
2.  Calculate the volume of the small pyramids removed from the corners (or edges).
3.  Subtract the removed volumes from the total.

**Method regarding Elevated (Stellated) Bodies**
Treat the elevated body as a regular body with "additions".
1.  Calculate the volume of the core regular body (the base of the elevations).
2.  Calculate the volume of the pyramids added to the faces.
3.  Add the pyramid volumes to the core volume.
    $$V_{elevated} = V_{core} + \sum V_{pyramids}$$

**Note**
This method relies on the known geometry of the 5 regular bodies.
