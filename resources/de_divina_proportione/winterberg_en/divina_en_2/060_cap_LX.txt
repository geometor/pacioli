LX. The Quadrangular Column (Prism)

**Definition**
A quadrangular column is a laterated body contained by two parallel quadrangular bases and four lateral parallelogram faces.

**Classification by Base**
The form of the column follows the form of its base:
1.  **Square**: Base is a square (equal sides, right angles).
2.  **Oblong Tetragon (Rectangle)**: Base is a rectangle (opposite sides equal, right angles).
3.  **Elmuaym (Rhombus)**: Base is a rhombus (equal sides, oblique angles).
4.  **Rhomboid**: Base is a parallelogram (opposite sides equal, oblique angles).
5.  **Elmuariffe (Trapezium/Irregular)**: Base is irregular.

**Structure**
*   **Bases**: 2 Quadrilaterals.
*   **Lateral Faces**: 4 Parallelograms.
*   **Total Surfaces**: 6.
If the bases are squares and the height equals the side, it becomes a Cube.
