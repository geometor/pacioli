XLV. Inclusion of the Tetrahedron in the Icosahedron

**Proposition**
The Tetrahedron can be inscribed in the Icosahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Inscribe a Cube in the Icosahedron (as in XLIV).
2.  Inscribe a Tetrahedron in that Cube (as in XXXV).
3.  Therefore, the Tetrahedron is inscribed in the Icosahedron.
    (Euclid XV.12).
