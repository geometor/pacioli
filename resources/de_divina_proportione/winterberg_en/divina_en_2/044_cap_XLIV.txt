XLIV. Inclusion of the Cube in the Icosahedron

**Proposition**
The Cube can be inscribed in the Icosahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Inscribe a Dodecahedron in the Icosahedron (as in XXXIX).
2.  Inscribe a Cube in that Dodecahedron (as in XLI).
3.  Therefore, the Cube is inscribed in the Icosahedron.
    The 8 vertices of the Cube rest on the centers of 8 of the 12 Pentagonal faces of the inscribed Dodecahedron, which in turn are derived from the Icosahedron.
    (Euclid XV.11).
