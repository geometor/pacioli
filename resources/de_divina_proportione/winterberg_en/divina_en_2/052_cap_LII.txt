LII. Variations of the Dodecahedron

**I. Plane/Solid Dodecahedron**
The standard body. 12 faces, 20 vertices, 30 edges.

**II. Abscinded (Truncated) Dodecahedron**
Created by cutting the vertices of the Dodecahedron at the midpoints of the edges (Icosidodecahedron).
*   Faces: 32 (12 Pentagons, 20 Triangles).
*   Vertices: 30.
*   Edges: 60.
*   Solid Angles: 30.
*   Note: The term "Abscinded" here describes the Rectified Dodecahedron (Icosidodecahedron).

**III. Elevated Dodecahedron**
Created by constructing pyramids on the 12 faces of the Dodecahedron.
*   Faces: 60 (Triangular).
*   Vertices: 32 (20 original, 12 new apices).
*   Edges: 90.
*   Structure: Composed of 12 pentagonal pyramids.

**IV. Abscinded Elevated Dodecahedron**
A complex form composed of the "Abscinded Dodecahedron" (Icosidodecahedron) with pyramids erected on its faces (both pentagonal and triangular).
*   Edges: 180.
*   Pyramids: 32 (12 Pentagonal, 20 Triangular).
