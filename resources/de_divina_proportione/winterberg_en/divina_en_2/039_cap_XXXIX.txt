XXXIX. Inclusion of the Dodecahedron in the Icosahedron

**Proposition**
The Dodecahedron can be inscribed in the Icosahedron.

**Construction**
1.  Construct the Icosahedron.
2.  Find the center of each of its 20 triangular faces (Euclid IV.5).
3.  Connect these centers with 30 straight lines.
4.  These lines form 12 pentagons, each opposite a vertex of the Icosahedron.
5.  The result is a Dodecahedron inscribed in the Icosahedron.
    The sides of the pentagons cross the sides of the Icosahedron perpendicularly.
    (Euclid XV.6).
