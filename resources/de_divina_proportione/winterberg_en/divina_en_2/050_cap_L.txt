L. Variations of the Octahedron

**I. Plane/Solid Octahedron**
The standard body. 8 faces, 6 vertices, 12 edges.

**II. Abscinded (Truncated) Octahedron**
Created by cutting the vertices of the Octahedron at 1/3 of the edge length.
*   Faces: 14 (8 Hexagons, 6 Squares).
*   Vertices: 24.
*   Edges: 36.

**III. Elevated Octahedron (Triakis Octahedron)**
Created by constructing pyramids on the faces of the Octahedron.
*   Faces: 24 (Triangular).
*   Vertices: 14 (6 original, 8 new apices).
*   Edges: 36.
