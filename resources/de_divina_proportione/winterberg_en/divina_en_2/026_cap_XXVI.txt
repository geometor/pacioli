XXVI. Construction of the Tetrahedron

**Construction**
From a given sphere with diameter $AB$:
1.  Divide $AB$ at $C$ such that $AC = 2 \cdot CB$.
2.  Desribe a semicircle on $AB$. Erect perpendicular $CD$.
3.  $CD$ is the radius of the circle circumscribing the face of the Tetrahedron.
4.  Construct an equilateral triangle in this circle (Euclid IV.2).
5.  Erect a perpendicular from the center of the circle equal to $AC$.
6.  Connect the vertex to the triangle vertices.

**Ratio**
The Square of the Sphere Diameter is to the Square of the Tetrahedron Side as 3 is to 2 (Sesquialter ratio).
$D^2 : S^2 = 3 : 2$.
If $D^2 = 6$, then $S^2 = 4$.

**Reference**
(Euclid XIII.13)
