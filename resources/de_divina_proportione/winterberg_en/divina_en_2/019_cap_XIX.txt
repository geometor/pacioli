XIX. Tenth Effect

**Proposition**
All quantities divided according to the Divine Proportion share the same properties and ratios, regardless of their size.
The ratio of the Whole to the Greater Segment is constant.
The ratio of the Greater Segment to the Lesser Segment is constant.
The ratio of the Whole to the Lesser Segment is constant.

**Example**
Line 1: Whole = 10. Greater = $\sqrt{125} - 5$.
Line 2: Whole = 12. Greater = $\sqrt{180} - 6$.
Ratio $10 : (\sqrt{125} - 5)$ is equal to $12 : (\sqrt{180} - 6)$.

**Conclusion**
All such quantities exhibit an "Irrational Symphony" and harmony.
(Euclid XIV.2)
