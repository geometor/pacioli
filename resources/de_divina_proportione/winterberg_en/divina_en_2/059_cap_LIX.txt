LIX. The Triangular Column (Prism)

**Definition**
A triangular column is a laterated (angular) body contained by two parallel triangular bases and three lateral parallelogram faces.

**Structure**
*   **Bases**: Two triangles (Equilateral, Isosceles, or Scalene).
*   **Lateral Faces**: Three parallelograms (Rectangles if the prism is right).
*   **Total Surfaces**: 5.
*   Also known as a "Seratile Body" (wedge-shaped or roof-like).
