LXII. Measurement of the Round Column (Cylinder)

**Rule**
Volume = Area of Base × Height

**Area of Base (Circle)**
Using the Archimedean approximation ($\pi \approx 22/7$):
Area = $D^2 \times \frac{11}{14}$
(Since Area = $\frac{\pi D^2}{4} \approx \frac{22}{7 \times 4} D^2 = \frac{22}{28} D^2 = \frac{11}{14} D^2$).

**Example**
*   Height ($h$) = 10.
*   Diameter ($D$) = 7.
*   Square of Diameter ($D^2$) = 49.
*   Area of Base = $49 \times \frac{11}{14} = 38.5$.
*   Volume = $38.5 \times 10 = 385$.
