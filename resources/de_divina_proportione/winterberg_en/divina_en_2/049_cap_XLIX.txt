XLIX. Variations of the Cube

**I. Plane/Solid Cube**
The standard body. 6 faces, 8 vertices, 12 edges.

**II. Abscinded (Truncated) Cube**
Created by cutting the vertices of the Cube at the midpoints of the edges (Cuboctahedron).
*   Faces: 14 (6 Squares, 8 Triangles).
*   Vertices: 12.
*   Edges: 24.
*   Solid Angles: 12.
*   Note: "Abscinded" here describes the Cuboctahedron (rectified cube) if cut at midpoints.

**III. Elevated Cube (Tetrakis Hexahedron)**
Created by constructing pyramids on the faces of the Cube.
*   Faces: 24 (Triangular).
*   Vertices: 14 (8 original, 6 new apices).
*   Edges: 36.

**IV. Abscinded Elevated Hexahedron**
Use complex construction involving 72 edges and 48 faces.
