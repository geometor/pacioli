XXII. Thirteenth Effect

**Proposition**
The Divine Proportion is essential for the construction of the Pentagon.
Consequently, it is essential for the construction of the Dodecahedron (bounded by 12 pentagons), which Plato attributed to the Heavens (Quintessence).

**Demonstration**
To construct an equilateral and equiangular pentagon (Euclid IV.11), one must first construct an isosceles triangle where each base angle is double the vertex angle (Euclid IV.10).
To construct this triangle, one must divide a line such that the square of one segment equals the product of the other segment and the whole line (Euclid II.11).
This division is exactly the Divine Proportion (Euclid VI, Definition 3).

**Conclusion**
Without the Divine Proportion, neither the Pentagon nor the Dodecahedron can be constructed.
