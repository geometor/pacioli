XXVIII. Construction of the Octahedron

**Construction**
From a given sphere with diameter $AB$:
1.  Divide $AB$ at midpoint $C$.
2.  Construct semicircle and perpendicular $CD$.
3.  Draw $BD$.
4.  Construct a square with side $BD$.
5.  Erect perpendiculars from the center of the square upwards and downwards, equal to half the diagonal.
6.  Connect vertices to the square corners to form 8 equilateral triangles.

**Ratio**
The Square of the Sphere Diameter is double the Square of the Octahedron Side.
$D^2 : S^2 = 2 : 1$.
If $D = 8$ ($D^2=64$), then $S^2 = 32$ ($S=\sqrt{32}$).

**Reference**
(Euclid XIII.14)
