XXIV. The Five Regular Bodies

**Definition**
Regular bodies are solids with equal sides, equal angles, and equal bases.
There are exactly five such bodies in nature, corresponding to the five elements:
1.  **Earth**: Cube (Headrahedron/Hexahedron).
2.  **Water**: Icosahedron.
3.  **Air**: Octahedron.
4.  **Fire**: Tetrahedron (Pyramid).
5.  **Quintessence (Heaven)**: Dodecahedron.

**Proposition**
It is impossible for there to be more than five regular bodies.
(Plato, Timaeus).
