XIII. Fourth Effect

**Proposition**
If a quantity is divided according to the Divine Proportion, and to the whole is added its greater segment:
The sum and the greater segment are parts of a second quantity also divided in Divine Proportion.
The greater segment of this second quantity is the original whole quantity.

**Example**
Original Quantity ($AB$) = 10.
Greater Segment ($AC$) = $\sqrt{125} - 5$.
New Quantity ($L$) = $10 + (\sqrt{125} - 5) = \sqrt{125} + 5$.
Parts of $L$:
1. $10$ (Original Whole)
2. $\sqrt{125} - 5$ (Original Greater Segment)

Check Divine Proportion for $L$:
Whole ($L$) $\times$ Lesser ($\sqrt{125} - 5$) = $(\sqrt{125} + 5)(\sqrt{125} - 5) = 125 - 25 = 100$.
Square of Greater (10) = $10^2 = 100$.

**Conclusion**
The product of the whole new quantity and the lesser part equals the square of the greater part.
Thus, the new quantity is divided in Divine Proportion.
(Euclid XIII.4)
