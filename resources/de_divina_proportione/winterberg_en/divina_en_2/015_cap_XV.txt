XV. Sixth Effect

**Proposition**
No rational quantity can be divided according to the Divine Proportion such that its parts are rational.
If the whole is rational, the parts must be irrational (Residuals/Apotomes).

**Example**
Whole = 10 (Rational).
Parts:
$\sqrt{125} - 5$ (Irrational, Apotome).
$15 - \sqrt{125}$ (Irrational, Apotome).

**Reference**
(Euclid XIII.6; X.79).
