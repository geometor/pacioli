XXVII. Construction of the Cube

**Construction**
From a given sphere with diameter $AB$:
1.  Divide $AB$ at $C$ such that $AC = 2 \cdot CB$.
2.  Construct semicircle and perpendicular $CD$ (as in XXVI).
3.  Draw $BD$.
4.  Construct a square with side equal to $BD$.
5.  Erect perpendiculars from the corners equal to $BD$ to form the Cube.

**Ratio**
The Square of the Sphere Diameter is triple the Square of the Cube Side.
$D^2 : S^2 = 3 : 1$.
If $D^2 = 300$, then $S^2 = 100$ ($S=10$).

**Reference**
(Euclid XIII.15)
