XXXVI. Inclusion of the Octahedron in the Cube

**Proposition**
The Octahedron can be inscribed in the Cube.

**Construction**
1.  Construct the Cube.
2.  Find the center of each of its 6 square faces.
3.  Connect these 6 centers with straight lines.
4.  The result is an Octahedron, with its vertices resting on the centers of the Cube's faces.
    (Euclid XV.3).
