LVIII. The Round Column (Cylinder)

**Definition**
A round column (cylinder) is a solid figure described by the revolution of a rectangular parallelogram about one of its sides, which remains fixed (the axis).
(Euclid XI, Def. 21).

**Properties**
*   **Bases**: Two equal and parallel circles.
*   **Axis**: The fixed side of the generating rectangle.
*   **Circumscription**: A sphere can be circumscribed about a cylinder if the diameter of the sphere equals the diagonal of the generating rectangle. The center of the sphere bisects the axis of the cylinder.
