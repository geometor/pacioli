XLVII. Inscribing the Sphere in the Regular Bodies

**Proposition**
A sphere can be inscribed in each of the five regular bodies.

**Construction**
1.  Find the center of the body.
2.  Draw perpendiculars from the center to each of the faces.
3.  These perpendiculars are all equal in length.
4.  A sphere with radius equal to this perpendicular length will touch all faces at their centers.
    (Euclid XV, Corollary).
