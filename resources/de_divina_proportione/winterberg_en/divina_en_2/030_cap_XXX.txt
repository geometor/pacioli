XXX. Construction of the Dodecahedron

**Construction**
From a given Cube inscribed in the sphere:
1.  Divide the sides of the Cube faces.
2.  Divide specific segments according to the Divine Proportion.
3.  Construct Pentagons on the faces of the Cube using these divisions ("roofs" upon the cube faces).
4.  The 12 Pentagons form the Dodecahedron.

**Properties**
The side of the Dodecahedron is the "Apotome" (Residuum) if the sphere diameter is rational.
The vertices of the Dodecahedron touch the encompassing sphere.

**Ratio**
The Square of the Sphere Diameter is triple the Square of the Cube Side (from which the Dodecahedron is derived).
Relationships involving the Divine Proportion are central to this construction.

**Reference**
(Euclid XIII.17)
