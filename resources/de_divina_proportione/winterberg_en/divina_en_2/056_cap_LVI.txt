LVI. Definition of the Sphere

**Definition**
A sphere is a solid figure described by the revolution of a semicircle about its diameter, which remains fixed.
(Euclid XI, Def. 14).

**Properties**
*   **Center**: The center of the sphere is the same as the center of the generating semicircle.
*   **Surface**: The surface is the locus of the arc of the semicircle as it rotates.
*   **Equality**: All straight lines drawn from the center to the surface are equal.
