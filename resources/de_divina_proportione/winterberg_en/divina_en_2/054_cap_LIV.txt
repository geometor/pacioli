LIV. The Body of 72 Bases and Architectural Reflections

**I. The Body of 72 Bases**
A complex irregular body.
*   Faces: 72.
    *   48 Quadrangular (Inequilateral).
    *   24 Triangular (Inequilateral).
*   Construction linked to the Dodecahedron or Dodecagonal forms.

**II. Architectural Application**
This form and its elevated variants are suitable for vaults, tribunes, and domes ("Heavens").
Examples:
*   Pantheon (Rotunda) in Rome.
*   Sanctuary of San Satiro (San Scetro) in Milan.
*   Church of Santa Maria delle Grazie (Temple of Graces) in Milan (Tribune/Dome by Bramante?).

**Critique**
Comparison of ancient architects (Vitruvius) who followed strict geometric proportions vs. modern "bunglers" who lack knowledge of angles and lines.
Emphasizes the importance of the "Angle of Justice" (Right Angle) and the study of Vitruvius.
