LXV. The Laterated Pyramid

**Definition**
A laterated pyramid is a solid figure contained by planes that originate from a base and converge to a single point (the cone or apex).
(Euclid XI, Def. 12).

**Structure**
*   **Base**: A rectilinear polygon (Triangle, Quadrilateral, Pentagon, etc.).
*   **Lateral Faces**: Triangles meeting at the apex.
*   **Naming**: Derived from the base (Triangular, Quadrangular, Pentagonal, etc.).

**Types**
*   **Right Pyramid**: If the apex lies directly above the center of the base (Perpendicular).
*   **Oblique Pyramid (Declining)**: If the apex is not over the center.
*   **Truncated Pyramid**: If the top part including the apex is cut off.
