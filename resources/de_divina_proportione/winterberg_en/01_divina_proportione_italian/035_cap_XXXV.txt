Cap. XXXV.

(How said tetrahedron is formed and placed in the cube.)

The said tetrahedron in the cube will be placed in this mode that is. First we will make the cube according to the modes above given then in each of its 6 square surfaces we will draw the diagonal or true diameter and will be the purpose concluded as the first of the 15. demonstrates because said tetrahedron as was said has 6. sides corresponding to the number of the 6 surfaces of the cube and those come to be its 6 diagonals in its surfaces protracted. And the 4 angles of the pyramid come to enable themselves in 4 of the 8 of said cube. Which also the mistress of all things holy experience in their materials renders clear.
