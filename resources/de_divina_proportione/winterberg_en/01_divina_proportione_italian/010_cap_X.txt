Cap. X.

(Sequel of the first proposed effect.)

Which things well noted to its first proposed effect let us make regress. And that with evident examples let us render clear and to its elucidation let the same case of 10 be taken back in that place adduced without traveling anymore in other laborious quantities since the same always happens in each that is said in this. And by way of Arithmetic to fuller notice of your Highness the others all we will go knowing presupposing always the scientific proofs of that whole which our process will contain in the places which we will adduce from our philosopher Euclid to be assigned with every geometric diligence according to the opportune exigency of the conclusions. I say then that 10 divided: according to our proportion and its greater part will be R. 125 minus 5 above which by said effect placed 5. that is the half of whole 10. will make R. 125 precisely. Because that minus 5 comes to be restored and filled with plus 5 half of 10. This conjoint that is R. 125 multiplied in itself which makes 125 for its square will be 5 times the square of the half of 10. which is 5. its square 25. Wherefore 125 will be precisely quintuple to said 25. square of said half of 10 as was said. And this effect has place in every quantity of whatever nature it be as clearly demonstrates the first of the 13. of our guide.
