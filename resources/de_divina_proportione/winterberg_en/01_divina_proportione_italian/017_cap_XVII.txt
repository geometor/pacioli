Cap. XVII.

(Of the 8th effect converse of the preceding.)

If a line be divided according to the proportion having the mean and two extremes always of that circle of which the greater part will be side of the hexagon of the same the smaller will be side of the decagon. Verbi gratia. If the divided line were 10, its greater part which is R. 125 minus 5. always will be the side of the hexagon of a circle, of which the diameter will be the double of R. 125 minus 5. that is R. 500 minus 10. I say that of that same circle 15 minus R. 125. smaller part will be side of the equilateral decagon placed in it. And of this converse much makes use Ptolemy in the 9. chapter of the first direction of his Almagest to demonstrate the quantity of the chords of the arcs of the circle. Which similarly openly is demonstrated above the predicted 9. of the 13. geometrically.
