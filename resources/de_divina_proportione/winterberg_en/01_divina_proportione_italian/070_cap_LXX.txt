Cap. LXX.

(How are to be found all the bodies ordinately as they are placed in this made in perspective and also their material forms according to their particular table placed patent in public.)

Because where is not order always will be confusion therefore for full intelligence of this our compendium to know to find all the proper figures in perspective aspect in this proposed and also the material after their public table the your celsitude will observe this mode, that is when you will read above in their chapters of their creations and formations will look in which place of the book the number signed by abacus, ancient, that is thus commencing from the 1. to the 48. chapter saying. I. II. III. IV. V. and following up to their term. And that same number exactly you will make to find before where in this said bodies are by order all figured. The which number similarly in that place will be placed, referring I. to I and II. to II. and III. to III. and so in all. And that such figure will be of the said body made in plane with all perfection of perspective as knows our Lionardo Vinci. And these same numbers also you will research among the material forms of said bodies pendent with their name in greek and in latin placed in a brief above each fixed in its cord between two black ambers, pure referring each as is said to the number there placed where of that such is treated. and Your celsitude, to the one and to the other mode will have their dispositions. The which not of vile matter (as by inopia [poverty] to me has been force) but of precious metal and fine gems would merit to be adorned. But your celsitude will consider the effect and the mind in his perpetual servant.
