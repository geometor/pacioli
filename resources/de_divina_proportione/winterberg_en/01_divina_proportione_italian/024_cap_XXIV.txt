Cap. XXIV.

(How said effects concur to the composition of all the regular bodies and their dependents.)

Now exalted Duke the virtue and power of the aforesaid our proportion with its singular effects chiefly as above we said manifests itself in the formation and composition of the bodies both regular and dependent. Of which so that better is apprehended here following ordinately we will speak of them. And first of the 5 essential which by another name are called regular. And then successively of as many enough of their egregious dependents. But first is to clarify why they be called regular bodies. Secondarily is to prove how in nature it be not possible to form a 6th. Wherefore the said are called regular because they are of sides and angles and bases equal and the one from the other exactly is contained as will be shown and correspond to the 5 simple bodies in nature that is earth, water, air, fire and fifth essence that is celestial virtue that sustains all the others in their being. And just as these 5 simple are sufficient and enough in nature otherwise would be to argue God superfluous or diminished to the natural need. Which thing is absurd as affirms the philosopher that God and nature do not operate in vain that is do not lack to the need and do not exceed that so similarly the forms of these 5 bodies of which is to say exactly are 5 ad decorem universi [to the decorum of the universe] and they cannot be more for that which will follow. And therefore not unmeritedly as will be said below the ancient Plato in his Timaeus attributed the figures of said regular to the 5 simple bodies as in the fifth convenience of the divine name to our proportion attributed above was said and this as to their denomination.
