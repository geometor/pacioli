Cap. XIX.

(Of its 10th supreme effect.)

If a quantity be divided according to the prediction proportion all the effects that of it and its parts can come those same in habitude number species and genus come from whatever other quantity so divided. Verbi gratia. Let there be two lines so divided that is one ab. divided in c. and its greater part be ac. and the other de. and its greater part be df. And as we say of these two so we understand of infinite others which easily can by way of arithmetic be assigned placing ab. 10. ac. would be R. 125. minus 5. and the other 15 minus R. 125. And placing de. 12. df. would be R. 180. minus 6. and the other would be 18 minus R. 180. I say that all that which ever can happen to one of said lines compared multiplied divided and in all other modes worked. The similar happens always to the other that is from each to its greater part will be the same proportion and so from each to its smaller part will be the same proportion. And so conversely from each of their parts to them all, and so the product of one in its parts and converse to said parts and so in dividing and subtracting happens. Wherefore the proportion which is from 10. to its greater part R. 125 minus 5. will be that same which is from 12 to its greater part R. 180 minus 6. and the proportion which from the sum of 10 and R. 125 minus 5, to R. 125 minus 5 that same will be of the sum of 12 and R. 180 minus 6. to R. 180 minus 6. And so briefly in infinite [cases] taken and turned quantocumque qualitercumque by the permuted converse conjoint disjoint eversed and equal proportionality always will agree to a same denomination and to the same effects intensively which thing without fail demonstrates greatest harmony in all quantities so divided. As below will appear in the regular and dependent bodies, and all this concludes in substance the 2. of the 14. geometrically.
