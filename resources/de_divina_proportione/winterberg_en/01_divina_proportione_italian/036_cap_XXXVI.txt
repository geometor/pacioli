Cap. XXXVI.

(Of the inclusion of the octahedron in the cube.)

And wishing the eight bases that is octahedron in the hexahedron to form. First needs in the cube to have the equilateral triangular pyramid fabricated whose sides as was said are the 6 diameters of its bases. And therefore if each of said diameters by equals we will divide and those mid points with straight lines one with the other we will join without doubt in the proposed cube will be exactly the octahedron formed and every its solid angle exactly will stop itself in the bases of said cube by the 3. of the 15.
