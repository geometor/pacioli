Cap. XXXVII.

(The fabric of the hexahedron in the octahedron.)

The hexahedron or true cube in the octahedron will be made in this mode that is. First we will make said octahedron according to the documents given above in this. Which so formed from each of its triangular bases by the 5. of the 4. find the center. Which 8 centers then we will join one with the other by means of 12 straight lines. And we will have the intent concluded. And each of the solid angles of the cube will come to stop itself on the base of said octahedron as the 4. of the 15. declares.
