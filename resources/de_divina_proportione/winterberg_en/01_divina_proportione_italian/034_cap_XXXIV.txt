Cap. XXXIV.

(Of the inclusions of the 5 regular [bodies] one in the other and the other in the one and how many they be in all and why.)

Follows now to clarify how one of these 5 essential bodies that is regular one be contained by the other and which yes and which no and why. Wherefore first of the tetrahedron speaking is shown it not to be able by any mode in itself receive other than the octahedron that is body of 8 triangular bases and of 6 solid angles. Because in it are neither sides nor bases nor angles in which can the sides of the cube nor of its angles nor surfaces lean in mode that they touch equally according to what requires their true inscription as its material form to the eye demonstrates to us and by true science in the 1. of the 15. will be manifest. Nor also of none of the other two that is icosahedron or dodecahedron. When then we would want said octahedron in said 4 bases or true tetrahedron to inscribe or form in this mode we will do it that is. First we will fabricate said tetrahedron as above we have taught. The which so made then we will divide each side of it by equals and their mid points all we will continue with straight lines one with the other and the other with the one. Which thing done that be without doubt said body in that exactly we will have situated in mode that its 6 solid angles on the 6 sides of the said tetrahedron will be leaned equally. Which thing the material experience will render open and the 2. of the 15. manifest.
