Cap. XLIV.

(Of the fabric of the cube in the icosahedron.)

Is formed the cube in the icosahedron if first in that be made the dodecahedron as before we said and then in [it] dodecahedron be made the cube in the mode given. Which things done will appear the intent to be expedited by the things before said. Because the solid angles of the dodecahedron all fall in the center of the bases of the icosahedron. And the solid angles of the cube fall in said solids of the dodecahedron and by consequence the intent will be expedited. which also from the 11. of the 15 to us is declared.
