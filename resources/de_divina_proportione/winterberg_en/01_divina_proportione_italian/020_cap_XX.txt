Cap. XX.

(Of its 11th most excellent effect.)

If one will divide the side of an equilateral hexagon according to our divine proportion always its greater part of necessity will be the side of the decagon circumscribed by the same circle as the hexagon. Verbi gratia. If the side of the hexagon were 10 divided in said mode its greater part will be R. 125 minus 5. which I say exactly to be the side of the decagon from the same circle circumscribed. Of which the diameter would come to be 20. and this will be concluded by the 3. of the 14. Wherefore by evidence had the side of one easily is found the side of the other and so had the diameter of the circle or its circumference or its area or of whatever part his always by that we can come to the notice of the one or of the other by the one and so by converse in all the modes of circle hexagon decagon and also triangle operating arithmetically and geometrically which most useful thing will be just as above in the 9. effect of the pentagon was said.
