Cap. XL.

(Of the collocation of the icosahedron in the dodecahedron.)

When one will want in the dodecahedron the icosahedron to form first that we will fabricate according to the document above in this given. And of its 12 pentagons that contain it the center we will find as teaches the 14. of the 4. And those among them with 30 lines we will join in mode that in it will be caused 20 triangles and 12 solid angles each contained by 5 superficial angles of said triangles. Of which their points will be in the 12 centers of its 12 pentagons. And similarly these its 30 lines oppose themselves in cross to the 30 of the dodecahedron just as those to these was said and also by the 7. of the said 15. appears.
