Cap. LXVIII.

(How are measured the short pyramids.)

For the short or true cut pyramids their measure is found by means of their integral [ones], to the which as the imperfect to its perfect they reduce themselves in this mode. First the said short we will reduce to the integral up to its cone with the mode given in our public work. And that such integral we will measure by the modes before said, and we will have clear all its capacity which we will save. Afterwards we will take the measure of that small pyramid that was added to the cut to make it integral pure with the modes given, and the quantity of this small pyramid we will take from the quantity of all the large that we saved. The remainder of necessity comes to be the quantity exactly of the said truncate pyramid and of the other ways this be the briefest and most secure. and be they round or laterated the same is observed etc.
