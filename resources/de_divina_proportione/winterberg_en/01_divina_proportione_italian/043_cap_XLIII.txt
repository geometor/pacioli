Cap. XLIII.

(Of the inclusion of the tetrahedron in said dodecahedron.)

The tetrahedron also in the same dodecahedron will be placed if first in it be formed the cube as was said and then in said cube be placed the tetrahedron as also was shown. The which things done that they be clear will appear to be our purpose concluded in this mode that is. Since that the solid angles of the cube pose themselves in the solid angles of the dodecahedron. And the solid angles of the tetrahedron stop themselves in those of the cube follows the said tetrahedron duly to the proposed dodecahedron to be included which our experience in the materials by us composed and to the hands of your celsitude offered makes it manifest with the scientific demonstration of the 10. of said 15.
