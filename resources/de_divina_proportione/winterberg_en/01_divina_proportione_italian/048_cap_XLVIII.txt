Cap. XLVIII.

(Of the form and disposition of the tetrahedron plane solid or true vacuum [hollow] and of the abscinded solid plane or true vacuum and of the elevated solid or true vacuum.)

I. II. The tetrahedron plane solid or true vacuum will be formed by 6 equal lines which contain 12 superficial angles and 4 solid and make among them 4 equilateral and equiangular triangular bases.

III. IV. Of the cut or true abscinded.

The tetrahedron cut or we want to say abscinded plane solid or true vacuum will be contained by 18 lines which cause 36 superficial angles and 12 solid and 8 bases surround it of which 4 are hexagonal and 4 equilateral trigonal that is of 6 sides but material to our eye renders clear and born from the preceding in its sides by third uniform cut.

V. VI. The tetrahedron elevated or we want to say pointed solid or true vacuum has similarly 18 lines of which 6 are common and has 36 superficial angles and 8 solid of which 4 are cones of the superficial pyramids and 4 are common to the 5 pyramids that is to that interior that the eye cannot see but only the intellect takes it and to the other 4 exterior of which 5 pyramids said body be composed when they be among them equilateral triangular and equiangular as its proposed material form to us demonstrates. And its surfaces that clothe it which not properly are called bases in all are 12 by number all triangular. And of this one cannot by any mode assign the elevated abscinded for the defect of the hexagons that do not make solid angles.
