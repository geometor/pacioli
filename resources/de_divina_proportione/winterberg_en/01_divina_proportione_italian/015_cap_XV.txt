Cap. XV.

(Of its sixth unnameable effect.)

No rational quantity is possible to be divided according to our said proportion that its every part be not irrational called residue. Verbi gratia. Let 10. be the rational quantity which is to be divided after the proportion having the mean and two extremes. I say of necessity each of the parts must be residue. Wherefore one will be 15 minus R. 125. that is the smaller and the other greater will be R. 125 minus 5. The why appears each to be residue, because so they are called in the art according to the 79. of the 10. And this such effect we have from the sixth of the 13.
