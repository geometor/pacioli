Cap. XLI.

(Of the situation of the cube in the dodecahedron.)

The cube also we will make in said dodecahedron easily considering that it is formed on the 12 sides of the cube as in the 17. of the 13. is contained. Because if to each of its 12 pentagons after the exigency of the said be drawn 12 chords without doubt will be formed 6 quadrangular equilateral surfaces and [to] each of those will be opposite two solid angles of said dodecahedron and in 8 its [angles] will be formed 8 of the cube inscribed in mode that on each base of the cube comes to remain the form almost of the seratile [serrated/saw-toothed?] body which all will be clear by the 8 of the 15.
