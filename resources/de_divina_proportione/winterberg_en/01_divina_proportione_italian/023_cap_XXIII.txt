Cap. XXIII.

(How by reverence of our salvation said effects terminate.)

It does not seem to me exalted Duke in more [of] its infinite effects at present to extend myself because the paper would not supply the ink to express them all but only these 13 we have among the others elected to reverence of the crowd dozen [twelve] and of its most holy head our redeemer Christ Jesus since having attributed to them the divine name also for the number of our salvation of the 13 articles, and 12 apostles with our savior usually to terminate of which college I comprehend Your Ducal celsitude to have singular devotion for having it in the pre-adduced place most sacred temple of graces by our prefaced Lionardo with his light brush made to dispose nevertheless in the sequent process one will not rest more others according to the occurrences to adduce since as will be said that it be not possible to be able to form nor imagine the harmony and worthy convenience among them of all the regular bodies and their dependents, to whose end the already said we have proposed so that their sequel more clear renders itself.
