Cap. LX.

(Of the quadrilateral laterated columns.)

XLIII. XLVI. Of the laterated the second sort are quadrilateral and are those that have the two bases in mode said quadrangular and four other surfaces that surround it are pure quadrilateral equidistant among them according to their opposition. and these similarly are at times equilateral at times inequilateral according to the disposition of their bases, because of the plane quadrilateral rectilinear figures are assigned 4 sorts: the one called square, and be that which has the sides all equal, and the angles right as here at side the figure. A. The other called tetragon oblong and be that which has the opposite sides equal and the angles similarly right: but is more long than wide, as here at side the figure B. The third sort be called elmuaym. which is figure equilateral but not rectangular and by other name be called rhombus as here the figure C. The fourth sort be of the similar to the elmuaym or true rhomboid by other name, of the which the sides only opposite are equal and among them equidistant and has not right angles, as appears the figure D. All the other figures from these outside that be of 4 sides are called elmuariffe. that is irregular. as are the figures marked E. Now according to all these diversities of bases can be varied said quadrilateral columns. But as one wants always the equidistance between their bases by height must be understood. And these such we can call regular to similitude of their bases, the others regular [irregular] or true elmuariffe.
