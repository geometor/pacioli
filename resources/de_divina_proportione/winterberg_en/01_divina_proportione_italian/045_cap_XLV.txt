Cap. XLV.

(Of the mode to form the tetrahedron in the icosahedron.)

Is no doubt if in the said icosahedron is formed the cube as above we taught and then in [it] cube is fabricated the tetrahedron of necessity that also will come to be inscribed to the said icosahedron. Because the solid angles of the pyramid [of] 4 triangular bases touch those solids of the cube and those of the cube touch those of the icosahedron follows from first to last those of the tetrahedron to touch equally those of the icosahedron. And by consequence our purpose concluded by the 12. of the 15. And this as to their proposed inclusions appertains.
