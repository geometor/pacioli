Cap. LIX.

(Of the laterated columns and first of the trilateral.)

XLVI. XLVII. Another species or true sort of columns are called laterated of the which the first is triangular of the which its bases that is supreme and inferior: are two triangles equidistant between them according to the height of the column as the here figured. Of the which the supreme base be the triangle abc. and the inferior the triangle def. And this similar figure says our author to be called seratile body and be similar to the peak of a roof of a house that has 4 faces or true walls that only from two cantos its roof rain: as the eye demonstrates and can be the bases equilateral and not equilateral. And of similar columns the 3 faces are always parallelograms that is of 4 sides and rectangular: so that said seratile body be contained by 5 surfaces of the which 3 are quadrangular and the two are [triangular].
