Cap. XLII.

(Of the octahedron in the dodecahedron how it is formed.)

If in the dodecahedron first the cube be disposed as in the preceding was said easily in the said dodecahedron will be formed the octahedron. Because we will divide the 6 opposite sides of the dodecahedron to the 6 surfaces of the cube by equals that is those sides which almost make summit to the seratile which exactly are 6. And those their 6 mid points we will continue by 12 straight lines all among them in mode that they will come to cause 6 solid angles contained each by 4 superficial angles of the 4 triangles of the octahedron. And each touches one of said 6 sides of the dodecahedron and by consequence determines to be the question concluded just as in the 9. of the 15 is contained.
