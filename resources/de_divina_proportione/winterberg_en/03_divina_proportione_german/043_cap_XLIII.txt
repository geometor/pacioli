Cap. XLIII.

Of the inclusion of the Tetrahedron in said Dodecahedron.

The Tetrahedron further will be placed into the same Dodecahedron,
if one first forms the Cube in it, as sta-
ted, and then, as likewise shown, places the Tetra-
hedron in said Cube. If these things have happened,
so illuminates clearly, that our proposed be enclosed,
and indeed in this way: So far as the corners of the Cube place themselves in the
corners of the Dodecahedron and the corners of the Tetra-
hedron fix themselves in those of the Cube, so follows, that said
Tetrahedron is enclosed in the assumed Dodecahedron,
as it must be, what our experience in the by us de-
signed models and brought to Your Highness' hands, at the same
time with the scientific proof of the tenth of the
said twelfth reveals.
