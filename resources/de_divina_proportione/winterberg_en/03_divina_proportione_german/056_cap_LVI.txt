Cap. LVI.

Of the spherical Body and its Formation.

XL. By Many has been defined the sphere, what it
be; especially by Dionysius, a worthy Mathematician.
Also our Author describes it with greatest brevity in his
eleventh and that description is pulled by all Later ones,
where he says as follows: Sphere is the body, which contains the
Trace of the Arc of the Periphery of the Semicircle in every Rotation
and one take on any way the Semicircle, by holding fast
the line of the diameter, named Arc turn itself so far around, until it returns to the place, from which it
began to move, i.e. after one has made the semicircle over
any arbitrary line, so one lead, by holding
the latter fast, said semicircle all around with its whole
revolution: the so described body is called Sphere.
Its Center is that of the named so led around Semi-
circle. It be e.g. the over the line ab described Semi-
circle e, by making the point e the Center, and its
whole Arc be the part of the Periphery adb. I assert,
that, if one holds fast the line ab, which is diameter of na-
med Semicircle, and leads that one over it around, by beginning
from the point d, to go with its Arc to the lower
side and to return to the upper to the named
point d, i) from where it moved first; or
also, if one goes oppositely to the upper and
then also to the upper with the named Arc to the point d
returns, that by the said Semicircle at its revolu-
tion described Rounding said spherical body and
Sphere is, by imagining, as it must be, that sa-
id Semicircle be for example a half material plate,
because it in other way would form no body, because the
Arc alone at its leading around leaves no trace behind,
since it a line without extension and depth. And that be to
its knowledge and about its origin said.
