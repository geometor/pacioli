Cap. LIX.

Of the angular, and indeed first of the three-sided Columns.

XLVI.XLVII. Another kind or sort of columns are sa-
id angular, whereof the first is three-edged, whose two base sur-
faces, i.e. upper and lower, are two corresponding to the height of the column 
equally far from each other standing Triangles, as
the here recorded, whose highest Basis the triangle abc, and
the lower the triangle def is. And such a figure, says our
Author, be called bolt-shaped body and it is similar to the gable
of a roof of a house, which has four side surfaces
or walls, so that only from two edges its roof
rains: as the appearance teaches it, and the base sur-
faces can be equilateral and inequilateral. And at such
columns are the three side surfaces always Parallelograms, i.e. of
four sides and right-angled, so that said bolt-shaped
body is contained by five surfaces, whereof three four-sided
and two three-sided are.
