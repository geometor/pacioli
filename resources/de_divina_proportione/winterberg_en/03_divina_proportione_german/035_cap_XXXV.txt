Cap. XXXV.

How the named Tetrahedron be formed and placed into the Cube.

The named Tetrahedron is placed in following way into the
Cube, namely: First we will form the Cube, according to the above
stated methods, then we will in
each of its six quadratic surfaces draw the diagonal or
the diameter and the demanded body becomes
thereby enclosed, as the first of the fifteenth proves,
because named Tetrahedron, as has been said, has six edges,
which correspond to the number of the six surfaces of the Cube,
and as such have resulted its six diagonals, which
drawn on its surfaces. And the four corners of the pyramid
have established themselves in four of the eight of the named Cube,
as besides the Teacher of all things, the holy
Experience reveals in its material forms.
