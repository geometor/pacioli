Cap. XV.

Of its sixth unnamable effect.

No rational magnitude can ever be divided according to our said
proportion such, without that each of its segments be
irrational - called Residual - , e.g. let ten be the rational
magnitude, which one shall divide according to the proportion containing a mean and two extreme
segments. I say, that each of its parts must necessarily be a Residual. Accordingly
the one will be 15 - sqrt(125), i.e. the smaller, and the other,
greater sqrt(125) - 5. And therefore it is evident, that each be a
Residual, as they are called in technical language, according to the 79th
of the tenth. And this so constituted effect we have
from the sixth of the 13th.
