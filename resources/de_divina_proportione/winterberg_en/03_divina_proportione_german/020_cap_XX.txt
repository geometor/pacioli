Cap. XX.

Of its 11th most distinguished effect.

If one will divide the side of an equilateral hexagon according to
our divine proportion, so will its greater
segment always necessarily be the side of the decagon circumscribed by the same circle as the
hexagon. If e.g. the side of the hexagon divided in the
said way were = 10, so
will its greater segment be sqrt(125) - 5, which, I assert,
is exactly the side of the decagon circumscribed by the same circle,
whose diameter would amount to 20. And
this is concluded from the third of the fourteenth. Therefore
becomes apparent, after one obtained the side of the one,
the side of the other easily found and likewise one can,
after obtained the diameter of the circle or its circumference or
its area or that of any part of it,
always arrive thereby at the knowledge of the one or the other
through the one, and likewise in all ways vice versa
from the circle, hexagon, decagon and also triangle through
arithmetic and geometric operation - which is a very use-
ful thing, as has been said above at the ninth effect regarding the
pentagon. Therefore etc.
