Cap. XXXVII.

The insertion of the Hexahedron into the Octahedron.

The Hexahedron or the Cube will be inscribed in following way into the
Octahedron. First will we on ground
of the previously in this Tractate given teachings form the named
Octahedron. After this so produced, so find of each
of its triangular surfaces according to the fifth of the fourth its center.
These eight midpoints we will then connect with each other by
twelve straight lines, and obtain thus the inten-
ded Enclosed. And each corporeal corner of the Cube will
stand on the basis of the named Octahedron, as the fourth
of the fifteenth explains.
