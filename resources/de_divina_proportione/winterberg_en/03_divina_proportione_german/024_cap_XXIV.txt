Cap. XXIV.

How the named effects contribute to the composition of all regular bodies and of those dependent on them.

Now, Exalted Duke, reveals itself the virtue and
power of our aforementioned proportion with its pecu-
liar effects mostly, as we have said above in
the formation and composition as well of the regular
bodies, as of those dependent on them. For better understanding
we will here in the following, speak of them according to the order;
and indeed first of the five essential ones, which with
other name are called regular, then gradually
of some sufficiently outstanding ones dependent on them.
First however is to be explained, why the named bodies be regu-
lar. Secondly is to be proven, how in nature the
formation of a sixth is not possible. Therefore the
named are designated as regular, since they are of equal sides
angles and surfaces and the one is exactly contained in the other, *)
as will show itself, and they correspond to
the five simple elements in nature, i.e. Earth, Water,
Air, Fire and fifth element namely heavenly virtue, which
supports all others in their existence. And as these five sim-
ple elements in nature are sufficient and adequate, other-
wise God would be to be considered superfluous or too
little for the natural need which is absurd, as the Philosopher
assures, since God and Nature do not operate in vain, i.e.
let it not be lacking in the Necessary, nor exceed that:
likewise are in same manner of the forms of these five
bodies, of which is to be said, exactly five for the ornament of the Uni-
verse, and there can not be more of them according to what
will follow. And not unjustly therefore wrote as further
below will be mentioned, the old Plato in his Timaeus the
figures of named regular bodies to the five simple
elements, as at the fifth suitability reason for the
name attributed to our proportion: the divine has been
said above. And so much regarding their naming.
