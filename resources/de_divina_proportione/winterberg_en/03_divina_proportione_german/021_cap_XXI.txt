Cap. XXI.

Of its twelfth almost incomprehensible effect.

If a magnitude is divided according to our named proportion,
so behaves the root of the sum of
the square of the whole magnitude and the square of its
greater segment to the root of the sum of the square
of named magnitude and the square of its smaller segment,
as the side of the Cube to the side of the Triangle of the icosahedral
body.*) Let be for example 10 the magnitude divided according to the proportion, which
has a mean and two extreme segments,
so that the one part, namely the greater, as frequently
said, is sqrt(125) - 5 and the smaller 15 - sqrt(125). Now
one squares the named given magnitude 10, i.e. multi-
plies it with itself, so that yields 100, and one squares
further its greater segment, namely sqrt(125) - 5, which multi-
plied with itself will yield 150 - sqrt(12500), and
one squares also the smaller segment, i.e. 15 - sqrt(125), which
multiplied with itself makes 350 - sqrt(112500). Now one adds to
the square of the greater segment, namely to 150 - sqrt(12500)
the square of the whole magnitude namely 10, which is 100, so
results 250 - sqrt(12500). The same square of the named magnitude,
i.e. 100, one adds to the square of the smaller segment,
which, as we found, 350 - sqrt(112500), to which 100 added,
will result 450 - sqrt(112500). Now I assert, that the
ratio of the root of the one sum, i.e. 250 - sqrt(12500)
which was formed from the square of the named magnitude and that of the
greater segment, to the root of the other
sum, which is formed from the square of the named magnitude and that
of its smaller segment, i.e. 450 - sqrt(112500),
be exactly the same, as the ratio of the side of the Cube to the
Triangle-side of the icosahedral body; if both bodies
are circumscribed or enclosed by one and the same sphere.
These roots of the sums are designated as Power-lines *) of the
named sums, i.e. sqrt(250 - sqrt(12500)) signifies
a magnitude, whose power or square is exactly the named
sum. And likewise signifies sqrt(450 - sqrt(112500)) a magnitude,
whose power, or we will say square, is exactly 450 - sqrt(112500),
which roots with other name are called by the practitioners
universal roots or also connected roots
as also becomes clear in our aforementioned work in the third
Tractate of its eighth distinctio at the beginning of Sheet 120
of the named Volume. The investigation of these magnitudes
is highly subtle and they refer to speculative practice,
as it reveals itself in detailed manner in the named Volume.
- And these all, Exalted Prince, it is not possible,
to indicate with lower characteristics. And this whole specu-
lative effect is proven geometrically by means of the ninth of the fourteenth
besides some others cited in that place by
Campanus.
