Cap. XXIII.

How out of reverence for our Salvation the named effects end.

It seems to me, Exalted Duke, not appropriate, to spread myself
over still more of its infinite effects for now,
because the paper would not suffice the ink, to express them
all, rather we have chosen only these thirteen among
the others, out of reverence for the Troop of the Twelve and
its holiest Head, our Redeemer Jesus Christ. For since
we have attributed to it the divine name also according to the number of 13 articles
with reference to our Salvation and indeed of the twelve Apostles with our
Redeemer, so be they herewith ended; before which
College I understand, that Your Ducal Highness has special
devotion, insofar as You have had it designed in the aforementioned place, the
holiest Temple of Graces by our aforementioned Leonardo
with his skillful brush. Neverthe-
less in the following progress will not be omitted according to the
occasional needs to cite still Others thereof,
insofar as it, as will be explained, is not possible,
to be able to produce and think the harmony and worthy agreement of all regular
bodies and of those dependent on them among themselves [without it]. To this end we have set up the already
named, so that the consequence from them would result more clearly.
