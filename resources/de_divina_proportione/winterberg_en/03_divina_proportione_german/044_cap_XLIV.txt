Cap. XLIV.

(How the Cube be formed in the Icosahedron).

The Cube is formed in the Icosahedron, if one
in the latter first describes the Dodecahedron, as we said before,
and then describes in this Dodecahedron the Cube in the stated
manner. After this happened, will illuminate, that the
Intended is completed according to the previously Said. For
all corners of the Dodecahedron fall in the Center of the
Icosahedron surfaces and the corners of the Cube in the said corners
of the Dodecahedron, and consequently is the body in speech
completed, what also is explained to us by the eleventh of the fif-
teenth.
