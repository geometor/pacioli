Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Plates Referenced**:

  - **Plate XXXVII** (``0265.rst``): :term:`*Vigintisex Basium Elevatum Solidum* <vigintisex-basium-elevatum>` (Cumulated :term:`rhombicuboctahedron <vigintisex-basium>`, 96 triangular faces, solid).
  - **Plate XXXVIII** (``0267.rst``): :term:`*Vigintisex Basium Elevatum Vacuum* <vigintisex-basium-elevatum>` (Cumulated :term:`rhombicuboctahedron <vigintisex-basium>`, skeletal).
- **Jacopo de' Barbari's 1495 Portrait of Pacioli**: The glass :term:`*Vigintisex Basium* <vigintisex-basium>` half-filled with water hanging from a thin cord in Jacopo de' Barbari's famous 1495 Capodimonte portrait of :person:`Fra Luca Pacioli <pacioli>` (and the wooden :term:`dodecahedron <dodecahedron>` resting on :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` *Summa*) is this exact 26-base :term:`rhombicuboctahedron <vigintisex-basium>`!

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Plates Referenced**:
  - **Plate XXXIX** (``0269.rst``): *Septuagintaduarum Basium Planum Solidum* (72-base faceted sphere, solid).
  - **Plate XL** (``0271.rst``): *Septuagintaduarum Basium Vacuum* (72-base faceted sphere, hollow/coffered).
- **Euclid Book XII, Proposition 17 (Campanus XII.14)**: :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s construction in :cite:`Euclid XII.17 <euclid:elements:XII.17>` inscribes a polyhedron of quadrangular (trapezoidal) zones and polar triangles between two concentric spheres by subdividing the equator and meridians into :math:`4n` equal parts—the exact classical source for Renaissance perspective drawings of the *mazzocchio* (by Paolo Uccello, :person:`Piero della Francesca <piero-della-francesca>` in :cite:`*De Prospectiva Pingendi* <piero:prospectiva>`, and :person:`Leonardo da Vinci <leonardo>`) and for the coffered hemisphere of the :term:`**Pantheon** <rotonda-pantheon>` (:term:`*La Rotonda* <rotonda-pantheon>`) and :person:`Bramante <bramante>`'s **Santa Maria presso San Satiro** and :term:`**Santa Maria delle Grazie** <santa-maria-delle-grazie>` in Milan.
- **Comparison with Earlier Draft Track**: The earlier ``divina_en_2/54.txt`` draft omitted :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` discussion of the :term:`Pantheon <rotonda-pantheon>` (:term:`*La Rotonda* <rotonda-pantheon>`), :term:`Santa Maria delle Grazie <santa-maria-delle-grazie>`, San Satiro, :person:`Ludovico Sforza <ludovico-sforza>`'s devices (*la scopetta* and *la cetta*), and :person:`Pythagoras <pythagoras>`'s *Angulus Iustitiae*.
