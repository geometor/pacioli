Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Date and Context (Venice, May 1, 1509)**: While Part I (:cite:`*Compendio de Divina Proportione* <pacioli:divina-proportione>`) was completed in Milan on December 14, 1498, this dedicatory epistle to Part II (:cite:`*Tractato de l'Architectura* <pacioli:architectura>`) is explicitly dated **Venice, Kalends of May 1509** (*Ex Venitiis Kal. Maii MDIX*), just one month before :person:`Paganino de' Paganini <paganino-de-paganini>` finished printing the entire volume on June 1, 1509.
- **Autobiographical & Prosopographical Treasure**: This preface is one of the primary biographical sources for :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` life and family:
  - It names :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` **two blood brothers** (*miei carnali fratelli*), both Franciscan friars: **Master Ginepro** (*M. Zinipero*) and **Fra Ambrogio**, and his nephew **Francesco Pacioli** (who died of dysentery at Ragusa/Dubrovnik with Captain Benedetto Baiardo).
  - It records that :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>` lectured on :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s *Elements* for **three years** to the condottiere :person:`**Camillo Vitelli** <camillo-vitelli>` (c. 1459–1496, brother of Niccolò and Paolo Vitelli of Città di Castello), and participated in discussions on fortification in Naples at the palace of Count Francesco Coppola of Sarno with the humanist :person:`**Giovanni Pontano** <giovanni-pontano>` and the Florentine diplomat :person:`**Pier Vettori** <pier-vettori>`, and in Milan with :person:`**Gian Giacomo Trivulzio** <gian-giacomo-trivulzio>`, :person:`**Galeazzo Sanseverino** <galeazzo-sanseverino>`, and :person:`**Ludovico Sforza** <ludovico-sforza>`.
  - It documents :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` closeness to :person:`**Piero della Francesca** <piero-della-francesca>` (*nostro conterraneo e contemporale... Monarca Maestro Petro de' Franceschi*), :person:`**Lorenzo Canozi da Lendinara** <lorenzo-canozi>` (1425–1477), and Lorenzo's son :person:`**Giovan Marco Canozi** <giovan-marco-canozi>` (:person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` *compare*).
- **Comparison with Earlier Draft Track**: The earlier ``winterberg_en`` draft compressed the three-folio roll of honor of the captains of :term:`Borgo Sansepolcro <san-sepolcro>`. Here every single name, battle, castle, and biographical detail from Folios ``0061.rst``–``0065.rst`` (``f. 23r``–``f. 25r``) is translated in full without abridgment.
