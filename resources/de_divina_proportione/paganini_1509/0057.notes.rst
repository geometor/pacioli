Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagrams**: Folio 0057 (``f. 21r``) displays two woodcut diagrams (``0057.g1.png`` and ``0057.g2.png``) showing the quadrilateral :math:`abcd` divided by diagonal :math:`bd` into 2 triangles (:math:`\triangle abd, \triangle bcd`) and the pentagon :math:`abcde` divided from vertex :math:`a` by chords :math:`ac, ad` into 3 triangles (:math:`\triangle abc, \triangle acd, \triangle ade`).
- **Additivity of Ratios (Euclid V.12)**: Notice how :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>` uses :cite:`Euclid V.12 <euclid:elements:V.12>` (if :math:`A_1 : B_1 = A_2 : B_2 = A_3 : B_3 = 3 : 1`, then :math:`(A_1+A_2+A_3) : (B_1+B_2+B_3) = 3 : 1`) to extend the :math:`3:1` prism-to-pyramid volume theorem from the triangular prism (:term:`*corpo seratile* <corpo-seratile>`, :cite:`Euclid XII.7 <euclid:elements:XII.7>`) to any :math:`n`-gonal prism decomposed into :math:`n-2` triangular prisms.

Cap. LXVIII — How Short or Truncated Pyramids (*Pyramidi Corte ouer Scapezze*) Are Measured.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Subtraction of the *Pyramidella* vs. Heron's Mean-Base Formula**: In the *:cite:`Summa de Arithmetica <pacioli:summa>`* (Part II, *Tractatus Geometriae*, Dist. VI), Pacioli gives both this geometric completion-and-subtraction method (:math:`V = \frac{1}{3}B_1 h_{\text{total}} - \frac{1}{3}B_2 h_{\text{top}}`) and the equivalent algebraic formula :math:`V = \frac{1}{3}h(B_1 + \sqrt{B_1 B_2} + B_2)`. Notice how the completion-and-subtraction method prepares the exact conceptual model for measuring **truncated (*:term:`abscisi <abscisum>`*) polyhedra** in the very next chapter (``Cap. LXIX``)!
