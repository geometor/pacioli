Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagrams & Full-Page Plate**:
  - Folio 0066 (``f. 25v``) contains the two woodcut profile diagrams ``0066.g1.png`` and ``0066.g2.png``, and Folio 0137 (``0137.rst``) presents the large full-page woodcut of the human head inscribed in the equilateral triangle :math:`amk` and grid :math:`kmsb` labeled **DIVINA PROPORTIO**.
- **Catalog of Fifteen-Century Artillery**: Notice :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` remarkable list of 11 siege engines and gunpowder artillery weapons on Folio 0065 (``f. 25r``): *briccole, trabocchi, catapulte, baliste, bombarde, passavolanti, schioppetti, archibusi, cortaldi, basilischi*.
- **Rational Canon vs. Irrational *Gratia***: Pacioli's aesthetic reflection on Folio 0066—that the primary structural divisions of the face (:math:`\frac{1}{3}`, :math:`\frac{1}{9}`) are **rational**, whereas the living curvature of the chin, brow, and nose-tip involves **irrational proportions** left to the *gratia e arbitrio del prospectivo*—is a foundational statement of High Renaissance art theory (anticipating Vasari's concept of *grazia* and *giudizio dell'occhio*).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- *:person:`*Alberti's * <alberti>`Velo*, Leonardo's *Pariete*, and Dürer's *Drahtgitter* (*La Rete*)**: Pacioli's detailed description on Folio 0067 (``f. 26r``) of the painter's **perspective net** (*rete*)—a wooden frame strung with lute-strings, silk threads, or sinews fixed between the artist's eye and the subject and matched to a squared drawing surface—links Leon Battista Alberti's *velo* (*De pictura*, II.31, 1435), Piero della Francesca's coordinate projections in *:cite:`De Prospectiva Pingendi <piero:prospectiva>`*, Leonardo da Vinci's *pariete di vetro* / net, and Albrecht Dürer's famous woodcuts of the draftsman's net in the *Underweysung der Messung* (1525, composed after Dürer's 1506–1507 visit to Venice and Bologna where he met :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` circle!).
