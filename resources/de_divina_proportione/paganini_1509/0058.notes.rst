Cap. LXVIII — How Short or Truncated Pyramids (*Pyramidi Corte ouer Scapezze*) Are Measured.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Subtraction of the *Pyramidella* vs. Heron's Mean-Base Formula**: In the *:cite:`Summa de Arithmetica <pacioli:summa>`* (Part II, *Tractatus Geometriae*, Dist. VI), Pacioli gives both this geometric completion-and-subtraction method (:math:`V = \frac{1}{3}B_1 h_{\text{total}} - \frac{1}{3}B_2 h_{\text{top}}`) and the equivalent algebraic formula :math:`V = \frac{1}{3}h(B_1 + \sqrt{B_1 B_2} + B_2)`. Notice how the completion-and-subtraction method prepares the exact conceptual model for measuring **truncated (*:term:`abscisi <abscisum>`*) polyhedra** in the very next chapter (``Cap. LXIX``)!

Cap. LXIX — On the Measure of All the Regular and Dependent Bodies, and the Panegyric to Duke Ludovico Maria Sforza and the 1498 Franciscan General Chapter in Milan.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Additivity and Subtractivity of Polyhedral Volumes**: Mathematically, ``Cap. LXIX`` formulates the exact modern decomposition method for computing the volumes of Archimedean truncations (:math:`V_{\text{trunc}} = V_{\text{parent}} - \sum V_{\text{vertex pyramids}}`) and Catalan/stellated cumulations (:math:`V_{\text{elev}} = V_{\text{core}} + \sum V_{\text{face pyramids}}`).
- **Historical Witness to the Milanese Court in 1498**:
  - **The Ducal Chapel Choir (*dignissimi cantori*)**: Under Ludovico Sforza, the *Cappella Musicale* of the Castello Sforzesco (directed by Franchino Gaffurio and having included Josquin des Prez and Loyset Compère) was one of the premier musical institutions of Europe.
  - :term:`**Santa Maria delle Grazie** <santa-maria-delle-grazie>`: :person:`Bramante <bramante>`'s monumental tribune (commissioned by :person:`Ludovico Sforza <ludovico-sforza>` as the Sforza dynastic mausoleum) and :person:`Leonardo da Vinci <leonardo>`'s :term:`*Last Supper* <simulacro>` in its refectory were both being completed in 1497–1498.
  - **The Franciscan General Chapter of Pentecost 1498 in Milan**: Presided over by the famous Conventual Minister General **Francesco Sansone da Brescia** (1414–1499, General 1475–1499, also a patron of learning) and attended by **Cardinal Ippolito I d'Este** (1479–1520, Archbishop of Milan and brother of Duchess Beatrice d'Este). This passage confirms that Pacioli was writing the final chapters of the *Compendio* in Milan between June and December 1498.
