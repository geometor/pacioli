Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- *:person:`*Piero della Francesca's * <piero-della-francesca>`Legend of the True Cross* at Arezzo and *:cite:`De Prospectiva Pingendi <piero:prospectiva>`* at Urbino**: This passage on Folio 0081 (``f. 33r``) is one of the earliest and most authoritative Renaissance tributes to *:person:`*Piero della Francesca* <piero-della-francesca>`* (c. 1415–1492), listing every major city where Piero worked (**Urbino, Bologna, Ferrara, Rimini, Ancona, Sansepolcro, and Arezzo**), noting his mastery of both **oil and tempera/gouache on wall and panel** (*in muro e taula a oglio e guazzo*), singling out the **Cappella Maggiore of San Francesco in Arezzo** (*la magna capella dela tribuna del'altar grande*) as *"vna dele dignissime opere de Italia e da tutti commendata"*, and citing Piero's manuscript treatise *:cite:`De Prospectiva Pingendi <piero:prospectiva>`* in the Ducal Library of Urbino!

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- *:person:`*Leonardo da Vinci's * <leonardo>`Sala delle Asse* (*Camera dei Moroni*) in the Castello Sforzesco (1498)**: Look closely at what Pacioli reveals on Folio 0081 (``f. 33r``): in **1498**, in the *Camera dei Moroni* ("Chamber of the Mulberry Trees"—Leonardo da Vinci's famous **Sala delle Asse** in the northeast tower of the Castello Sforzesco, painted by Leonardo in **1498** with sixteen giant tree trunks rising up the walls and intertwining their branches across the vault!), Duke *:person:`*Ludovico Maria Sforza* <ludovico-sforza>`*, **Cardinal Ippolito d'Este**, *:person:`*Galeazzo Sanseverino* <galeazzo-sanseverino>`*, **Count Onofrio Paganini of Brescia** (kinsman of the printer Paganino de' Paganini!), and *:person:`*Fra Luca Pacioli* <pacioli>`* gathered to hear a formal exposition of **Vitruvius V.1.3 on how architecture imitates the tapering trunks and branches of living trees**! This firsthand eyewitness testimony directly connects Vitruvius V.1.3 and Plato's *:cite:`Timaeus <plato:timaeus:53c-56c>`* 53d to the iconographic program of Leonardo's *Sala delle Asse* in 1498.
- **The 1509 Venetian Colophon**: Folio 0082 (``f. 33v``) concludes the text of Part II with the official colophon of **Paganino de' Paganini of Brescia**, recording the 15-year copyright privilege granted by the Venetian Senate under **Doge Leonardo Loredan** (r. 1501–1521) and **Pope Julius II** (Year VI, **June 1, 1509**).
