Cap. LXVI — On the Way and Rule to Know How to Measure Every Complete Pyramid, Round or Lateral.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Epistemological Clarity on :math:`\pi \approx \frac{22}{7}` vs. :math:`V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3}V_{\text{cylinder}}`**: :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>` makes a remarkably sharp mathematical distinction on Folio 0056 (``f. 20v``): while the *numerical* area of the circular base (:math:`\frac{11}{14}d^{2}`) is an Archimedean approximation (*per la ignorantia dela quadratura del cerchio*), the *proportional* theorem that the cone is **exactly one-third** (:math:`\frac{1}{3}`) of the cylinder (:cite:`Euclid XII.10 <euclid:elements:XII.10>`, proved by Eudoxus's method of exhaustion) is **exact without the slightest error**!

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
