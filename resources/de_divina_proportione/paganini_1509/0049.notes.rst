Cap. LV — On the Way to Know How to Form Still More Bodies Beyond Those Mentioned, How Their Forms Proceed to Infinity, and Why Plato Assigned the Five Regular Bodies to the Five Simple Bodies of the Universe.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagrams**: Folio 0049 (``f. 17r``) contains two woodcut diagrams (``0049.g1.png`` and ``0049.g2.png``) illustrating the subdivision of the regular pentagon into 5 central triangles and each triangle into 6 scalene right triangles (:math:`5 \times 6 = 30` per pentagon, :math:`12 \times 30 = 360` over the whole :term:`dodecahedron <dodecahedron>`).
- **Philosophical Authorities Cited**:
  - :person:`**Plato** <plato>`, :cite:`*Timaeus* <plato:timaeus:53c-56c>` 53c–56c (the geometric atomism of the four elements and the cosmos).
  - **Alcinous** (*Didaskalikos* / *Epitome doctrinae Platonicae*, 13, translated into Latin by Marsilio Ficino and Pietro Balbi), source of the 12 signs / 360 triangles zodiacal correspondence (:math:`12 \times 5 \times 6 = 360`).
  - **Calcidius** (4th-century Latin translator and commentator on the :cite:`*Timaeus* <plato:timaeus:53c-56c>`), :person:`**Macrobius** <macrobius>` (*Commentarii in Somnium Scipionis*), and :person:`**Apuleius** <apuleius>` (*De Platone et eius dogmate*).
- **Comparison with Earlier Draft Track**: The earlier ``divina_en_2/55.txt`` draft compressed this major cosmological and Neoplatonic chapter into four short paragraphs and omitted the citations of Alcinous, Calcidius, :person:`Macrobius <macrobius>`, and :person:`Apuleius <apuleius>` as well as the :math:`12 \times 5 \times 6 = 360` Zodiacal triangulation explained by diagrams ``0049.g1.png`` and ``0049.g2.png``.

Cap. LVI — On the Spherical Body and How It Is Formed (Plates XLI–XLII).
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Plates Referenced**:
  - **Plate XLI** (``0273.rst``): *Sperico Solido* (Solid Sphere, labeled ``XL`` in the marginal roman numeral on Folio 0049).
  - **Plate XLII** (``0275.rst``): *Sperico Vacuo* (Hollow/Armillary Sphere with meridian and parallel ribs).
- **Euclid's Genetic Definition (Euclid XI.Def.14) vs. Theodosius's Locus Definition**: :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>` contrasts the static locus definition of the sphere (attributed in medieval Latin manuals to :person:`*Dionysius* <dionysius>` or *Theodosius*, *Sphaerica* I.Def.1) with :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s kinematic definition (:person:`Euclid <euclid>` XI.Def.14) by the revolution of a semicircle around its fixed diameter—the exact kinematic method :person:`Euclid <euclid>` uses throughout Books XI–XIII to circumscribe a sphere around each of the five Platonic solids.
