src.geometor.pacioli.ledger_conservation¶

Exact conservation algebra of Pacioli’s Venetian Quaderno & Problem of Points (1494).

Models Part I, Distinctio IX, Tractatus X–XI (fols. 197r–210v) of Fra Luca Pacioli’s Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita (Venice, 1494):

  1. Venetian Money of Account (fol. 201v): Exact rational arithmetic in the four ledger columns l[ire] s[oldi] g[rossi] p[icioli] (a oro, where \(1\text{ lira} = 10\text{ ducati} = 20\text{ soldi}\), \(1\text{ soldo} = 12\text{ grossi}\), \(1\text{ ducato} = 24\text{ grossi}\), \(1\text{ grosso} = 32\text{ piccoli}\)).

  2. Skew-Symmetric Conservation Algebra of Double-Entry Bookkeeping (Particularis de computis et scripturis, fols. 198v–210v): Models every Giornale entry Per <Debitore> // A <Creditore> as a directed edge in a conservation network with oriented incidence matrix \(B\) (\(\mathbf{1}^T B = \mathbf{0}^T\)) and skew-symmetric flow matrix \(F = -F^T\), proving the Summa summarum identity \(\sum \text{Dare} = \sum \text{Avere}\), Pacioli’s Cassa non-negativity rule (fol. 201r), and Bilancio del libro closure via Pro e Danno \(\to\) Capitale.

  3. The Problem of Points (*Giuoco della Balla & Balestrare*, fol. 197r–197v): Exact rational comparison between Pacioli’s 1494 past-score proportionality rule and the 1654 Pascal–Fermat combinatorial expectation for two or more players.

incidence_matrix() → sympy.Matrix¶

Return oriented vertex-edge incidence matrix \(B \in \{-1, 0, +1\}^{|V| \times |E|}\).

For entry \(e\) (Per u // A v), \(B_{u, e} = +1\) (Dare) and \(B_{v, e} = -1\) (Avere), so \(\mathbf{1}^T B = \mathbf{0}^T\).

weight_vector_ducati() → sympy.Matrix¶

skew_symmetric_flow_matrix() → sympy.Matrix¶

Return skew-symmetric pairwise flow matrix \(F \in \mathbb{Q}^{|V| \times |V|}\).

Entry \(F_{i,j}\) equals net ducats debited to account \(i\) against credit to account \(j\), satisfying \(F^T = -F\) identically.

close_to_bilancio(date: str = '1493-12-31', pro_e_danno_account: str = 'Pro e Danno', capitale_account: str = 'Cavedal', bilancio_account: str = 'Bilancio del libro') → sympy.Rational¶

Execute Pacioli’s Cap. 27 & Cap. 34 closing cycle (Pro e Danno -> Cavedal -> Bilancio).

First sweeps all "mercanzia" (closed venture) and "nominal" accounts into Pro e Danno, transfers net profit/loss from Pro e Danno to Cavedal (Cap. 27), and finally closes all remaining open asset/liability/capital accounts into Bilancio del libro (Cap. 34), bringing every account’s net balance to 0.

Parameters:

  • date – Closing date string.

  • pro_e_danno_account – Profit-and-loss summary account name.

  • capitale_account – Merchant’s capital account name.

  • bilancio_account – Final closing balance account name.

Returns:

Exact net profit (positive) or loss (negative) in ducats transferred to Cavedal.

src.geometor.pacioli.ledger_conservation.compare_problem_of_points(target_rounds: int = 6, current_scores: tuple[int, ...] = (5, 3), total_stake_ducati: int | sympy.Expr = 10) → ProblemOfPointsResult¶

Compare Pacioli’s 1494 balla / balestrare division against Pascal–Fermat (1654).

Covers both the classic two-player giuoco della balla on fol. 197r (e.g., (5, 3) or (5, 2) playing to 6 for 10 ducats) and Problem 51 on fol. 197v (three crossbowmen a balestrare playing to 6 best shots for 10 ducats, interrupted at (4, 3, 2)).

Parameters:

  • target_rounds – Winning score threshold (e.g., 6).

  • current_scores – Tuple of positive/non-negative scores below target_rounds.

  • total_stake_ducati – Total pot in ducats (default 10).

Returns:

Exact ProblemOfPointsResult with sp.Rational shares and discrepancies.
