geometor.pacioli.proportions¶

Exact symbolic proportion theory and Cossic algebra from Pacioli’s Summa (1494).

Formalizes three pillars of Part I of Fra Luca Pacioli’s Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita (Venice, 1494):

  1. Boethian Ratio Taxonomy & Six Euclidean Modes (Part I, Distinctio VI, fols. 68v–98r): Exact classification of rational ratios into the five Boethian genera of greater and lesser inequality (multiplex, superparticularis, superpartiens, multiplex superparticularis, multiplex superpartiens), compound ratios (proportioni composte), and the six Euclidean modi arguendi on fol. 74r (conversa, permutata, congionta, disgiunta, eversa, equa).

  2. Semicircle Construction of the Three Classical Means & Golden Section: Targeted geometor.model straightedge-and-compass constructions proving \(A \ge G \ge H\) and \(G^2 = A \cdot H\), harmonic cross-ratio \(-1\), and Euclidean division of \(10\) in extreme and mean ratio into \(\sqrt{125} - 5\) and \(15 - \sqrt{125}\) (Summa Dist. VI & Compendio Cap. VII–X).

  3. Six Canonical Cossic Quadratics (Part I, Distinctio VIII, fols. 145r–150r): Symbolic solver and gnomon completion-of-the-square parameters matching Pacioli’s Primi/Secundi/Tertii canonis versus and marginal woodcuts on fol. 146r.

geometor.pacioli.proportions.classify_boethian_ratio(p: int | sympy.Expr, q: int | sympy.Expr) → BoethianClassification¶

Classify a positive rational ratio \(p : q\) according to Summa Dist. VI.

Implements Severinus Boethius’s and Pacioli’s fivefold taxonomy of greater (maior inaequalitas) and lesser (minor inaequalitas, prefixed sub-) inequality on fols. 70r–83r.

Parameters:

  • p – Positive rational or integer antecedent.

  • q – Positive rational or integer consequent.

Returns:

Exact BoethianClassification record with reduced integers and species name.

Raises:

ValueError – If p or q is non-positive or non-rational.

geometor.pacioli.proportions.apply_six_euclidean_modes(a: int | sympy.Expr, b: int | sympy.Expr, c: int | sympy.Expr, d: int | sympy.Expr, e: int | sympy.Expr | None = None, f: int | sympy.Expr | None = None) → SixModesResult¶

Verify and transform \(a:b = c:d\) under Pacioli’s six modes (Summa fol. 74r).

On fol. 74r (De sex speciebus sive modis arguendi proportionalitatum), Pacioli enumerates: conversa (\(b:a = d:c\)), permutata (\(a:c = b:d\)), congionta (\((a+b):b = (c+d):d\)), disgiunta (\((a-b):b = (c-d):d\)), eversa (\(a:(a-b) = c:(c-d)\)), and equa (\(a:e = c:f\) when \(b:e = d:f\)).

Parameters:

  • a – First antecedent.

  • b – First consequent.

  • c – Second antecedent.

  • d – Second consequent.

  • e – Optional third term for proportione equa (Euclid V.22).

  • f – Optional sixth term for proportione equa (Euclid V.22).

Returns:

Exact SixModesResult verifying equality of both sides in every valid mode.

geometor.pacioli.proportions.construct_three_means(a: int | sympy.Expr = 9, b: int | sympy.Expr = 4) → ThreeMeansResult¶

Construct Arithmetic, Geometric, and Harmonic means of \(a\) and \(b\) in Model.

Places collinear points \(A=(0,0)\), \(P=(a,0)\), \(B=(a+b,0)\). In the semicircle on diameter \(AB\) with center \(M\):

  • Radius \(MQ = (a+b)/2 = A\) (Arithmetic mean),

  • Altitude \(PQ = \sqrt{ab} = G\) (Geometric mean, Euclid VI.13),

  • Leg \(QN = 2ab/(a+b) = H\) on radius \(MQ\) where \(PN \perp MQ\) (Harmonic mean), satisfying \(PQ^2 = MQ \cdot QN \iff G^2 = A \cdot H\).

Also constructs the collinear harmonic range \((A, C_H; P, D_H)\) with cross-ratio \(-1\).

Parameters:

  • a – First positive exact length (default 9, matching Summa / Compendio ratios).

  • b – Second positive exact length (default 4).

Returns:

Exact ThreeMeansResult with proven Model and exact symbolic means.

geometor.pacioli.proportions.construct_extreme_and_mean_ratio(length: int | sympy.Expr = 10) → ExtremeMeanResult¶

Divide a line of given length (default 10) in extreme and mean ratio.

Reproduces Pacioli’s canonical numerical archetype (Summa Dist. VI & Part II Dist. VIII; De Divina Proportione Cap. VIII–X): for \(AB = 10\), the major segment is \(AC = \sqrt{125} - 5 = 5(\sqrt{5} - 1)\) and the minor segment is \(CB = 15 - \sqrt{125} = 5(3 - \sqrt{5})\), satisfying \(AB \cdot CB = AC^2 = 150 - 10\sqrt{125}\) and Euclid XIII.1 \((AC + 5)^2 = 125 = 5 \cdot 5^2\).

Parameters:

length – Positive exact length of segment \(AB\) (default 10).

Returns:

Exact ExtremeMeanResult with proven Model and closed-form algebraic parts.

geometor.pacioli.proportions.solve_cossic_quadratic(canon_number: int, a_censi: int | sympy.Expr, b_cose: int | sympy.Expr, c_numero: int | sympy.Expr) → CossicQuadraticResult¶

Solve one of Pacioli’s six canonical Cossic equations (Summa fols. 145r–146r).

On fol. 145r, Pacioli classifies positive-coefficient equations into three simple (capitoli semplici, 1–3) and three compound (capitoli composti, 4–6) canons:

  1. a .ce. = b .co. (\(a x^2 = b x\))

  2. a .ce. = c (\(a x^2 = c\))

  3. b .co. = c (\(b x = c\))

  4. a .ce. p̄ b .co. = c (\(a x^2 + b x = c\) — Primi canonis versus: Si res et census numero coaequantur…)

  5. a .ce. p̄ c = b .co. (\(a x^2 + c = b x\) — Secundi canonis versus: two positive roots when \((b/(2a))^2 > c/a\))

  6. b .co. p̄ c = a .ce. (\(b x + c = a x^2\) — Tertii canonis versus)

Parameters:

  • canon_number – Integer 1 through 6 selecting Pacioli’s canon.

  • a_censi – Coefficient of censo (\(x^2\)), positive for canons 1, 2, 4, 5, 6.

  • b_cose – Coefficient of cosa (\(x\)), positive for canons 1, 3, 4, 5, 6.

  • c_numero – Constant numero, positive for canons 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

Returns:

Exact CossicQuadraticResult with Cossic statement, positive root(s), and completion-of-the-square gnomon invariants.
