geometor.pacioli.graphics.clean_image(pil_image: PIL.Image.Image, white_point: int = 226, preserve_color: bool = False) → PIL.Image.Image¶

Normalize aged parchment background and binding shadows to pure white (255).

Uses morphological closing + Gaussian blur to estimate local paper illumination per channel, eliminating yellowed paper cast, bleed-through, and gutter shadows while preserving crisp ink. When preserve_color=True (e.g., red rubrication on folios 0005 and 0311), chromatic ink is kept.

geometor.pacioli.graphics.detect_initials(image_path: pathlib.Path) → List[Dict[str, Any]]¶

Detect woodcut illuminated initial caps by finding 2D ink-density centers along the text column edge.

Woodcut initials in the 1509 Paganini edition have consistent square dimensions: - Standard chapter initials: 120x132 px (padded around the ~112x118 woodcut frame) - Large treatise-opening initials (folios 0017 and 0085): 260x304 px - Title-page red initial D (folio 0005): 184x188 px

geometor.pacioli.graphics.detect_margin_figures(image_path: pathlib.Path, is_recto: bool) → List[Dict[str, Any]]¶

Detect geometric diagrams and marginalia strictly in the outer margin of a text folio.

  • Odd (recto) folios: right outer margin (to the right of the main text column).

  • Even (verso) folios: left outer margin (to the left of the main text column).

Never falls back to full-page text blocks.
