geometor.pacioli.tractatus_geometriae¶

Exact symbolic constructions from Part II (Tractatus Geometriae) of Summa (1494).

Models three landmark geometric treatises from Part II of Fra Luca Pacioli’s Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita (Venice, Nov. 10, 1494):

  1. Heron’s Formula & the ``(13, 14, 15)`` Triangle (Part II, Distinctio I, Cap. 8, fol. 9v): Pure straightedge-and-compass geometor.model.Model construction of \(\triangle G B A\) with sides \(GA=13, GB=14, AB=15\), cathetus \(AD=12\), base segments \(GD=5, DB=9\), Heron product \(6 \cdot 7 \cdot 8 \cdot 21 = 7056\), area \(\sqrt{7056} = 84\), rational incenter \(I=(6,4)\) (\(r=4\)), and rational circumcenter \(O=(7, 33/8)\) (\(R=65/8\)).

  2. Proportional Division of Triangles (Part II, Distinctio IV: De divisione superficierum, after Leonardo Pisano’s Practica Geometriae): Exact Euclidean division of \(\triangle G B A\) in ratio \(p : q\) both through a vertex cevian (VI.1) and by a line parallel to the base constructed via the semicircle geometric mean (VI.13, VI.19).

  3. Regular & Archimedean Polyhedra Inscribed in the Sphere (Part II, Distinctio VIII: De corporibus regularibus, fols. 68v–70r): Exact closed-form edge lengths, squared edges (possanze), surface areas, and volumes over \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, \phi)\) for a sphere of diameter \(D = 6\) (including the truncated tetrahedron of edge \(\sqrt{72/11} = \sqrt{6\frac{6}{11}}\) and cuboctahedron of edge \(3\) on fol. 69v, and the dodecahedron of edge \(\sqrt{20}-2\) in the sphere of axis \(\sqrt{48}\) on fol. 70r).

geometor.pacioli.tractatus_geometriae.construct_heron_13_14_15() → HeronTriangleResult¶

Construct and verify Pacioli’s (13, 14, 15) Heron triangle (Part II, fol. 9v).

On Part II, fol. 9v (Distinctio prima, Capitulum octavum), Pacioli computes for \(\triangle ABG\) with \(AG=13\), \(BG=14\), \(AB=15\):

  • Perimeter \(13 + 14 + 15 = 42\), semiperimeter \(s = 21\),

  • Excesses \((s - 15, s - 14, s - 13) = (6, 7, 8)\),

  • Product \(6 \cdot 7 \cdot 8 = 336\), and \(336 \cdot 21 = 7056\),

  • Area \(\sqrt{7056} = 84\) braccia quadre, cathetus \(AD = 12\), segments \(5, 9\).

Returns:

Exact HeronTriangleResult containing the proven Model and invariants.

geometor.pacioli.tractatus_geometriae.construct_triangle_division_cevian(ratio_p: int = 1, ratio_q: int = 2) → TriangleDivisionResult¶

Divide \(\triangle GBA\) (13, 14, 15) in ratio p : q by a vertex cevian.

Implements the first problem of Part II, Distinctio IV: De divisione superficierum (following Euclid VI.1 and Leonardo Pisano’s Practica Geometriae): dividing base \(GB\) at \(K\) in ratio \(p : q\) via Thales’s intercept construction (VI.10) so \(\text{Area}(\triangle GAK) : \text{Area}(\triangle KAB) = p : q\).

Parameters:

  • ratio_p – Positive integer share adjacent to G (default 1).

  • ratio_q – Positive integer share adjacent to B (default 2).

Returns:

Exact TriangleDivisionResult with verified polygonal areas.

geometor.pacioli.tractatus_geometriae.construct_triangle_division_parallel(ratio_p: int = 1, ratio_q: int = 1) → TriangleDivisionResult¶

Divide \(\triangle GBA\) in ratio p : q by a cut parallel to side BA.

Implements Pacioli’s De divisione superficierum (Part II, Distinctio IV, Euclid VI.13 + VI.19): to cut off \(\triangle GUV \sim \triangle GBA\) with \(\text{Area}(\triangle GUV) / \text{Area}(\triangle GBA) = p / (p + q)\), first divide \(GB\) at \(K\) with \(GK / GB = p / (p+q)\), then construct the geometric mean \(GU = \sqrt{GB \cdot GK}\) via a semicircle on \(GB\) and draw \(UV \parallel BA\).

Parameters:

  • ratio_p – Positive integer share for sub-triangle GUV (default 1, bisecting area).

  • ratio_q – Positive integer share for trapezoid UBAV (default 1).

Returns:

Exact TriangleDivisionResult with verified radical cut coordinates and rational areas.

geometor.pacioli.tractatus_geometriae.inscribed_polyhedra_sphere_table(diameter: int | sympy.Expr = 6) → dict[str, InscribedPolyhedronSpec]¶

Compute exact symbolic metrics for regular & Archimedean solids in a sphere of diameter D.

Defaults to diameter = 6 (Summa Part II, Distinctio VIII: De corporibus regularibus, fol. 69v–70r), reproducing both Euclid Book XIII’s five regular solids and Pacioli’s dependent Archimedean solids inherited from Piero della Francesca:

  • truncated_tetrahedron (fol. 69v, prob. 15–16): \(e^2 = \frac{2}{11} D^2\) (\(= \frac{72}{11} = 6\frac{6}{11}\) for \(D=6\), matching Pacioli’s ℞.6 6/11!).

  • cuboctahedron (fol. 69v–70r, prob. 17–19): \(e = \frac{1}{2} D\) (\(= 3\) braccia for \(D=6\)).

Parameters:

diameter – Exact positive sphere diameter D (default 6).

Returns:

Dictionary mapping solid keys to InscribedPolyhedronSpec.

geometor.pacioli.tractatus_geometriae.dodecahedron_in_sphere_sqrt48() → DodecahedronAxis48Result¶

Reproduce Problem 21 on Part II, fol. 70r (dodecahedron in sphere of axis sqrt(48)).

On fol. 70r, Pacioli takes a sphere whose axis has squared length (possanza) 48: by Euclid XIII.15, the inscribed cube has squared edge 48 / 3 = 16, so edge 4; dividing 4 in extreme and mean ratio (Campanus XIV.18 / Euclid XIII.17 corollary) yields major part sqrt(20) - 2 (the dodecahedron edge) and minor part 6 - sqrt(20).

Returns:

Exact DodecahedronAxis48Result verified against inscribed_polyhedra_sphere_table().
