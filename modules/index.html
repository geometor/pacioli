geometor.pacioli¶

Symbolic modeling, digital codex tools, and conservation algebra for Fra Luca Pacioli.

Provides tools and exact SymPy / geometor.model modules spanning both of Fra Luca Pacioli’s Venetian folio masterpieces printed by Paganino de’ Paganini:

  • Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita (Venice, 1494):

    • geometor.pacioli.proportions: Boethian ratio taxonomy, Euclidean modi arguendi, semicircle construction of the three classical means, golden section of 10, and Pacioli’s six canonical Cossic quadratic equations.

    • geometor.pacioli.tractatus_geometriae: Heron’s (13, 14, 15) triangle construction, De divisione superficierum proportional cuts, and inscribed Platonic and Archimedean solids in the sphere of diameter 6.

    • geometor.pacioli.ledger_conservation: Venetian l. s. g. p. money of account, skew-symmetric conservation algebra of the Quaderno, and the Problem of Points.

    • geometor.pacioli.svg_woodcut: Deterministic .euclid-graphic SVG generator.

  • De Divina Proportione (Venice, 1509 / Vienna, 1889):

    • PacioliParser, folio extraction, and woodcut graphic calibration utilities.

Verify and transform \(a:b = c:d\) under Pacioli’s six modes (Summa fol. 74r).

On fol. 74r (De sex speciebus sive modis arguendi proportionalitatum), Pacioli enumerates: conversa (\(b:a = d:c\)), permutata (\(a:c = b:d\)), congionta (\((a+b):b = (c+d):d\)), disgiunta (\((a-b):b = (c-d):d\)), eversa (\(a:(a-b) = c:(c-d)\)), and equa (\(a:e = c:f\) when \(b:e = d:f\)).

Parameters:

  • a – First antecedent.

  • b – First consequent.

  • c – Second antecedent.

  • d – Second consequent.

  • e – Optional third term for proportione equa (Euclid V.22).

  • f – Optional sixth term for proportione equa (Euclid V.22).

Returns:

Exact SixModesResult verifying equality of both sides in every valid mode.

geometor.pacioli.construct_extreme_and_mean_ratio(length: int | sympy.Expr = 10) → ExtremeMeanResult¶

Divide a line of given length (default 10) in extreme and mean ratio.

Reproduces Pacioli’s canonical numerical archetype (Summa Dist. VI & Part II Dist. VIII; De Divina Proportione Cap. VIII–X): for \(AB = 10\), the major segment is \(AC = \sqrt{125} - 5 = 5(\sqrt{5} - 1)\) and the minor segment is \(CB = 15 - \sqrt{125} = 5(3 - \sqrt{5})\), satisfying \(AB \cdot CB = AC^2 = 150 - 10\sqrt{125}\) and Euclid XIII.1 \((AC + 5)^2 = 125 = 5 \cdot 5^2\).

Parameters:

length – Positive exact length of segment \(AB\) (default 10).

Returns:

Exact ExtremeMeanResult with proven Model and closed-form algebraic parts.

geometor.pacioli.construct_three_means(a: int | sympy.Expr = 9, b: int | sympy.Expr = 4) → ThreeMeansResult¶

Construct Arithmetic, Geometric, and Harmonic means of \(a\) and \(b\) in Model.

Places collinear points \(A=(0,0)\), \(P=(a,0)\), \(B=(a+b,0)\). In the semicircle on diameter \(AB\) with center \(M\):

  • Radius \(MQ = (a+b)/2 = A\) (Arithmetic mean),

  • Altitude \(PQ = \sqrt{ab} = G\) (Geometric mean, Euclid VI.13),

  • Leg \(QN = 2ab/(a+b) = H\) on radius \(MQ\) where \(PN \perp MQ\) (Harmonic mean), satisfying \(PQ^2 = MQ \cdot QN \iff G^2 = A \cdot H\).

Also constructs the collinear harmonic range \((A, C_H; P, D_H)\) with cross-ratio \(-1\).

Parameters:

  • a – First positive exact length (default 9, matching Summa / Compendio ratios).

  • b – Second positive exact length (default 4).

Returns:

Exact ThreeMeansResult with proven Model and exact symbolic means.

geometor.pacioli.solve_cossic_quadratic(canon_number: int, a_censi: int | sympy.Expr, b_cose: int | sympy.Expr, c_numero: int | sympy.Expr) → CossicQuadraticResult¶

Solve one of Pacioli’s six canonical Cossic equations (Summa fols. 145r–146r).

On fol. 145r, Pacioli classifies positive-coefficient equations into three simple (capitoli semplici, 1–3) and three compound (capitoli composti, 4–6) canons:

  1. a .ce. = b .co. (\(a x^2 = b x\))

  2. a .ce. = c (\(a x^2 = c\))

  3. b .co. = c (\(b x = c\))

  4. a .ce. p̄ b .co. = c (\(a x^2 + b x = c\) — Primi canonis versus: Si res et census numero coaequantur…)

  5. a .ce. p̄ c = b .co. (\(a x^2 + c = b x\) — Secundi canonis versus: two positive roots when \((b/(2a))^2 > c/a\))

  6. b .co. p̄ c = a .ce. (\(b x + c = a x^2\) — Tertii canonis versus)

Parameters:

  • canon_number – Integer 1 through 6 selecting Pacioli’s canon.

  • a_censi – Coefficient of censo (\(x^2\)), positive for canons 1, 2, 4, 5, 6.

  • b_cose – Coefficient of cosa (\(x\)), positive for canons 1, 3, 4, 5, 6.

  • c_numero – Constant numero, positive for canons 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

Returns:

Exact CossicQuadraticResult with Cossic statement, positive root(s), and completion-of-the-square gnomon invariants.

geometor.pacioli.construct_heron_13_14_15() → HeronTriangleResult¶

Construct and verify Pacioli’s (13, 14, 15) Heron triangle (Part II, fol. 9v).

On Part II, fol. 9v (Distinctio prima, Capitulum octavum), Pacioli computes for \(\triangle ABG\) with \(AG=13\), \(BG=14\), \(AB=15\):

  • Perimeter \(13 + 14 + 15 = 42\), semiperimeter \(s = 21\),

  • Excesses \((s - 15, s - 14, s - 13) = (6, 7, 8)\),

  • Product \(6 \cdot 7 \cdot 8 = 336\), and \(336 \cdot 21 = 7056\),

  • Area \(\sqrt{7056} = 84\) braccia quadre, cathetus \(AD = 12\), segments \(5, 9\).

Returns:

Exact HeronTriangleResult containing the proven Model and invariants.

geometor.pacioli.construct_triangle_division_parallel(ratio_p: int = 1, ratio_q: int = 1) → TriangleDivisionResult¶

Divide \(\triangle GBA\) in ratio p : q by a cut parallel to side BA.

Implements Pacioli’s De divisione superficierum (Part II, Distinctio IV, Euclid VI.13 + VI.19): to cut off \(\triangle GUV \sim \triangle GBA\) with \(\text{Area}(\triangle GUV) / \text{Area}(\triangle GBA) = p / (p + q)\), first divide \(GB\) at \(K\) with \(GK / GB = p / (p+q)\), then construct the geometric mean \(GU = \sqrt{GB \cdot GK}\) via a semicircle on \(GB\) and draw \(UV \parallel BA\).

Parameters:

  • ratio_q – Positive integer share for trapezoid UBAV (default 1).

Returns:

Exact TriangleDivisionResult with verified radical cut coordinates and rational areas.

geometor.pacioli.dodecahedron_in_sphere_sqrt48() → DodecahedronAxis48Result¶

Reproduce Problem 21 on Part II, fol. 70r (dodecahedron in sphere of axis sqrt(48)).

On fol. 70r, Pacioli takes a sphere whose axis has squared length (possanza) 48: by Euclid XIII.15, the inscribed cube has squared edge 48 / 3 = 16, so edge 4; dividing 4 in extreme and mean ratio (Campanus XIV.18 / Euclid XIII.17 corollary) yields major part sqrt(20) - 2 (the dodecahedron edge) and minor part 6 - sqrt(20).

Returns:

Exact DodecahedronAxis48Result verified against inscribed_polyhedra_sphere_table().

geometor.pacioli.inscribed_polyhedra_sphere_table(diameter: int | sympy.Expr = 6) → dict[str, InscribedPolyhedronSpec]¶

Compute exact symbolic metrics for regular & Archimedean solids in a sphere of diameter D.

Defaults to diameter = 6 (Summa Part II, Distinctio VIII: De corporibus regularibus, fol. 69v–70r), reproducing both Euclid Book XIII’s five regular solids and Pacioli’s dependent Archimedean solids inherited from Piero della Francesca:

  • truncated_tetrahedron (fol. 69v, prob. 15–16): \(e^2 = \frac{2}{11} D^2\) (\(= \frac{72}{11} = 6\frac{6}{11}\) for \(D=6\), matching Pacioli’s ℞.6 6/11!).

  • cuboctahedron (fol. 69v–70r, prob. 17–19): \(e = \frac{1}{2} D\) (\(= 3\) braccia for \(D=6\)).

Parameters:

diameter – Exact positive sphere diameter D (default 6).

Returns:

Dictionary mapping solid keys to InscribedPolyhedronSpec.
