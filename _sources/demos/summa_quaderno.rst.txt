De Computis et Scripturis & Problem of Points (1494)
====================================================

fols. 198v–210v) in exact ``l. s. g. p.`` money of account, verifies the oriented incidence
conservation law :math:`\mathbf{1}^T B = \mathbf{0}^T` and skew-symmetric flow :math:`F^T = -F`,
executes the *Pro e Danno* :math:`\to` *Cavedal* :math:`\to` *Bilancio del libro* closing cycle,
and compares Pacioli's 1494 *balla* and three-player *balestrare* stake divisions (fol. 197r–197v)
against Pascal and Fermat's 1654 combinatorial expectation.
