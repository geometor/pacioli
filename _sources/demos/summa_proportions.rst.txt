Proportioni & Regola dela Cosa (1494)
=====================================

Verifies Severinus Boethius's and Pacioli's five genera of rational inequality (*Summa* Part I,
*Distinctio VI*, fols. 70r–83r), the six Euclidean *modi arguendi* (fol. 74r), the exact
``geometor.model`` semicircle construction of the arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic means,
the Euclidean golden cut of ``10`` into :math:`\sqrt{125}-5` and :math:`15-\sqrt{125}`, and
Pacioli's six canonical Cossic quadratic equations (*Distinctio VIII*, fols. 145r–146r).
