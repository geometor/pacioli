=====
about
=====

``geometor.pacioli`` is part of the **GEOMETOR** initiative by **phi ARCHITECT**, dedicated to the digital reconstruction and geometric analysis of :person:`Fra Luca Pacioli's <pacioli>` treatises.

Our edition of :cite:`*De Divina Proportione* <pacioli:divina-proportione>` synthesizes the 1509 Venice *editio princeps* printed by Paganino Paganini (featuring :person:`Leonardo da Vinci's <leonardo>` sixty polyhedra woodcuts, the twenty-three Roman capital letter plates, and marginal Euclidean diagrams) with :person:`Constantin Winterberg <winterberg>`'s 1889 Vienna critical edition and commentary.
