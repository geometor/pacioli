Piero della Francesca
=====================

*Early Renaissance Master Painter, Geometer & Perspective Theorist of Borgo Sansepolcro*

Celebrated as the "monarch of painting and architecture in our times" (*monarca a li tempi nostri de la pictura e architectura*) by Pacioli, Piero was Pacioli's elder compatriot and foundational mathematical master in Borgo Sansepolcro. Beyond his celebrated frescoes—including *The Legend of the True Cross* in Arezzo and the *Resurrection* in Sansepolcro—Piero was an accomplished geometer who authored three rigorous mathematical treatises: *Trattato d'Abaco*, *De Prospectiva Pingendi* (the first comprehensive manual on linear perspective), and *Libellus de Quinque Corporibus Regularibus*.

Pacioli translated and incorporated large sections of Piero's Latin *Libellus* on regular polyhedra into Part III of *De Divina Proportione*, preserving Piero's stereometric theorems and techniques for calculating the edge-lengths and volumes of inscribed polyhedra.
