Constantin Winterberg
=====================

*German Classical Art Historian, Mathematician & Critical Translator*

Scholar of Renaissance perspective and geometry who produced the landmark 1889 Vienna critical edition and German translation of Pacioli's work: *Fra Luca Pacioli: Divina Proportione — Die Lehre vom Goldenen Schnitt* (published as Volume II of Rudolf Eitelberger von Edelberg's prestigious *Quellenschriften für Kunstgeschichte und Kunsttechnik des Mittelalters und der Neuzeit*, Vienna: Carl Graeser, 1889).

Winterberg provided an extensive scholarly introduction (*Einleitung*), detailed chapter-by-chapter philological annotations, collation with the 1498 Milan manuscript (Codex Ambrosianus), and seventeen rigorous substantive mathematical commentaries (*Sachliche Anmerkungen*) analyzing Leonardo's polyhedra constructions and Pacioli's proportion theorems. His 1889 text and notes provide the primary German critical track throughout the GEOMETOR edition.

* **Primary Contribution**: Critical German translation, introduction, and mathematical *Sachliche Anmerkungen* in :doc:`De Divina Proportione <../de_divina_proportione/index>`
