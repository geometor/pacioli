Leonardo da Vinci
=================

*Florentine Painter, Sculptor, Polymath, Architect & Engineer*

One of the greatest creative minds in human history. During his tenure as court artist and engineer to Ludovico Sforza in Milan (1482–1499), Leonardo forged a deep friendship and mathematical partnership with Fra Luca Pacioli, who arrived in Milan in 1496. Leonardo enthusiastically studied Euclid's *Elements* and Pacioli's *Summa*, noting in his journals to purchase a copy of the *Summa* and consult Master Luca on geometric propositions.

For Pacioli's *De Divina Proportione*, Leonardo drew the sixty exquisite perspective polyhedra plates (*schemata Vincii nostri Leonardi manibus scalpta*), depicting both solid (*vacui*) and hollowed-out skeletal (*vacui / pertusati*) regular and semi-regular Archimedean bodies suspended in dramatic perspective. These illustrations—cut in wood for the 1509 Venice edition—represent the earliest published depictions of Archimedean and elevated polyhedra in print and constitute Leonardo's only published book illustrations during his lifetime.
