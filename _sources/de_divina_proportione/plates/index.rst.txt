Appendices: Woodcut Plates & Mathematical Commentary
====================================================

.. rubric:: Roman Geometric Alphabet, Architectural Plates, Leonardo da Vinci's Sixty Polyhedra & Winterberg's Sachliche Anmerkungen

----

Outline of Appendices
---------------------

.. container:: toc-outline

   - **Appendix I —** :ref:`The Roman Geometric Alphabet <plates-alphabet-en>` *(23 Woodcut Plates · Folios 0139–0183)*

     - Compass-and-straightedge construction of the twenty-three Latin capitals in the unit square of nine and ten parts ([:ref:`English <plates-alphabet-en>` · :ref:`Italiano <plates-alphabet-it>` · :ref:`Deutsch <plates-alphabet-de>`])

   - **Appendix II —** :ref:`Architectural Plates & Arbor Proportionis <plates-architectural-en>` *(5 Woodcut Plates · Folios 0137, 0185, 0213, 0215, 0311)*

     - Human profile triangle, Corinthian & Ionic columns, *Porta Speciosa*, and the *Arbor Proportionis et Proportionalitatis* ([:ref:`English <plates-architectural-en>` · :ref:`Italiano <plates-architectural-it>` · :ref:`Deutsch <plates-architectural-de>`])

   - **Appendix III —** :ref:`Polyhedra Plates by Leonardo da Vinci <plates-polyhedra-en>` *(60 Perspective Plates, I–LIX · Folios 0187–0309)*

     - Paired *planus* (*solido*) and *vacuus* (*skeletal*) woodcuts across the Platonic, truncated, elevated, prisms, and pyramids series ([:ref:`English <plates-polyhedra-en>` · :ref:`Latin / Greek <plates-polyhedra-la>`])

   - **Appendix IV —** :ref:`Winterberg's Substantive Mathematical Notes <plates-anmerkungen-en>` *(Sachliche Anmerkungen 1–17 · 1889, pp. 338–352)*

     - Modern algebraic derivations of the golden section, circumsphere radii, mutual inscriptions, and stereometric mensuration ([:ref:`English <plates-anmerkungen-en>` · :ref:`Deutsch <plates-anmerkungen-de>`])
