CHAPTER XIX — Critical Commentary & Notes
=========================================

.. rubric:: How the Architect Must Govern Himself in Cramped and Irregular Sites, Why Continuous Geometric Proportion Is Wider Than Discrete Numerical Proportion, and Praise of Master Piero della Francesca.

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- *:person:`*Piero della Francesca's * <piero-della-francesca>`Legend of the True Cross* at Arezzo and *:cite:`De Prospectiva Pingendi <piero:prospectiva>`* at Urbino**: This passage on Folio 0081 (``f. 33r``) is one of the earliest and most authoritative Renaissance tributes to *:person:`*Piero della Francesca* <piero-della-francesca>`* (c. 1415–1492), listing every major city where Piero worked (**Urbino, Bologna, Ferrara, Rimini, Ancona, Sansepolcro, and Arezzo**), noting his mastery of both **oil and tempera/gouache on wall and panel** (*in muro e taula a oglio e guazzo*), singling out the **Cappella Maggiore of San Francesco in Arezzo** (*la magna capella dela tribuna del'altar grande*) as *"vna dele dignissime opere de Italia e da tutti commendata"*, and citing Piero's manuscript treatise *:cite:`De Prospectiva Pingendi <piero:prospectiva>`* in the Ducal Library of Urbino!
