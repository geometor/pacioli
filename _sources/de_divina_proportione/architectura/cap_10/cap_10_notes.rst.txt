CHAPTER X — Critical Commentary & Notes
=======================================

.. rubric:: On Round and Lateral Pyramids in Architecture, and Praise of Leonardo da Vinci's Incomparable Drawings.

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Weight (*Al Peso*) and Volume (*Ala Mesura*)**: Because the stonemasons of Sansepolcro needed to calculate the **dead weight** (*peso*) of stone spires, obelisks, and pyramidal roofs resting upon columns and walls, Pacioli points out that for homogeneous stone, Euclid's :math:`\frac{1}{3}` volume ratio (:cite:`Euclid XII.7 <euclid:elements:XII.7>`, :cite:`Euclid XII.10 <euclid:elements:XII.10>`) is simultaneously a **weight ratio**: a stone pyramid or cone weighs exactly one-third as much as a solid stone prism or cylinder of the same base and height!
