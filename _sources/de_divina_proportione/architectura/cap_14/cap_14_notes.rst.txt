CHAPTER XIV — Critical Commentary & Notes
=========================================

.. rubric:: On the Epistyle—Called the Architrave by the Moderns—and Why Excessive Intercolumniations Crack Stone Architraves (Vitruvius Book III).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Plate Referenced**: Folio 0215 (``0215.rst``, ``0215.g1.png``) displays the complete detailed profile and elevation of the **Epistyle, Zophorus (Frieze), and Cornice** with every single letter (:math:`a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, V, k, l, m, o`) explained in ``Cap. XIV``–``Cap. XVI``.
- **The 3 : 4 : 5 Arithmetic Progression of the Three Fasciae**: Following :cite:`Vitruvius III.5 <vitruvius:de-arch:III.5>`.10, after setting aside :math:`\frac{1}{7}` of the :term:`architrave <epistilio>` height for the top *cymatium/taenia* (:math:`h`), the remaining :math:`\frac{6}{7}` is divided into 12 parts distributed as **3, 4, and 5** to the lower, middle, and upper fasciae (:math:`a, c, e`), so that the fasciae step outward and grow taller as they ascend toward the eye!
