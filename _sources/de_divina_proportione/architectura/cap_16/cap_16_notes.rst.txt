CHAPTER XVI — Critical Commentary & Notes
=========================================

.. rubric:: On the Composition and Mouldings of the Cornice (*Cornicione*).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Vitruvius's Rule of Equal Height and Projection (*Ecphora*, Vitruvius III.5.11)**: Pacioli repeatedly emphasizes Vitruvius's fundamental rule of classical profile geometry: *uti proiecturae earundem sint quantae altitudines* ("that the projections [*sportafore*] of the mouldings be equal to their heights"), which places the outer profile of the cornice along a :math:`45^{\circ}` diagonal line!

Constantin Winterberg's Commentary (1889 Edition)
-------------------------------------------------

- :person:`Winterberg <winterberg>` Footnotes 1–2 (Arch. Cap. XVI, p. 328): (1) The repeated words *'e dali antichi'* in the 1509 print are a dittography to be deleted for sense. (2) Bead-and-reel (*Paternostri*) or olive-leaf moulding.
  (:person:`Winterberg <winterberg>` Fussnoten 1–2 (Arch. Cap. XVI, S. 328): *1) Die Worte des Textes: „e dali antichi“ des Sinnes wegen zu streichen. 2) Paternostri oder Olivenschmuck.*)
