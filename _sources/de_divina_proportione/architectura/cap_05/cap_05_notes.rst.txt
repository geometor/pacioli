CHAPTER V — Critical Commentary & Notes
=======================================

.. rubric:: On the Length (Height), Thickness, Entasis, and Base (*Spira*) of the Round Column.

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Vitruvius's Attic Base (Vitruvius III.5.2)**: :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` step-by-step arithmetic for the Attic column base (:ref:`Plate 0213 <plates-architectural-en>`) follows :cite:`Vitruvius III.5 <vitruvius:de-arch:III.5>`.2–3: total base height :math:`= \frac{1}{2}D`; plinth width :math:`= 1\frac{1}{2}D`; plinth height :math:`= \frac{1}{3}` of base height (:math:`= \frac{1}{6}D`), leaving :math:`\frac{2}{3}` of base height (:math:`= \frac{1}{3}D`), of which :math:`\frac{1}{4}` is the upper torus and the remaining :math:`\frac{3}{4}` is split equally (:math:`\frac{3}{8}` each) between the lower torus and the scotia (*trochilus*) with its fillets.
