CHAPTER XLI — Critical Commentary & Notes
=========================================

.. rubric:: How the Exacedron or Cube Is Placed in the Ycocedron.

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Elegance of Campanus's Mediated Inscription**: Rather than attempting a direct coordinate construction in the :term:`icosahedron <icosahedron>`, :person:`Campanus <campanus>` (and Pacioli) exploits the dual :term:`dodecahedron <dodecahedron>` (XV.6) together with :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s roof construction (:cite:`Euclid XIII.17 <euclid:elements:XIII.17>`): since every regular :term:`dodecahedron <dodecahedron>` contains 5 inscribed cubes (whose 12 edges are face diagonals of the 12 pentagons), inscribing the dual :term:`dodecahedron <dodecahedron>` inside the :term:`icosahedron <icosahedron>` immediately places 8 of the :term:`icosahedron <icosahedron>`'s 20 face-centers at the corners of a regular cube!

Constantin Winterberg's Commentary (1889 Edition)
-------------------------------------------------

- :person:`Winterberg <winterberg>` Footnote 1 (Cap. XLI, p. 231): Under the designation *pyramide* in Chapters XLI–XLVI, Pacioli always understands the regular triangular pyramid (the regular :term:`tetrahedron <tetrahedron>`).
  (:person:`Winterberg <winterberg>` Fussnote 1 (Cap. XLI, S. 231): *Unter dieser Bezeichnung ist im Folgenden stets das dreiseitige reguläre Tetraeder verstanden.*)
