CHAPTER XXVI — Critical Commentary & Notes
==========================================

.. rubric:: How the First Regular Body, Called the Tetracedron or Pyramid of Four Triangular Bases, Is Formed and Placed in the Sphere.

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagram**: Folio 0032 (``f. 8v``) includes diagram ``0032.g1.png``, showing the semicircle :math:`adb` cut at :math:`c` (:math:`ac = 2\,cb`) alongside the base circle and triangle of the :term:`tetrahedron <tetrahedron>`.
- **Mathematical Identity**: For a sphere of diameter :math:`D`, the edge :math:`a_{4}` of the inscribed regular :term:`tetrahedron <tetrahedron>` satisfies :math:`D^{2} : a_{4}^{2} = 3 : 2`, or :math:`a_{4} = D\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}`.
- **Comparison with Earlier Draft Track**: The earlier draft omitted Steps 5–7 (how the perpendicular :math:`hk = ac` from the center of the circumscribed circle of radius :math:`cd` proves :math:`ke = da` via :cite:`Euclid XIII.12 <euclid:elements:XIII.12>` and :cite:`Euclid XI.4 <euclid:elements:XI.4>`).
