CHAPTER XIII — Critical Commentary & Notes
==========================================

.. rubric:: On Its Fourth Ineffable Effect

Historical and Philological Commentary
--------------------------------------

1. **Campanus XIII.4 vs. Heiberg Euclid XIII.5 (Ordering Inversion in Book XIII)**: In :person:`Campanus of Novara <campanus>`'s Latin edition of *Elements* Book XIII, Heiberg's Propositions 4 and 5 are transposed! Specifically:

   - :cite:`**Campanus XIII.4** <campanus:euclid:XIII.4>` (= Heiberg :cite:`Euclid XIII.5 <euclid:elements:XIII.5>`): If a straight line :math:`AB` is cut in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <extrema-et-media-ratio>` at :math:`C` (with greater segment :math:`AC`), and :math:`AD = AC` is added to :math:`AB`, then the whole :math:`BD = AB + AC` is cut in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <extrema-et-media-ratio>` at :math:`A`, with greater segment :math:`AB`.
   - :cite:`**Campanus XIII.5** <campanus:euclid:XIII.5>` (= Heiberg :cite:`Euclid XIII.4 <euclid:elements:XIII.4>`): If a straight line :math:`AB` is cut in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <extrema-et-media-ratio>`, the sum of the squares on the whole :math:`AB` and the lesser segment :math:`BC` is triple the square on the greater segment :math:`AC`.

   :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>` follows :person:`Campanus <campanus>`'s numbering strictly in Chapters XIII and XIV: Chapter XIII gives Heiberg :cite:`Euclid XIII.5 <euclid:elements:XIII.5>` (citing :cite:`*la quarta del terzodecimo* <euclid:elements:XIII.4>`), and Chapter XIV gives Heiberg :cite:`Euclid XIII.4 <euclid:elements:XIII.4>` (citing *la quinta del 13.*).

2. **Difference of Squares Identity**: Notice how cleanly the product of the conjugate binomial and :term:`apotome <residuo>` evaluates: :math:`(\sqrt{125} - 5)(\sqrt{125} + 5) = 125 - 25 = 100 = 10^2`.

Constantin Winterberg's Commentary (1889 Edition)
-------------------------------------------------

.. rubric:: Note 4 (Cap. XIII): Algebraic Proof of the Fourth Effect (Euclid XIII.5) (:ref:`Sachliche Anmerkungen #4 <plates-anmerkungen-en>`, Cap. XIII, p. 203, l. 7 from bottom)

In algebraic form, let the segment :math:`bc` be divided in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <extrema-et-media-ratio>` at :math:`d` (:math:`cd > bd`), and let :math:`ac = cd` be adjoined to form the whole line :math:`ad = ac + bc`:

- **Hypothesis**: :math:`cd^2 = bc \cdot bd` and :math:`ac = cd`.
- **Claim**: :math:`bc^2 = ad \cdot cd = ad \cdot ac`.
- **Proof**: By hypothesis, :math:`cd^2 = bc(bc - cd) = bc^2 - bc \cdot cd`, whence

  .. math::

     bc^2 = cd^2 + bc \cdot cd = cd(cd + bc) = cd(ac + bc) = ad \cdot cd. \quad \text{Q.E.D.}

.. rubric:: Anmerkung 4 (Cap. XIII, S. 203): Mathematischer Nachweis des vierten Effects (Euklid XIII.5)

In mathematischer Form (wenn :math:`bc` in :math:`d` stetig getheilt und :math:`ac = cd` angesetzt wird):

- **Voraussetzung**: :math:`cd^2 = bc \cdot bd` und :math:`ac = cd`.
- **Behauptung**: :math:`bc^2 = ad \cdot cd`.
- **Beweis**: Nach Voraussetzung ist :math:`cd^2 = bc(bc - cd)`, folglich:

  .. math::

     ad \cdot cd = (ac + bc)\,cd = cd^2 + bc \cdot cd = bc^2. \quad \text{w. z. b. w.}
