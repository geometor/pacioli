CHAPTER LVI — Critical Commentary & Notes
=========================================

.. rubric:: On the Spherical Body and How It Is Formed (Plates XLI–XLII).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Plates Referenced**:

  - **Plate XLI** (``0273.rst``): *Sperico Solido* (Solid Sphere, labeled ``XL`` in the marginal roman numeral on Folio 0049).
  - **Plate XLII** (``0275.rst``): *Sperico Vacuo* (Hollow/Armillary Sphere with meridian and parallel ribs).

- **Euclid's Genetic Definition (Euclid XI.Def.14) vs. Theodosius's Locus Definition**: :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>` contrasts the static locus definition of the sphere (attributed in medieval Latin manuals to :person:`*Dionysius* <dionysius>` or *Theodosius*, *Sphaerica* I.Def.1) with :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s kinematic definition (:person:`Euclid <euclid>` XI.Def.14) by the revolution of a semicircle around its fixed diameter—the exact kinematic method :person:`Euclid <euclid>` uses throughout Books XI–XIII to circumscribe a sphere around each of the five Platonic solids.
