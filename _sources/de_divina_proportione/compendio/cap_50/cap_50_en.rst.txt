CHAPTER L
=========

.. rubric:: On the Disposition of the Octocedron: Regular (Plates XIII–XIV), Truncated (Plates XV–XVI), and Elevated (Plates XVII–XVIII).

.. folio:: 0044–0045 (f. 14v–f. 15r)
   :initial: 0044.c2.png

Next we describe the third regular body, the :term:`**octocedron** <octahedron>` of eight equilateral triangular bases, together with its truncated (*absciso*) and elevated (*elevato*) dependent bodies in solid and hollow perspective (:ref:`Plates XIII–XVIII <plates-polyhedra-en>`).

.. rubric:: 1. The Regular Octocedron — Solid (Plate XIII) and Hollow (Plate XIV)

- **Plate XIII (Folio 0217, Octocedron Planum Solidu[m])** and **Plate XIV (Folio 0219, Octocedron Planum Vacuum)**:
  This is the third regular body, whose construction in the sphere we demonstrated in :ref:`Chapter XXVIII <compendio-cap_28-en>` (:just:`XIII.14`). It has **8 equilateral triangular bases**, **6 solid angles** (each contained by 4 plane angles of :math:`\frac{2}{3}R`), and **12 equal edges**.

.. rubric:: 2. The Truncated Octahedron (*Octocedron Abscisum*) — Solid (Plate XV) and Hollow (Plate XVI)

- **Plate XV (Folio 0221, Octocedron Abscisum Solidu[m])** and **Plate XVI (Folio 0223, Octocedron Abscisum Vacuum)**:
  This dependent body is formed from the regular :term:`octocedron <octahedron>`:

  - Note carefully the two ways of truncating the :term:`octocedron <octahedron>`:

    1. If the **12 edges** of the :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` are divided into **three equal parts** (:math:`\frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{3}`) and the **6 four-faced solid angles** are sliced off at those trisection points, then the **6 cut-off vertices** become **6 squares** (because 4 triangles meet at each vertex of the :term:`octocedron <octahedron>`) and the **8 original triangular faces** become **8 regular hexagons**, producing a body of **14 faces (6 squares and 8 hexagons)**, 24 vertices, and 36 equal edges.
    2. If instead the cut is made at the **midpoints** (:math:`\frac{1}{2}`) of the 12 edges of the :term:`octocedron <octahedron>`, the 6 cut-off vertices become **6 squares** and the 8 original triangles are reduced to **8 equilateral triangles**, yielding the exact same **14-base body of 6 squares and 8 triangles** (the :term:`**cuboctahedron** <exahedron-abscisum>`) that was generated from the cube in Plates IX–X! (In Leonardo's Plates XV and XVI, the rectified 14-face :term:`cuboctahedron <exahedron-abscisum>` is drawn again from the dual octahedral perspective.)

.. rubric:: 3. The Elevated Octahedron (*Octocedron Elevatum* / Stella Octangula) — Solid (Plate XVII) and Hollow (Plate XVIII)

- **Plate XVII (Folio 0225, Octocedron Elevatum Solidu[m])** and **Plate XVIII (Folio 0227, Octocedron Elevatum Vacuum)**:
  This body is formed by erecting upon each of the **8 equilateral triangular bases** of the core :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` an **equilateral triangular pyramid** (a small :term:`*tetracedron* <tetrahedron>`):

  - Externally it is bounded by :math:`8 \times 3 =` **24 equilateral triangular faces** (while the 8 triangular bases of the core :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` lie within).
  - It has **14 vertices** in all (**6 interior re-entrant vertices** of the core :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` and **8 exterior projecting cones** of the eight elevated pyramids—which 8 exterior cones stand at the 8 corners of a circumscribing cube!) and **36 equal edges** (the 12 edges of the core :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` plus :math:`8 \times 3 = 24` rising edges).
