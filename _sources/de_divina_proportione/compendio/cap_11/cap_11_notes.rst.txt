CHAPTER XI — Critical Commentary & Notes
========================================

.. rubric:: On Its Second Essential Effect

Historical and Philological Commentary
--------------------------------------

1. **Converse of the First Effect (Euclid XIII.2)**: In :person:`Euclid <euclid>`'s *Elements* Book XIII, Proposition 2 states: "If the square on a straight line be five times the square on a segment of it, then when the double of the said segment is cut in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <extrema-et-media-ratio>`, the greater segment is the remaining part of the original straight line." :person:`Pacioli <pacioli>` formulates this directly as the converse of Chapter VII/X: if :math:`(a + c)^2 = 5c^2` where :math:`a + b = 2c`, then :math:`c = \frac{a+b}{2}` and :math:`a` is the major golden segment of :math:`a+b`.
