CHAPTER XXIX — Critical Commentary & Notes
==========================================

.. rubric:: How the Fourth Regular Body, Called the Ycocedron of Twenty Triangular Bases, Is Formed in the Sphere.

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagram**: Folio 0034 (``f. 9v``) includes diagram ``0034.g1.png``, showing the sphere diameter divided in :math:`4:1` ratio (:math:`ac = 4\,cb`) and the circle containing the pentagon and decagon.
- **Role of the Divine Proportion**: This construction brings together nearly every theorem proved in Section 2: :cite:`Euclid XIII.9 <euclid:elements:XIII.9>` (sum of hexagon and decagon sides divided in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <extrema-et-media-ratio>`), :cite:`Euclid XIII.10 <euclid:elements:XIII.10>` (:math:`s_{5}^{2} = s_{6}^{2} + s_{10}^{2}`), and :cite:`Euclid XIII.11 <euclid:elements:XIII.11>` (the side of the pentagon is a *Minor*, :cite:`Euclid X.76 <euclid:elements:X.76>`).
- **Exact Formula**: For a sphere of diameter :math:`D`, the radius of the circumcircle of the two parallel pentagonal cross-sections is :math:`R = \frac{D}{\sqrt{5}}`, and the edge of the :term:`icosahedron <icosahedron>` is :math:`a_{20} = R\sqrt{\frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{2}} = D\sqrt{\frac{5 - \sqrt{5}}{10}}`. For :math:`D = 10`, :math:`a_{20} = \sqrt{50 - 10\sqrt{5}} = \sqrt{50 - \sqrt{500}}`.
