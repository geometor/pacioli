Chapter IX: What the Root of a Number and of Any Other Quantity Is
==================================================================

.. rubric:: What the Root of a Number and of Any Other Quantity Is

.. folio:: 0026 (f. 5v)
   :initial: 0026.c1.png

Because in the course of our treatise we shall frequently have occasion to speak of **Roots** (*Radici*), it seems fitting to explain briefly here what that term signifies, even though we have treated of it at length in every way in our great work [*Summa*].

.. enunciation::

   I say, nevertheless, that the **root** (*radice*) of a quantity is likewise a quantity which, when multiplied by itself, produces that quantity of which it is said to be the root; and that multiplication made into itself is called the **square** (*quadrato*) of the said root.

Thus we say that the root of :math:`9` is :math:`3`, the root of :math:`16` is :math:`4`, and the root of :math:`25` is :math:`5`, and so in other cases; while :math:`9`, :math:`16`, and :math:`25` are called **squares** (*quadrati*).

From this it must be known that there are certain quantities which do not have a root that can be named exactly by a number: for instance, :math:`10` has no number which, multiplied by itself, makes :math:`10` exactly; and the same is true of :math:`11`, :math:`12`, :math:`13`, and others like them.

Wherefore there arise two kinds of roots:

- One is called **discrete** (*discreta*), or let us say **rational** (*rationale*), which is a root that can be assigned exactly by a number, just as the root of :math:`9` is :math:`3`;
- The other is called **surd** (*sorda*), which is one that cannot be given exactly by a number, as we have said of the root of :math:`10` and others.

These latter are called by another name **irrational** (:term:`*irrationali* <irrationale>`), because all quantities that cannot be assigned exactly by a number [or ratio of integers] are called irrational in the art, whereas those that can be given by a number are called **rational** (*rationali*). And let this suffice for our purpose concerning roots.
