CHAPTER LXIV — Critical Commentary & Notes
==========================================

.. rubric:: On Pyramids and All Their Differences, and First on the Round Pyramid (Cone, Plates XLIII–XLIV).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Archimedean & Apollonian Classification of Cones (:cite:`Euclid XI.Def.18 <euclid:elements:XI.Def.18>`)**: Before Apollonius of Perga generalized conic sections to slices of any circular cone, Menaechmus, :person:`Euclid <euclid>`, and :person:`Archimedes <archimedes>` generated the three conic sections—the **parabola** (*section of a right-angled cone*), the **ellipse** (*section of an acute-angled cone*), and the **hyperbola** (*section of an obtuse-angled cone*)—by slicing the three species of cones defined in :cite:`Euclid XI.Def.18 <euclid:elements:XI.Def.18>` with a plane perpendicular to a generator! Pacioli gives the complete Euclidean proof via :cite:`Euclid I.5 <euclid:elements:I.5>`, :cite:`Euclid I.19 <euclid:elements:I.19>`, and :cite:`Euclid I.32 <euclid:elements:I.32>`.
