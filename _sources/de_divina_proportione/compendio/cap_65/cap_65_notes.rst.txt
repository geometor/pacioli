CHAPTER LXV — Critical Commentary & Notes
=========================================

.. rubric:: On Lateral (Polygonal) Pyramids and Their Varieties: Right, Oblique, and Truncated (Plates LI–LIX).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagrams & Plates**:

  - Folio 0055 (``f. 20r``) and Folio 0056 (``f. 20v``) contain 10 marginal woodcut diagrams (``0055.g1.png``–``0055.g3.png`` and ``0056.g1.png``–``0056.g7.png``) depicting the triangular, quadrangular, and pentagonal pyramids and their truncated frusta (*tronca tonda A*, *corta triangola B*, *tagliata quadrangola C*).
  - **Plates LI–LIX** (``0293.rst``–``0309.rst``) show Leonardo's full-page perspective drawings of the triangular, quadrangular, and pentagonal pyramids and the truncated pyramid.

Constantin Winterberg's Commentary (1889 Edition)
-------------------------------------------------

- :person:`Winterberg <winterberg>` Footnote 1 (Cap. LXV, p. 262): By *pyramide corta non equale*, Pacioli means a right frustum of a pyramid (*gerade abgestumpfte Pyramide*) whose top and bottom bases are unequal parallel similar polygons.
  (:person:`Winterberg <winterberg>` Fussnote 1 (Cap. LXV, S. 262): *Gerade Pyramide.*)
