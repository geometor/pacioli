Sanudo, Marco
=============

*Venetian Patrician, Mathematician, and Financial Patron of the 1494 Summa*

Learned Venetian nobleman praised by Pacioli on prelim. fol. 1v as 'astrologiae, geometriae, arithmeticaeque cultor exquisitissimus', whose financial patronage underwrote the printing of the 1494 Summa in Venice.
