Sommaripa, Giorgio
==================

*Veronese Patrician and Vernacular Poet*

Author of the Italian caudate sonnet celebrating Pacioli's universal synthesis of arithmetic, geometry, and Venetian double-entry ledger craft on prelim. fol. 1v.

**Principal Works:** *Sonetto a Fra Luca Pacioli (Summa, prelim. fol. 1v)*
