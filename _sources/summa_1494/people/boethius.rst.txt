Boethius, Anicius Manlius Severinus
===================================

*Roman Philosopher and Codifier of the Quadrivium*

Transmitted Nicomachus's arithmetic and musical ratio nomenclature (multiplex, superparticularis, superpartiens) into the Latin scholastic curriculum expounded in Summa Part I, Dist. I & VI.

**Principal Works:** *De Institutione Arithmetica*, *De Institutione Musica*
