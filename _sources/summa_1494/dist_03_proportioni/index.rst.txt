TRACTATUS DE PROPORTIONIBUS ET PROPORTIONALITATIBUS
===================================================

.. rubric:: Part I, Distinctio VI: Universal Praise of Proportion, the Five Boethian Genera, Classical Means, Six Euclidean Modes & the Golden Ratio (fols. 68r–98r)

.. folio:: Part I, fol. 68v

.. rubric:: Distinctio Sexta, Tractatus Primus — Universal Praise of Proportion

This :term:`proportion <proportione>`, Magnanimous Duke, is that one thing among us which is indispensable to every art: without it one can rightly understand neither philosophy, nor medicine, nor astrology, nor music, nor architecture, nor perspective—as is demonstrated in full by **the monarch of painting in our own times**, our fellow citizen of Borgo Sansepolcro :person:`Maestro Piero della Francesca <piero-della-francesca>`, in his worthy book :cite:`*De prospectiva pingendi* <piero-della-francesca:de-prospectiva-pingendi>`, as well as by the Florentine :person:`Messer Leon Battista Alberti <alberti>` (:cite:`*De Re Aedificatoria* <alberti:de-re-aedificatoria>`) and by :person:`Vitruvius <vitruvius>` in the Third Book :cite:`*De Architectura* <vitruvius:de-architectura>` upon the symmetry of the human body.

.. folio:: Part I, fols. 70r–73r, 83r

.. rubric:: The Five Genera of Rational Ratio & Compounded Ratios

Every quantity compared with another of the same kind is either **equal** (*equale*) or **unequal** (*inequale*) to it. If unequal, either the greater is compared to the lesser (*maior inaequalitas*) or the lesser to the greater (*minor inaequalitas*, prefixed with *sub-*), following :person:`Boethius <boethius>` (:cite:`*De Institutione Arithmetica* <boethius:de-institutione-arithmetica>`, :cite:`*De Institutione Musica* <boethius:de-institutione-musica>`) and :person:`Jordanus Nemorarius <jordanus-nemorarius>`. And each has **five universal genera**:

1. :term:`**Multiplex** <multiplex>` (:math:`n : 1`): such as *dupla* (:math:`2:1`, musical diapason), *tripla* (:math:`3:1`), *quadrupla* (:math:`4:1`).
2. :term:`**Superparticularis** <superparticularis>` (:math:`(n + 1) : n`): such as *sesquialtera* (:math:`3:2`, diapente), *sesquitertia* (:math:`4:3`, diatessaron), *sesquiquarta* (:math:`5:4`), *sesquioctava* (:math:`9:8`, whole tone).
3. :term:`**Superpartiens** <superpartiens>` (:math:`(n + r) : n`, :math:`1 < r < n`, :math:`\gcd(r, n) = 1`): such as *superbipartiens tertias* (:math:`5:3`), *supertripartiens quartas* (:math:`7:4`).
4. **Multiplex superparticularis** (:math:`k n + 1 : n`, :math:`k \ge 2`): such as *dupla sesquialtera* (:math:`5:2`).
5. **Multiplex superpartiens** (:math:`k n + r : n`, :math:`k \ge 2, 1 < r < n`): such as *dupla supertripartiens quartas* (:math:`11:4`).

By compounding ratios (*proportioni composte*, `fol. 83r`), joining *sesquialtera* (:math:`3:2`) with *sesquitertia* (:math:`4:3`) yields *dupla* (:math:`\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{4}{3} = \frac{2}{1}`), whereas subtracting *sesquitertia* from *sesquialtera* leaves the tone *sesquioctava* (:math:`\frac{3/2}{4/3} = \frac{9}{8}`).

.. rubric:: The Three Principal Means (:just:`VI.13`) & Six Euclidean Modes (`fol. 74r`)

.. picture:: fig_04_three_means_semicircle.svg
   :alt: Exact semicircle construction of Arithmetic A = 13/2, Geometric G = 6, and Harmonic H = 72/13 means on segments 9 and 4

The celebrated :term:`proportionalities <proportionalita>` among the ancient philosophers are principally three: **Arithmetic** (where the differences are equal, as :math:`9, 6\frac{1}{2}, 4`), **Geometric** (where the ratios are alike, as :math:`9, 6, 4`), and **Harmonic** (where as the first extreme is to the third, so is the difference of the first and second to the difference of the second and third, as :math:`9, 5\frac{7}{13}, 4`).

.. enunciation::

   Between any two unequal positive magnitudes :math:`a = AP` and :math:`b = PB` set end-to-end on diameter :g:`AB <summa.I.6.means:/ A B />` of semicircle :g:`(MB) <summa.I.6.means:( M B )>` with midpoint :g:`M <summa.I.6.means:M>`, a single Euclidean construction exhibits the **Arithmetic** (:math:`A`), **Geometric** (:math:`G`), and **Harmonic** (:math:`H`) means and shows :math:`G^2 = A \cdot H`.

.. assertion:: [Euclid VI.13 & VI.8 Porism] <VI.13>

   Let :math:`AP = 9` and :math:`PB = 4`, so diameter :g:`AB <summa.I.6.means:/ A B />` :math:`= 13` and radius :g:`MQ <summa.I.6.means:/ M Q />` :math:`= \frac{9 + 4}{2} = \frac{13}{2} = A` (the **Arithmetic mean**). Erecting perpendicular :g:`PQ <summa.I.6.means:/ P Q />` at :g:`P <summa.I.6.means:P>` to meet semicircle :g:`(MB) <summa.I.6.means:( M B )>` at :g:`Q <summa.I.6.means:Q>`, right triangle :math:`\triangle AQB` (:just:`III.31`) has altitude :g:`PQ <summa.I.6.means:/ P Q />` :math:`= \sqrt{9 \cdot 4} = 6 = G` (the **Geometric mean**, :just:`VI.13`). Dropping :g:`PN <summa.I.6.means:/ P N />` perpendicular to radius :g:`MQ <summa.I.6.means:/ M Q />` at :g:`N <summa.I.6.means:N>`, right triangle :g:`MPQ <summa.I.6.means:< M P Q >>` is similar to :g:`PQN <summa.I.6.means:< P Q N >>` (:just:`VI.8`), whence:

   .. math::

      PQ^2 = MQ \cdot QN \implies QN = \frac{G^2}{A} = \frac{2ab}{a + b} = \frac{72}{13} = 5\frac{7}{13} = H

   showing that :math:`A > G > H` (:math:`\frac{13}{2} > 6 > \frac{72}{13}`) and :math:`G^2 = A \cdot H = 36`.

Six are the :term:`modes of arguing <modi-arguendi>` (*De sex speciebus sive modis arguendi proportionalitatum*, `fol. 74r`) set down by :person:`Euclid <euclid>` in Book V and expounded by :person:`Campanus <campanus>` upon four proportional quantities :math:`a : b = c : d`: (1) **conversa** (:math:`b:a = d:c`, :just:`V.7.por`), (2) **permutata** (*alternando*, :math:`a:c = b:d`, :just:`V.16`), (3) **congionta** (*componendo*, :math:`(a+b):b = (c+d):d`, :just:`V.18`), (4) **disgiunta** (*dividendo*, :math:`(a-b):b = (c-d):d`, :just:`V.17`), (5) **eversa** (*convertendo*, :math:`a:(a-b) = c:(c-d)`, :just:`V.19.por`), and (6) **equa** (*ex aequali*, :math:`a:e = c:f` when :math:`b:e = d:f`, :just:`V.22`).

.. rubric:: Division of :math:`10` in Extreme and Mean Ratio (:just:`II.11`, :just:`VI.30`, :just:`XIII.1`)

.. picture:: fig_05_extreme_mean_10.svg
   :alt: Euclidean II.11 construction dividing AB = 10 into AC = sqrt(125) - 5 and CB = 15 - sqrt(125)

.. assertion:: [Euclid II.11 & XIII.1] <II.11>

   To divide :g:`AB <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A B />` :math:`= 10` in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <media-et-estrema-ragione>` so :math:`AB : AC = AC : CB`: construct square :g:`ABKD <summa.I.6.divina10:< A B K D >>` of side :math:`10`, bisect :math:`AD` at :g:`E <summa.I.6.divina10:E>` (:math:`AE = 5`), join hypotenuse :g:`EB <summa.I.6.divina10:/ E B />` :math:`= \sqrt{10^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{125}` (:just:`I.47`), swing arc :g:`(EB) <summa.I.6.divina10:( E B )>` to cut :math:`DA` produced at :g:`F <summa.I.6.divina10:F>` so :math:`AF = \sqrt{125} - 5`, and transfer :math:`AF` via arc :g:`(AF) <summa.I.6.divina10:( A F )>` onto :g:`AB <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A B />` at :g:`C <summa.I.6.divina10:C>`, making :g:`AC <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A C />` :eq:`equal <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A C / = summa.I.6.divina10:/ A B />` in square :g:`ACHF <summa.I.6.divina10:< A C H F >>` to rectangle :math:`AB \cdot CB`:

   .. math::

      AC = \sqrt{125} - 5, \qquad CB = 15 - \sqrt{125}, \qquad AB \cdot CB = AC^2 = 150 - \sqrt{12500}

.. qed::

   Adding half the whole (:math:`5`) to the greater :term:`apotome <residuo>` :math:`AC = \sqrt{125} - 5` yields :math:`\sqrt{125}`, whose square :math:`125` is exact *quintupla* (:math:`5 : 1`) to :math:`5^2 = 25` (:just:`XIII.1`; *cf.* :ref:`*De Divina Proportione*, Cap. VIII <compendio-cap_08-en>`).
