Bibliography¶

Primary Incunables, Critical Editions, Translations & Historical Sources

This unified bibliography brings together the authoritative Renaissance print witnesses, manuscript archetypes, and nineteenth-century critical editions underpinning the GEOMETOR digital editions of Fra Luca Pacioli’s De Divina Proportione (1509) and Summa de Arithmetica (1494).

Primary Incunables & Critical Editions¶

De Divina Proportione (Venice: Paganino Paganini, 1509)¶

  • Citation Key: pacioli:divina-proportione

  • Author: Fra Luca Pacioli, O.F.M.

  • Printer: Paganino de' Paganini (Venetiis: Impressum per probum virum Paganinum de Paganinis de Brixia, Kalen. Iunii M.D.VIIII, June 1, 1509).

  • Illustrations: Sixty woodcut polyhedra plates after the original perspective drawings of Leonardo da Vinci, twenty-three Roman geometric alphabet plates, and marginal geometric figures.

The landmark Renaissance masterpiece on the divine proportion (Golden Ratio), regular and Archimedean polyhedra, Vitruvian architectural proportions, and classical lettering.

Summa de Arithmetica, Geometria, Proportioni et Proportionalita (Venice: 1494)¶

  • Citation Key: pacioli:summa

  • Author: Fra Luca Pacioli, O.F.M.

  • Printer: Paganino de' Paganini (Venetiis: Paganinus de Paganinis de Brixia, M.CCCC.LXXXXIIII, die X. Novembris, November 10–20, 1494).

  • Dedication: Guidobaldo da Montefeltro, Duke of Urbino, and Marco Sanudo.

The 616-folio incunable encyclopedia uniting commercial arithmetic, syncopated Cossic algebra (Regola dela Cosa), Boethian and Euclidean proportions, the earliest printed system of double-entry bookkeeping (Particularis de computis et scripturis), and practical stereometry.

Fra Luca Pacioli: Divina Proportione (Winterberg, Vienna 1889)¶

  • Citation Key: winterberg:1889

  • Editor & Translator: Constantin Winterberg

  • Full Title: Fra Luca Pacioli: Divina Proportione — Die Lehre vom Goldenen Schnitt. Nach der venezianischen Ausgabe vom Jahre 1509 neu herausgegeben, übersetzt und erläutert (Quellenschriften für Kunstgeschichte und Kunsttechnik, N.F. Band II, Wien: Carl Graeser, 1889).

The foundational critical edition providing the definitive German translation, extensive introduction, chapter notes, and seventeen mathematical Sachliche Anmerkungen.

Codex Witnesses & Manuscript Tradition¶

  • Codex Ambrosianus 275 inf. (Milan, Biblioteca Ambrosiana): Presentation manuscript of De Divina Proportione completed December 14, 1498 for Ludovico Sforza, Duke of Milan, containing watercolored perspective polyhedra drawings by Leonardo da Vinci.

  • Codex Urbinas Latinus 265 (Vatican Library): Companion dedication manuscript prepared for Guidobaldo da Montefeltro, Duke of Urbino.

  • Codex Palatinus 577 (Florence, Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale): Contemporary Italian manuscript witness.
