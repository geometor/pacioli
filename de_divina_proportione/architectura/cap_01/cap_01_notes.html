CHAPTER I — Critical Commentary & Notes¶

On the Measure and Proportions of the Human Body, of the Head, and of Its Other Members as the Model and Simulacrum of Architecture.

Historical & Mathematical Commentary¶

  • Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagrams & Full-Page Plate:

    • Folio 0066 (f. 25v) contains the two woodcut profile diagrams 0066.g1.png and 0066.g2.png, and Folio 0137 (0137.rst) presents the large full-page woodcut of the human head inscribed in the equilateral triangle \(amk\) and grid \(kmsb\) labeled DIVINA PROPORTIO.

  • Catalog of Fifteen-Century Artillery: Notice Pacioli's remarkable list of 11 siege engines and gunpowder artillery weapons on Folio 0065 (f. 25r): briccole, trabocchi, catapulte, baliste, bombarde, passavolanti, schioppetti, archibusi, cortaldi, basilischi.

  • Rational Canon vs. Irrational *Gratia*: Pacioli’s aesthetic reflection on Folio 0066—that the primary structural divisions of the face (\(\frac{1}{3}\), \(\frac{1}{9}\)) are rational, whereas the living curvature of the chin, brow, and nose-tip involves irrational proportions left to the gratia e arbitrio del prospectivo—is a foundational statement of High Renaissance art theory (anticipating Vasari’s concept of grazia and giudizio dell’occhio).
