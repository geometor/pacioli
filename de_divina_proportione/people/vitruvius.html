Vitruvius Pollio, Marcus¶

Roman Architect and Military Engineer

Author of De Architectura Libri X, the primary classical authority for Pacioli’s Tractato de l’Architectura on human proportion (Homo ad circulum et quadratum), the Doric, Ionic, and Corinthian orders, and entablatures.

Principal Works: De Architectura Libri Decem
