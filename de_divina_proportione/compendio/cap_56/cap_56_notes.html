CHAPTER LVI — Critical Commentary & Notes¶

On the Spherical Body and How It Is Formed (Plates XLI–XLII).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary¶

  • Paganini 1509 Plates Referenced:

    • Plate XLI (0273.rst): Sperico Solido (Solid Sphere, labeled XL in the marginal roman numeral on Folio 0049).

    • Plate XLII (0275.rst): Sperico Vacuo (Hollow/Armillary Sphere with meridian and parallel ribs).

  • Euclid’s Genetic Definition (Euclid XI.Def.14) vs. Theodosius’s Locus Definition: Pacioli contrasts the static locus definition of the sphere (attributed in medieval Latin manuals to Dionysius or Theodosius, Sphaerica I.Def.1) with Euclid’s kinematic definition (Euclid XI.Def.14) by the revolution of a semicircle around its fixed diameter—the exact kinematic method Euclid uses throughout Books XI–XIII to circumscribe a sphere around each of the five Platonic solids.
