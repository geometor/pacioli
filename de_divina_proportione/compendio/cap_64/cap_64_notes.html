CHAPTER LXIV — Critical Commentary & Notes¶

On Pyramids and All Their Differences, and First on the Round Pyramid (Cone, Plates XLIII–XLIV).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary¶

  • Archimedean & Apollonian Classification of Cones (:cite:`Euclid XI.Def.18 <euclid:elements:XI.Def.18>`): Before Apollonius of Perga generalized conic sections to slices of any circular cone, Menaechmus, Euclid, and Archimedes generated the three conic sections—the parabola (section of a right-angled cone), the ellipse (section of an acute-angled cone), and the hyperbola (section of an obtuse-angled cone)—by slicing the three species of cones defined in Euclid XI.Def.18 with a plane perpendicular to a generator! Pacioli gives the complete Euclidean proof via Euclid I.5, Euclid I.19, and Euclid I.32.
