Appendix III: Polyhedra Plates by Leonardo da Vinci¶

Sixty Perspective Woodcuts of the Regular and Dependent Solids in Solid and Skeletal Views

Sixty full-page woodcut plates of the Five Regular Platonic Solids and their Truncated (abscisa), Elevated/Stellated (elevata), and Prismatic/Pyramidal/Spherical Dependent Bodies in Solid (planum/solidum) and Skeletal (vacuum) perspective views, drawn at Milan by Leonardo da Vinci (per mano del nostro Lionardo da Vinci Fiorentino). Opening any plate provides synchronized switching between Leonardo’s English descriptive commentary and the original 1509 Latin and Greek woodcut cartouches.
