.. _persons:

================
Index of Persons
================

.. rubric:: Prosopographical Register of Mathematicians, Architects, Patrons & Philosophers

Biographical register of ancient, medieval, and Renaissance authorities and contemporaries referenced in Fra Luca Pacioli's *De Divina Proportione* (1509).

.. _person-alberti:

Alberti, Leon Battista (1404 – 1472)
------------------------------------

*Florentine Humanist, Architect, and Theorist*

Hosted young Luca Pacioli for several months in his own home in Rome during the pontificate of Pope Paul II (Pietro Barbo); author of De Re Aedificatoria, De Pictura, and De Statua.

**Principal Works:** *De Re Aedificatoria*, *De Pictura*, *Ludi Rerum Mathematicarum*

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Arch. index </de_divina_proportione/architectura/index>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 3 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_03/cap_03_notes>`

.. _person-alexander-the-great:

Alexander III of Macedon (Alexander the Great) (356 – 323 BC)
-------------------------------------------------------------

*King of Macedon*

Patron of the architect Dinocrates in the founding of Alexandria (Architectura Prohemio).

.. _person-ambrogio-da-rosate:

Ambrogio Varese da Rosate (c. 1437 – 1522)
------------------------------------------

*Ducal Physician and Court Astrologer to Ludovico Sforza*

Professor at Pavia and chief physician and astrologer at the Sforza court; participant in the 1498 scientific duel in the Castello Sforzesco.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_en>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_it>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_notes>`

.. _person-andrea-da-novara:

Andrea da Novara (fl. 1498)
---------------------------

*Physician and Astronomer at the Sforza Court*

Participant in the February 9, 1498 scientific disputation at the Castello Sforzesco in Milan.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_en>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_it>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_notes>`

.. _person-apelles:

Apelles of Kos (fl. 4th century BC)
-----------------------------------

*Classical Greek Painter*

Court painter to Alexander the Great; Pacioli declares in Chapters I and XXIII that Apelles, Myron, and Polykleitos must yield before Leonardo da Vinci's Last Supper.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (DE) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_de>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_en>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_it>`

.. _person-apuleius:

Apuleius of Madaura (c. 124 – after 170 AD)
-------------------------------------------

*Middle Platonic Philosopher and Rhetorician*

Author of De Platone et eius Dogmate, cited with Chalcidius and Macrobius in Compendio Chapter LV on Platonic natural philosophy.

**Principal Works:** *De Platone et eius Dogmate*

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 55 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_55/cap_55_en>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 55 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_55/cap_55_it>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 55 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_55/cap_55_notes>`

.. _person-archimedes:

Archimedes of Syracuse (c. 287 – 212 BC)
----------------------------------------

*Greek Geometer, Physicist, and Military Engineer*

Celebrated by Pacioli both for his defense of Syracuse against Marcus Claudius Marcellus using geometrical war machines and for his measurement of the circle (3 10/71 < π < 3 1/7), sphere, and cylinder, as well as the semi-regular (Archimedean) polyhedra.

.. _person-aristotle:

Aristotle of Stagira (384 – 322 BC)
-----------------------------------

*Greek Philosopher ('El Philosopho' / 'Il Maestro di color che sanno')*

Cited throughout De Divina Proportione on the primacy of mathematical certainty ('in primo gradu certitudinis'), sense perception ('nihil est in intellectu quin prius fuerit in sensu'), philosophical wonder ('propter admirari'), and the rejection of the void (De Caelo).

**Principal Works:** *Analytica Posteriora*, *Physica*, *De Caelo et Mundo*, *Metaphysica*, *De Anima*, *Meteorologica*, *Ethica Nicomachea*

.. _person-augustine:

Augustine of Hippo, St. (Aurelius Augustinus) (354 – 430 AD)
------------------------------------------------------------

*Bishop of Hippo and Doctor of the Church*

Author of De Civitate Dei, cited by Pacioli in Compendio Chapter II on the supreme Artisan who created all things in number, weight, and measure.

**Principal Works:** *De Civitate Dei*, *De Genesi ad Litteram*

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 2 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_02/cap_02_en>`

.. _person-augustus:

Augustus, Caesar (Octavian) (63 BC – 14 AD)
-------------------------------------------

*First Roman Emperor*

Cited in Compendio Chapter LXIX alongside the Temple of Universal Peace in Rome as a classical parallel to Ludovico Sforza's patronage of Santa Maria delle Grazie.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 2 (DE) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_02/cap_02_de>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 69 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_69/cap_69_notes>`

.. _person-averroes:

Averroes (Ibn Rushd) (1126 – 1198)
----------------------------------

*Andalusian Philosopher and Commentator on Aristotle*

Cited with Aristotle in Compendio Chapter I (from Metaphysics II comm. 16) for the maxim that the mathematical sciences stand in the first degree of certainty ('in primo gradu certitudinis').

**Principal Works:** *Commentaria Magna in Aristotelem*

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (DE) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_de>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_en>`, :doc:`Comp. .. _person-avicenna:

Avicenna (Ibn Sīnā) (980 – 1037)
--------------------------------

*Persian Physician, Astronomer, and Philosopher*

Author of the Canon Medicinae; cited in Compendio Chapter I both among the authorities expounded by Ambrogio da Rosate and alongside Galen and Hippocrates on the seat of vital spirit.

**Principal Works:** *Canon Medicinae*

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (DE) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_de>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_en>`, :doc:`Comp. .. _person-bartolus:

Bartolus of Sassoferrato (Bartolo da Sassoferrato) (1313 – 1357)
----------------------------------------------------------------

*Medieval Italian Jurist and Commentator on Roman Law*

Cited by Pacioli in Compendio Chapter II for his geometric treatise De Fluminibus seu Tyberiadis ('la Teberina'), which used Euclidean compass-and-straightedge constructions to divide alluvial river deposits.

**Principal Works:** *Tractatus de Fluminibus seu Tyberiadis*

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 2 (DE) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_02/cap_02_de>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 2 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_02/cap_02_en>`, :doc:`Comp. .. _person-bramante:

Bramante, Donato (1444 – 1514)
------------------------------

*Renaissance Architect*

Active alongside Leonardo da Vinci and Luca Pacioli at the Sforza court in Milan; designed the tribune of Santa Maria delle Grazie and the sacristy of Santa Maria presso San Satiro, whose perspective domes Pacioli relates to the 72-base body in Chapter LIV.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 54 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_54/cap_54_en>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 54 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_54/cap_54_notes>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 69 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_69/cap_69_notes>`

.. _person-duns-scotus:

Duns Scotus, John, O.F.M. ('Il Dottor Sottile') (c. 1266 – 1308)
----------------------------------------------------------------

*Franciscan Scholastic Philosopher and Theologian*

Praised by Pacioli in Compendio Chapter II for attaining the loftiest speculations of theology through his mastery of Euclid's Elements, particularly in Book II of his Commentary on the Sentences on the definitive location of angels and indivisibles.

**Principal Works:** *Ordinatio / Quaestiones in Quattuor Libros Sententiarum*

.. _person-ippolito-deste:

Este, Cardinal Ippolito I d' (1479 – 1520)
------------------------------------------

*Cardinal-Deacon of Santa Lucia in Selci and Archbishop of Milan*

Brother-in-law of Ludovico Sforza (brother of Beatrice d'Este); witnessed the presentation of De Divina Proportione in Milan, attended the 1498 Franciscan General Chapter, and participated in the Vitruvian architectural discussion on Milan Cathedral.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 69 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_69/cap_69_notes>`

Zeuxis of Heraclea (fl. late 5th century BC)
--------------------------------------------

*Classical Greek Painter*

Celebrated with his rival Parrhasius in Compendio Chapter III (from Pliny's Naturalis Historia XXXV.65) for painting grapes so lifelike that birds flew down to peck at them.
