Glossary of Geometric, Architectural & Latin Terms

.. _term-abaco:

abaco (abacus) (abaco)
----------------------

**Language:** Greek / Latin / Italian | **Translation:** *Abacus of a Capital*

The flat slab forming the uppermost member of a column capital immediately beneath the architrave (Architectura Cap. VII, Folio 0213).

.. _term-abscisum:

abscisum / absciso / scapezzo (ἀποτετμημένον) (abscisum)
--------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Italian / Greek | **Translation:** *Truncated Polyhedron*

A dependent (Archimedean) polyhedron derived from a regular body by cutting off its vertices at one-third or one-half of each edge (generating the truncated tetrahedron, cuboctahedron, truncated octahedron, truncated icosahedron, and icosidodecahedron).

.. _term-acroterio:

acroterio / regolo (acroterio)
------------------------------

**Language:** Greek / Italian | **Translation:** *Acroterium / Crowning Fillet*

Pedestal at the apex and eaves of a pediment, or the uppermost crowning fillet (regolo) at the top of the cornice on Folio 0215 (Architectura Cap. XIV, XVI).

.. _term-algebra-e-almucabala:

algebra e almucabala (regola de la cosa) (algebra-e-almucabala)
---------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Arabic / Italian | **Translation:** *Algebra and Almucabala (The Rule of the Thing)*

The speculative practice of restoration (al-jabr) and balancing (al-muqābala) of equations, termed in the Italian vernacular 'la regola de la cosa' (Chapter VIII).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 52 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_52/cap_52_it>`

.. _term-ambliogonio:

ambliogonio (triangulo obtusangulo) (ambliogonio)
-------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Greek / Italian | **Translation:** *Obtuse-Angled Triangle (Amblygonion)*

A triangle containing one obtuse angle greater than 90° (Compendio Cap. LX).

.. _term-angulo-acuto:

angulo acuto (angulo-acuto)
---------------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Acute Angle (< 90°)*

A plane angle smaller than a right angle (Euclid I.def.12; Compendio Cap. LX).

.. _term-angulo-dela-iustitia:

angulo dela iustitia (angolo recto) (angulo-dela-iustitia)
----------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Angle of Justice (Right Angle, 90°)*

Pacioli's name in Chapter LIV for the invariant 90° right angle determined by the plummet and square, without which good cannot be distinguished from bad in architecture and measurement.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 54 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_54/cap_54_en>`

.. _term-angulo-obtuso:

angulo obtuso (angulo-obtuso)
-----------------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Obtuse Angle (> 90°)*

A plane angle greater than a right angle (Euclid I.def.11; Compendio Cap. LX).

.. _term-angulo-recto:

angulo recto (angulo-recto)
---------------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Right Angle (90°)*

An angle formed when a straight line standing on another makes the adjacent angles equal (Euclid I.def.10); called by Pacioli the 'Angle of Justice' (Compendio Cap. LIV, LX).

.. _term-angulo-solido:

angulo solido (angulo-solido)
-----------------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Solid Angle (Polyhedral Vertex)*

An angle formed by the meeting of three or more plane angles at a single point not lying in the same plane; the sum of its plane angles must be strictly less than four right angles (360°, Euclid XI.21).

.. _term-arbor-proportionis:

Arbor Proportio et Proportionalitas (arbor-proportionis)
--------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin | **Translation:** *Tree of Ratio and Proportion*

The full-page dichotomous classification diagram printed in red and black on Folio 0311, dividing continuous and discrete quantity into rational and irrational ratios and branching into the five species of commensurate ratios (multiplex, superparticularis, superpartiens, multiplex superparticularis, multiplex superpartiens).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Plates: Architectural (DE) </de_divina_proportione/plates/architectural_de>`, :doc:`Plates: Architectural (EN) </de_divina_proportione/plates/architectural_en>`, :doc:`Plates: Architectural (IT) </de_divina_proportione/plates/architectural_it>`

.. _term-archipendolo:

archipendolo / perpendiculo (piombo) (archipendolo)
---------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Plumb-Level (Plummet and A-Frame Level)*

Instrument using a weighted plumb-line (catheto) to determine true verticality and horizontal leveling in building (Compendio Cap. LIV, LXXI).

.. _term-arco:

arco (arcus) (arco)
-------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Circular Arc*

A portion of the circumference of a circle cut off by a chord (Compendio Cap. LXII, LXXI).

.. _term-astragalo:

astragalo / tondino (astragalo)
-------------------------------

**Language:** Greek / Italian | **Translation:** *Astragal / Bead Molding (Tondino)*

Small convex semicircular molding with fillet at the top of a column shaft beneath the capital necking, or dividing fasciae of the architrave (Architectura Cap. VII, Folio 0213, 0215).

.. _term-astronomia:

astronomia / astrologia (astronomia)
------------------------------------

**Language:** Greek / Latin / Italian | **Translation:** *Astronomy (Science of Celestial Motion)*

The mathematical science of mobile continuous magnitude and the revolutions of the celestial spheres (Compendio Cap. III).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_it>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 3 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_03/cap_03_it>`

.. _term-binomio:

binomio
-------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Binomial Irrational (a + √b)*

The sum of two segments commensurable only in square (Euclid X.36, X.48–53), conjugate to the apotome (residuo).

.. _term-censo:

censo (ce.) (censo)
-------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *The Square of the Unknown (x²) in Cossic Algebra*

Renaissance abacus term (translating Arabic māl / Latin census) for the square of the unknown quantity.

.. _term-censo-de-censo:

censo de censo (ce. ce.) (censo-de-censo)
-----------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Fourth Power of the Unknown (x⁴) in Cossic Algebra*

Abacus algebraic term for the square of the square (x⁴), extensively used by Piero della Francesca and Pacioli in the biquadratic problems of the Libellus.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Arch. Cap. 3 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/architectura/cap_03/cap_03_en>`, :doc:`Arch. Cap. 3 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/architectura/cap_03/cap_03_it>`, :doc:`Arch. Cap. 3 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/architectura/cap_03/cap_03_notes>`

.. _term-centro:

centro
------

**Language:** Greek / Italian | **Translation:** *Center (κέντρον)*

Defined in Chapter LXXI: the middle point inside a circle or sphere from which all straight lines drawn to the circumference are equal.

.. _term-cimento-e-copella:

cimento e copella (cimento-e-copella)
-------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Touchstone and Assaying-Cup (Cupel)*

Metallurgical metaphor used by Pacioli in Chapters I–II for the mathematical disciplines as the infallible test of intellectual rigor ('aurum probatur igni et ingenium mathematicis').

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_en>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 1 (IT) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_01/cap_01_it>`

.. _term-circino:

circino / sesto (compasso) (circino)
------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Italian | **Translation:** *Pair of Compasses (Dividers)*

Along with the straightedge/ruler (regolo/norma), one of the two universal instruments of geometry and all mechanical arts, generating the circle and transferring proportions (Compendio Cap. LIV; Alphabet Folios 0139–0183).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Arch. Cap. 6 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/architectura/cap_06/cap_06_en>`, :doc:`Arch. Cap. 9 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/architectura/cap_09/cap_09_en>`, :doc:`Arch. .. _term-circonferentia:

circonferentia / peripheria (circonferentia)
--------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Italian / Greek | **Translation:** *Circumference / Periphery of a Circle*

The curved boundary line of a circle, whose ratio to its diameter was shown by Archimedes to lie between 3 10/71 and 3 1/7 (approx. 22:7; Compendio Cap. LXII).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Plates: Alphabet (IT) </de_divina_proportione/plates/alphabet_it>`

.. _term-circulo:

circulo / cerchio (circulo)
---------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Italian | **Translation:** *Circle*

A plane figure bounded by a single curved line (circumference) such that all straight lines drawn to it from an interior center are equal (Euclid I.def.15).

.. _term-colonna-corinthia:

colonna corinthia (colonna-corinthia)
-------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Corinthian Column and Order*

The most slender and ornate of the three classical orders, invented by Callimachus at Corinth in imitation of maidenly grace and an acanthus-wreathed basket over a maiden's tomb, with a capital height equal to an entire lower shaft diameter (Architectura Cap. III–IV, VII; Folio 0215).

.. _term-colonna-dorica:

colonna dorica (colonna-dorica)
-------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Doric Column and Order*

The first and most virile of the three classical Greek orders, modeled on the naked proportion of a man (6:1 or 7:1 height-to-diameter), built without a base in ancient Greek temples and crowned by a plain echinus-and-abacus capital beneath a triglyph frieze (Architectura Cap. III–IV, VII, XII).

.. _term-colonna-exagona:

Columna laterata exagona (Κίων πλευρώδης ἑξάγωνος) (colonna-exagona)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Greek / Italian | **Translation:** *Hexagonal Prismatic Column (Hexagonal Prism)*

Six-sided right prism with parallel regular hexagonal bases and six rectangular lateral faces (Compendio Cap. LXI, Plates XLVII–XLVIII).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (EN) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_en>`, :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (LA) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_la>`

.. _term-colonna-ionica:

colonna ionica (pulvinata) (colonna-ionica)
-------------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Ionic Column and Order*

The middle classical order, modeled on matronly slenderness (8:1 height-to-diameter), furnished with a base with scotia and tori, fluted shaft, and a voluted ('pulvinated') capital mimicking coiled hair (Architectura Cap. III, V, VII; Folio 0213).

.. _term-colonna-laterata:

colonna laterata (columna laterata / κίων πλευρώδης) (colonna-laterata)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin / Greek | **Translation:** *Prismatic (Polygonal) Column / Prism*

An oblong solid whose two opposite bases are congruent rectilinear polygons (triangles, squares, pentagons, hexagons) connected by rectangular lateral faces (Compendio Cap. LVIII–LXI, LXIII).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Arch. Cap. 9 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/architectura/cap_09/cap_09_en>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 59 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_59/cap_59_en>`, :doc:`Comp. .. _term-colonna-pentagona:

Columna laterata pentagona (Κίων πλευρώδης πεντάγωνος) (colonna-pentagona)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Greek / Italian | **Translation:** *Pentagonal Prismatic Column (Pentagonal Prism)*

Five-sided right prism with parallel regular pentagonal bases and five rectangular lateral faces (Compendio Cap. LXI, Plates XLV–XLVI).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (EN) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_en>`, :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (LA) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_la>`

Columna laterata quadrilatera (Κίων πλευρώδης τετράπλευρος) (colonna-quadrilatera)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Greek / Italian | **Translation:** *Quadrangular Prismatic Column (Square Prism)*

Four-sided right prism with parallel square bases and four oblong rectangular lateral faces (Compendio Cap. LIX–LXI, Plates XLIII–XLIV).

.. _term-colonna-rotonda:

colonna rotonda (columna rotunda / κίων στρογγύλος) (colonna-rotonda)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin / Greek | **Translation:** *Round Column / Right Circular Cylinder*

cono dela pyramide (cono-pyramidale)
------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Apex (Vertex) of a Pyramid or Cone*

Defined in Chapter LXXI: the supreme summit point of a pyramid where all lateral edges ascending from its base concur.

.. _term-corausto:

corausto (coraustus) (corausto)
-------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Italian | **Translation:** *Coraustus (Upper Horizontal Chord Joining Erected Perpendiculars)*

Defined in Chapter LXXI (from Campanus on Euclid XIII.16): a straight line joining the upper extremities of two equal perpendiculars (catheti) erected upon a base plane, forming the upper horizontal ring of the icosahedron.

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Comp. Cap. 26 (Notes) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_26/cap_26_notes>`, :doc:`Comp. Cap. 45 (EN) </de_divina_proportione/compendio/cap_45/cap_45_en>`, :doc:`Comp. .. _term-corda:

corda (chorda) (corda)
----------------------

**Language:** Italian / Latin | **Translation:** *Chord of a Circle or Polygon*

A straight line joining the endpoints of a circular arc or two non-adjacent vertices of a polygon (Compendio Cap. XVIII, LXII, LXXI).

.. _term-corda-pentagonica:

.. _term-tetrahedron-abscisum:

Tetrahedron abscisum (Τετράεδρον ἀποτετμημένον) (tetrahedron-abscisum)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Greek | **Translation:** *Truncated Tetrahedron (8-Base Archimedean Solid)*

The first dependent body derived from the regular tetrahedron by trisecting its edges and cutting off its four corners, bounded by 4 regular hexagons and 4 equilateral triangles (8 faces, 18 edges, 12 vertices; Compendio Cap. XLVII, Plates III–IV).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (EN) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_en>`, :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (LA) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_la>`

.. _term-tetrahedron-elevatum:

Tetrahedron elevatum (Τετράεδρον ἐπηρμένον) (tetrahedron-elevatum)
------------------------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Latin / Greek | **Translation:** *Elevated (Stellated) Tetrahedron*

Stellated polyhedron formed by erecting 4 equilateral triangular pyramids upon the 4 faces of a core regular tetrahedron, bounded externally by 12 equilateral triangles (Compendio Cap. XLVIII, Plates V–VI).

**Occurrences:** :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (EN) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_en>`, :doc:`Plates: Polyhedra (LA) </de_divina_proportione/plates/polyhedra_la>`

.. _term-tondo-e-quadro:

tondo e quadro (circolo e quadrato) (tondo-e-quadro)
----------------------------------------------------

**Language:** Italian | **Translation:** *Circle and Square (Compass and Straightedge Grid)*

The two primary geometric figures—representing the curved line (compass/circino) and the straight line (ruler/libella)—from which both the Vitruvian human body and all twenty-three letters (A–Y) of Pacioli's Roman geometric alphabet (Folios 0139–0183) are generated.
