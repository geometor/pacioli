CHAPTER XVII — Critical Commentary & Notes
==========================================

.. rubric:: On the Site of the Triglyphs and the Union of All the Preceding Parts in the Temple Portal Called *Porta Speciosa* (Plate 0185).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Plate Referenced**: Folio 0185 (``0185.rst``, ``0185.g1.png``) is the magnificent full-page woodcut inscribed ``PORTA TEMPLI DOMINI DICTA SPECIOSA`` ("The Gate of the Lord's Temple Called Beautiful", referencing Acts 3:2 and Ezekiel 40–41), showing the complete portal elevation flanked by Corinthian/composite columns on tall stylobates supporting the three-fascia epistyle, carved frieze, dentil cornice, and triangular pediment.
- **Pacioli Day and Night at Paganino de' Paganini's Printing Presses (May 1509)**: Notice :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` vivid snapshot on Folio 0079 (``f. 32r``): *"Però che de continuo dì e nocte me conviene in su li torcoli e lor calcographi a governar l'opere nostre con tutta diligentia comme se richiede"*—standing day and night beside Paganini's presses and woodcutters in Venice in May 1509 as both the Latin :person:`*Euclid* <euclid>` and :cite:`*De Divina Proportione* <pacioli:divina-proportione>` were being printed!
