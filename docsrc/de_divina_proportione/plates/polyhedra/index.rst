Appendix III: Polyhedra Plates by Leonardo da Vinci
===================================================

.. rubric:: Sixty Perspective Woodcuts of the Regular and Dependent Solids in Solid and Skeletal Views

Sixty full-page woodcut plates of the Five Regular Platonic Solids and their Truncated (*abscisa*), Elevated/Stellated (*elevata*), and Prismatic/Pyramidal/Spherical Dependent Bodies in Solid (*planum/solidum*) and Skeletal (:term:`*vacuum* <vacuum>`) perspective views, drawn at Milan by :person:`**Leonardo da Vinci** <leonardo>` (*per mano del nostro Lionardo da Vinci Fiorentino*). Opening any plate provides synchronized switching between Leonardo's English descriptive commentary and the original 1509 Latin and Greek woodcut cartouches.
