Fromme, Carl)
=============

*Imperial-Royal Court Printer in Vienna*

Viennese court printing house (k. k. Hofbuchdruckerei Carl Fromme) that printed Constantin Winterberg's 1889 edition of Pacioli's Divina Proportione.
