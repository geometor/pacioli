CHAPTER XXXV
============

.. rubric:: How the Octocedron Is Formed and Inscribed in the Tetracedron.

.. folio:: 0041 (f. 13r)
   :initial: 0041.c2.png

We begin with the first proposition of the fifteenth book (:cite:`Campanus XV.1 <campanus:euclid:XV.1>`), which shows how to inscribe the :term:`**octocedron** <octahedron>` (8 triangular bases) inside the :term:`**tetracedron** <tetrahedron>` (4 triangular bases).

.. enunciation:: Inscribing the Octahedron in the Tetrahedron (Campanus XV.1)

   In a given regular pyramid of four equilateral triangular bases (:term:`*tetracedron* <tetrahedron>`), to inscribe the regular body of eight equilateral triangular bases (:term:`*octocedron* <octahedron>`) so that the six :term:`solid angles <angulo-solido>` of the :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` stand upon the midpoints of the six edges of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>`.

.. assertion:: Construction and Proof (:just:`VI.2`, :just:`VI.4`, :just:`I.8`, :just:`XI.10`)

   1. Let the given :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>` have base triangle :math:`abc` and upper vertex (cone) :math:`d`, bounded by the four equilateral triangles :math:`abc, abd, acd, bcd` and the six equal edges :math:`ab, bc, ca, da, db, dc` (see the marginal diagram ``0038.g1.png`` on Folio 0038).
   2. Bisect each of the six edges of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>` at its midpoint: let :math:`e, f, g` be the midpoints of the three base edges :math:`ab, bc, ca`, and let :math:`h, k, l` be the midpoints of the three ascending edges :math:`da, db, dc`.
   3. Join these six midpoints by twelve straight lines:

      - Three in the base plane: :math:`ef, fg, ge`;
      - Three in the upper horizontal plane: :math:`hk, kl, lh`;
      - Six along the three lateral faces: :math:`eh, hf, fk, kg, gl, le`.

   4. In each of the four equilateral faces of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>`, the lines joining the midpoints of the sides are parallel to the third side (:just:`VI.2`) and equal to **half** that side (:just:`VI.4`). Since all six edges of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>` are equal, each of the twelve connecting lines :math:`ef, fg, ge, hk, kl, lh, eh, hf, fk, kg, gl, le` equals **half the edge of the tetracedron**!
   5. Therefore, the eight triangular faces bounded by these twelve equal lines—four lying flat within the four faces of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>` (:math:`efg, ehf, fkg, gle`) and four cutting off the four corner pyramids at :math:`a, b, c, d` (:math:`eh l, hfk, kgl, hkl`)—are all equilateral and mutually equal; and their :term:`solid angles <angulo-solido>` are mutually equal (:just:`XI.10`).

Thus the regular :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` :math:`efghkl` is inscribed in the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>` :math:`abcd`, with its edge equal to **half** the edge of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>` (so the edge of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>` is double the edge of its inscribed :term:`octocedron <octahedron>`, and the volume of the :term:`tetracedron <tetrahedron>`—consisting of the central :term:`octocedron <octahedron>` plus four corner tetrahedra of half the linear scale—is **double** the volume of the inscribed :term:`octocedron <octahedron>`!).
