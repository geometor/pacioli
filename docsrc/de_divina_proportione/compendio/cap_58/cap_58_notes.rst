CHAPTER LVIII — Critical Commentary & Notes
===========================================

.. rubric:: On Oblong Bodies, That Is, Bodies Longer or Higher Than They Are Wide: And First on the Round Column (Cylinder, Plates XLIII–XLIV).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagram & Plates**:

  - Folio 0052 (``f. 18v``) displays diagram ``0052.g1.png`` showing the axial rectangle :math:`dcef` of the cylinder with its axis :math:`ab` and diagonal :math:`de` intersecting at the center :math:`g`.
  - **Plate XLIII** (``0277.rst``) and **Plate XLIV** (``0279.rst``): Note that in the 1509 plates, Plates XLIII–XLIV show the round cone (*Cono Solido / Vacuo*) while the marginal text numerals shifted slightly between prisms and cones; ``Cap. LXX`` explains how to match the Roman numerals in the margins.

- **Comparison with Earlier Draft Track**: The earlier ``divina_en_2/58.txt`` draft omitted the entire Euclidean proof (:cite:`Euclid I.15 <euclid:elements:I.15>`, :cite:`Euclid I.26 <euclid:elements:I.26>`, :cite:`Euclid III.31 <euclid:elements:III.31>`) that the center :math:`g` bisects both the axis :math:`ab` and the diagonal :math:`de` so that every right cylinder is inscribable in a sphere.
