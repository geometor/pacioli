CHAPTER LXII — Critical Commentary & Notes
==========================================

.. rubric:: On the Way to Measure All Kinds of Columns, and First the Round Columns (Cylinders).

Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Paganini 1509 Marginal Diagrams**: Folio 0054 (``f. 19v``) includes the unit cube diagram ``0054.g7.png`` and the circle inscribed in the square :math:`abcd` with diameter :math:`ef` and center :math:`g` (``0054.g8.png``), visually showing why the circle occupies :math:`\frac{11}{14}` of the circumscribed square and the four corner spandrels occupy :math:`\frac{3}{14}`.
- **Archimedes' *Measurement of a Circle***: Proposition 3 of Archimedes' *:cite:`Dimensio Circuli <archimedes:dimensio-circuli>`* proves :math:`3\frac{10}{71} < \pi < 3\frac{1}{7}`, whence Proposition 2 states that the area of a circle to the square on its diameter is very nearly :math:`11 : 14`.
