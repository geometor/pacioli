CHAPTER XIV — Critical Commentary & Notes
=========================================

.. rubric:: On Its Fifth Marvelous Effect

Historical and Philological Commentary
--------------------------------------

1. **Algebraic Identity Behind the Fifth Effect (Euclid XIII.4)**: Since $b(a+b) = a^2$ and $b = (a+b) - a$, we have:
   $$b^2 + (a+b)^2 = b^2 + (a+b)^2 = (a+b)^2 - 2(a+b)b + b^2 + 2(a+b)b = a^2 + 2a^2 = 3a^2$$
   In :person:`Pacioli's <pacioli>` single-radical notation, multiplying $\sqrt{12500}$ by $3$ brings $3^2 = 9$ inside the radical: $\sqrt{9 \times 12500} = \sqrt{112500}$.

Constantin Winterberg's Commentary (1889 Edition)
-------------------------------------------------

.. rubric:: Note 5 (Cap. XIV): Algebraic Proof of the Fifth Effect (Euclid XIII.4) (:ref:`Sachliche Anmerkungen #5 <plates-anmerkungen-en>`, Cap. XIV, p. 204, l. 14 from top)

In general algebraic form, for the line $bc$ divided in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <extrema-et-media-ratio>` at $d$ ($cd > bd$):

- **Hypothesis**: $cd^2 = bc \cdot bd$.
- **Claim**: $bd^2 + bc^2 = 3\,cd^2$.
- **Proof**:
  a) From Note 4, $bc^2 = bc \cdot cd + cd^2$.
  b) Expanding $bd^2 = (bc - cd)^2 = bc^2 - 2\,bc \cdot cd + cd^2$.
  Adding (a) and (b) together and substituting $bc^2 - bc \cdot cd = bc(bc - cd) = bc \cdot bd = cd^2$ gives:

  .. math::

     bd^2 + bc^2 = 2\,cd^2 + bc(bc - cd) = 2\,cd^2 + bc \cdot bd = 3\,cd^2. \quad \text{Q.E.D.}

.. rubric:: Anmerkung 5 (Cap. XIV, S. 204): Mathematischer Nachweis des fünften Effects (Euklid XIII.4)

In allgemeiner Form unter der früheren Voraussetzung ($cd^2 = bc \cdot bd$):

- **Behauptung**: $bd^2 + bc^2 = 3\,cd^2$.
- **Beweis**:
  a) $bc^2 = bc \cdot cd + cd^2$ (nach Anm. 4).
  b) $bd^2 = (bc - cd)^2 = bc^2 - 2\,bc \cdot cd + cd^2$.
  Durch Addition von a) und b) erhält man:

  .. math::

     bd^2 + bc^2 = 2\,cd^2 + bc(bc - cd) = 2\,cd^2 + bc \cdot bd = 3\,cd^2. \quad \text{w. z. b. w.}
