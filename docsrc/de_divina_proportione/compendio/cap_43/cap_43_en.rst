CHAPTER XLIII
=============

.. rubric:: How the Ycocedron Is Formed in the Duodecedron.

.. folio:: 0042 (f. 13v)

Now we come to the ninth proposition of the fifteenth book (:cite:`Campanus XV.9 <campanus:euclid:XV.9>`), where we begin inscribing all four of the other regular bodies inside the fifth and noblest body, the :term:`**duodecedron** <dodecahedron>` (12 pentagonal bases)—which, as :person:`Plato <plato>` says in the :cite:`*Timaeus* <plato:timaeus:53c-56c>`, is the receptacle and lodging (*receptaculo e albergo*) of all the others—starting with the :term:`**ycocedron** <icosahedron>` (20 triangular bases).

.. enunciation:: Inscribing the Icosahedron in the Dodecahedron (Campanus XV.9)

   In a given regular body of twelve pentagonal bases (:term:`*duodecedron* <dodecahedron>`), to inscribe the regular body of twenty equilateral triangular bases (:term:`*ycocedron* <icosahedron>`) so that the twelve vertices of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>` stand at the twelve centers of the pentagonal faces of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>`.

.. assertion:: Construction and Proof (:just:`IV.14`, :just:`XIII.17`, :just:`XI.10`)

   1. In each of the twelve regular pentagonal faces of the given :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>`, find the **center** of that pentagon by the :just:`fourteenth of the fourth <IV.14>` (bisecting two adjacent angles of the pentagon; the intersection of the bisectors is the center of the pentagon), giving **twelve face-centers** in all (see the marginal diagram ``0040.g3.png`` on Folio 0040).
   2. Around each of the twenty :term:`solid angles <angulo-solido>` (vertices) of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>`, exactly **three** regular pentagonal faces meet (:ref:`Chapter XXV <compendio-cap_25-en>`). Join the three centers of the three pentagons meeting at each vertex of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>` by three straight lines.
   3. Because the three pentagonal faces meeting at any vertex of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>` are equal and equally inclined to one another, the three straight lines connecting their centers are mutually equal and form an **equilateral triangle** opposite that vertex.
   4. Carrying out this construction around all **twenty** vertices of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>` produces **twenty equal equilateral triangles** bounded by thirty equal edges meeting five by five at the **twelve** centers of the pentagonal faces of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>`.

Therefore the regular :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>` is inscribed in the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>`, with its twelve :term:`solid angles <angulo-solido>` touching the centers of the twelve pentagonal bases of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>`.

.. qed::
