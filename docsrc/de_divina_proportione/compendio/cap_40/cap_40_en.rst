CHAPTER XL
==========

.. rubric:: How the Duodecedron Is Formed in the Ycocedron.

.. folio:: 0042 (f. 13v)

The sixth proposition of the fifteenth book (:cite:`Campanus XV.6 <campanus:euclid:XV.6>`) demonstrates how to inscribe the :term:`**duodecedron** <dodecahedron>` (12 pentagonal bases) inside the :term:`**ycocedron** <icosahedron>` (20 triangular bases).

.. enunciation:: Inscribing the Dodecahedron in the Icosahedron (Campanus XV.6)

   In a given regular body of twenty equilateral triangular bases (:term:`*ycocedron* <icosahedron>`), to inscribe the regular body of twelve equilateral and equiangular pentagonal bases (:term:`*duodecedron* <dodecahedron>`) so that the twenty vertices of the :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>` stand at the twenty centers of the triangular faces of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>`.

.. rubric:: Construction and Proof (:just:`IV.4`, :just:`XIII.16`, :just:`XIII.7`)

.. assertion::

   1. In each of the twenty equilateral triangular faces of the given :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>`, find the **center** of that triangle (:just:`IV.4`), giving twenty points in all—one at the middle of each face of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>` (see the marginal diagram ``0039.g3.png`` on Folio 0039).
   2. Around each of the twelve :term:`solid angles <angulo-solido>` (vertices) of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>`, exactly **five** equilateral triangular faces meet (:ref:`Chapter XXV <compendio-cap_25-en>`, :ref:`Chapter XXIX <compendio-cap_29-en>`). Join in order the five centers of the five triangles surrounding each vertex of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>` by five straight lines.
   3. Because the five triangles meeting at a vertex of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>` are mutually equal and similarly inclined around the axis passing from the center of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>` through that vertex, the five straight lines joining their centers lie in one plane perpendicular to that axis and form an **equilateral and equiangular pentagon**.
   4. Since the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>` has **twelve** vertices, and around each vertex five triangular faces meet, joining the centers around all twelve vertices produces **twelve equal regular pentagons** bounded by thirty equal edges meeting three by three at the **twenty** face-centers of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>`.

Therefore the regular :term:`duodecedron <dodecahedron>` is inscribed in the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>`, with its twenty :term:`solid angles <angulo-solido>` touching the centers of the twenty triangular bases of the :term:`ycocedron <icosahedron>`.

.. qed::
