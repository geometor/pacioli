Summa de Arithmetica, Geometria, Proportioni et Proportionalita
===============================================================

.. rubric:: Fra Luca Pacioli (Venice, 1494) — Critical Multi-Track Digital Codex & Symbolic Edition

.. container:: title-page

A folio-verified digital edition of :person:`Fra Luca Pacioli's <pacioli>` encyclopedic Renaissance synthesis, :cite:`*Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita* <pacioli:summa>` (Venice, November 10–20, 1494), uniting speculative arithmetic, Hindu–Arabic algorism, Cossic algebra (*Regola dela Cosa*), Boethian and Euclidean proportion theory, Venetian double-entry bookkeeping (*Particularis de computis et scripturis*), early probability (*Problem of Points*), and stereometry of the regular and Archimedean solids across four synchronized tracks: **English**, **Italiano (1494)**, **Summary**, and **Notes**.
