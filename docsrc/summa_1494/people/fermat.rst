Fermat, Pierre de
=================

*French Mathematician and Co-Founder of Probability and Number Theory*

Resolved both two-player and multi-player cases of Pacioli's Problem of Points in 1654 via equiprobable completion sequences and discovered Fermat's Little Theorem governing Mersenne primes (cf. Summa fol. 8r).

**Principal Works:** *Correspondence with Blaise Pascal (1654)*
