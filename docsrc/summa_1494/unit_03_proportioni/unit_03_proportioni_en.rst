UNIT III — TRACTATUS DE PROPORTIONIBUS ET PROPORTIONALITATIBUS
==============================================================

.. rubric:: Part I, Distinctio VI: Universal Praise of Proportion, the Five Boethian Genera, Classical Means, Six Euclidean Modes & the Golden Ratio (fols. 68r–98r)

.. folio:: Part I, fol. 68v

.. rubric:: Universal Sovereignty of Proportion in Philosophy, Perspective & Architecture

Opening *Distinctio VI* (`fols. 68r–68v`), :person:`Fra Luca Pacioli <pacioli>` proclaims :term:`proportion <proportione>` to be the mother and queen of every speculative and practical art: without proportion no syllogism holds in logic, no consonance sounds in music, no physician compounds medicines in due *gradus*, no painter foreshortens figures in perspective—where Pacioli salutes his fellow citizen :person:`Maestro Piero della Francesca <piero-della-francesca>` (*"el monarca a li tempi nostri de la pictura"*) and his :cite:`*De prospectiva pingendi* <piero-della-francesca:de-prospectiva-pingendi>`—and no architect erects durable temples, invoking :person:`Vitruvius <vitruvius>` (:cite:`*De Architectura* III.1 <vitruvius:de-architectura>`) and :person:`Leon Battista Alberti <alberti>` (:cite:`*De Re Aedificatoria* <alberti:de-re-aedificatoria>`).

.. folio:: Part I, fols. 70r–73r, 83r

.. rubric:: The Five Boethian Genera of Rational Ratio & Musical Compounding

Following :person:`Boethius <boethius>` (:cite:`*De Institutione Arithmetica* <boethius:de-institutione-arithmetica>` and :cite:`*De Institutione Musica* <boethius:de-institutione-musica>`) and :person:`Jordanus Nemorarius <jordanus-nemorarius>`, Pacioli divides rational :term:`ratio (*proportione*) <proportione>` into equality (*aequalitas*, $1:1$) and inequality (*inaequalitas*), subdividing greater inequality ($p > q$) into five exhaustive genera (paired with five *sub-* genera of lesser inequality):

1. :term:`**Multiplex** <multiplex>` ($n : 1$): *dupla* ($2:1$, musical diapason/octave), *tripla* ($3:1$), *quadrupla* ($4:1$).
2. :term:`**Superparticularis** <superparticularis>` ($(n + 1) : n$): *sesquialtera* ($3:2$, musical diapente/fifth), *sesquitertia* ($4:3$, diatessaron/fourth), *sesquioctava* ($9:8$, whole tone).
3. :term:`**Superpartiens** <superpartiens>` ($(n + r) : n$, $1 < r < n$, $\gcd(r, n) = 1$): *superbipartiens tertias* ($5:3$), *supertripartiens quartas* ($7:4$).
4. **Multiplex superparticularis** ($k n + 1 : n$, $k \ge 2$): *dupla sesquialtera* ($5:2$).
5. **Multiplex superpartiens** ($k n + r : n$, $k \ge 2, 1 < r < n$): *dupla supertripartiens quartas* ($11:4$).

On `fol. 83r`, Pacioli illustrates **compounded ratios** (*proportioni composte*) with arched chord diagrams: compounding *sesquialtera* ($3:2$) with *sesquitertia* ($4:3$) produces *dupla* ($\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{4}{3} = \frac{2}{1}$), while subtracting *sesquitertia* from *sesquialtera* isolates the Pythagorean whole tone *sesquioctava* ($\frac{3/2}{4/3} = \frac{9}{8}$).

.. rubric:: Semicircle Construction of the Three Classical Means (:just:`VI.13`) & Six Euclidean Modes (`fol. 74r`)

.. picture:: fig_04_three_means_semicircle.svg
   :alt: Exact semicircle construction of Arithmetic A = 13/2, Geometric G = 6, and Harmonic H = 72/13 means on segments 9 and 4

.. enunciation::

   Between any two unequal positive magnitudes $a = AP$ and $b = PB$ set end-to-end on diameter :g:`AB <summa.I.6.means:/ A B />` of semicircle :g:`(MB) <summa.I.6.means:( M B )>` with midpoint :g:`M <summa.I.6.means:M>`, a single Euclidean figure exhibits the **Arithmetic** ($A$), **Geometric** ($G$), and **Harmonic** ($H$) means and proves their geometric proportionality $G^2 = A \cdot H$.

.. assertion:: [Euclid VI.13 & VI.8 Porism] <VI.13>

   Let $AP = 9$ and $PB = 4$, so diameter :g:`AB <summa.I.6.means:/ A B />` $= 13$ and radius :g:`MQ <summa.I.6.means:/ M Q />` $= \frac{9 + 4}{2} = \frac{13}{2} = A$ (the **Arithmetic mean**). Erecting the perpendicular :g:`PQ <summa.I.6.means:/ P Q />` at :g:`P <summa.I.6.means:P>` to meet semicircle :g:`(MB) <summa.I.6.means:( M B )>` at :g:`Q <summa.I.6.means:Q>`, right triangle $\triangle AQB$ (:just:`III.31`) has altitude :g:`PQ <summa.I.6.means:/ P Q />` $= \sqrt{9 \cdot 4} = 6 = G$ (the **Geometric mean**, :just:`VI.13`). Dropping :g:`PN <summa.I.6.means:/ P N />` perpendicular to radius :g:`MQ <summa.I.6.means:/ M Q />` at :g:`N <summa.I.6.means:N>`, right triangle :g:`MPQ <summa.I.6.means:< M P Q >>` is similar to :g:`PQN <summa.I.6.means:< P Q N >>` (:just:`VI.8`), whence:

   $$PQ^2 = MQ \cdot QN \implies QN = \frac{G^2}{A} = \frac{2ab}{a + b} = \frac{72}{13} = 5\frac{7}{13} = H$$

   demonstrating visually that $A > G > H$ ($\frac{13}{2} > 6 > \frac{72}{13}$) and $G^2 = A \cdot H = 36$.

On `fol. 74r` (*De sex speciebus sive modis arguendi proportionalitatum*), Pacioli codifies the six transformational modes of :term:`proportionality <proportionalita>` from :person:`Euclid <euclid>` Book V on $a : b = c : d$: (1) **conversa** ($b:a = d:c$, :just:`V.7.por`), (2) **permutata** ($a:c = b:d$, :just:`V.16`), (3) **congionta** ($(a+b):b = (c+d):d$, :just:`V.18`), (4) **disgiunta** ($(a-b):b = (c-d):d$, :just:`V.17`), (5) **eversa** ($a:(a-b) = c:(c-d)$, :just:`V.19.por`), and (6) **equa** ($a:e = c:f$ when $b:e = d:f$, :just:`V.22`).

.. rubric:: Division of $10$ in Extreme and Mean Ratio (:just:`II.11`, :just:`VI.30`, :just:`XIII.1`)

.. picture:: fig_05_extreme_mean_10.svg
   :alt: Euclidean II.11 construction dividing AB = 10 into AC = sqrt(125) - 5 and CB = 15 - sqrt(125)

.. assertion:: [Euclid II.11 & XIII.1] <II.11>

   To divide :g:`AB <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A B />` $= 10$ in :term:`extreme and mean ratio <media-et-estrema-ragione>` so $AB : AC = AC : CB$: construct the square :g:`ABKD <summa.I.6.divina10:< A B K D >>` of side $10$, bisect $AD$ at :g:`E <summa.I.6.divina10:E>` ($AE = 5$), join hypotenuse :g:`EB <summa.I.6.divina10:/ E B />` $= \sqrt{10^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{125}$ (:just:`I.47`), swing arc :g:`(EB) <summa.I.6.divina10:( E B )>` to cut $DA$ produced at :g:`F <summa.I.6.divina10:F>` so $AF = \sqrt{125} - 5$, and transfer $AF$ via arc :g:`(AF) <summa.I.6.divina10:( A F )>` onto :g:`AB <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A B />` at :g:`C <summa.I.6.divina10:C>`, making :g:`AC <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A C />` :eq:`equal <summa.I.6.divina10:/ A C / = summa.I.6.divina10:/ A B />` in golden square :g:`ACHF <summa.I.6.divina10:< A C H F >>` to rectangle $AB \cdot CB$:

   $$AC = \sqrt{125} - 5, \qquad CB = 15 - \sqrt{125}, \qquad AB \cdot CB = AC^2 = 150 - \sqrt{12500}$$

.. qed::

   Adding half the whole ($5$) to the major :term:`apotome <residuo>` $AC = \sqrt{125} - 5$ yields $\sqrt{125}$, whose square $125$ is exact *quintupla* ($5 : 1$) to $5^2 = 25$ (:just:`XIII.1`)—the foundational seed of :ref:`*De Divina Proportione* (1509, Cap. VII–X) <compendio-cap_08-en>`.
