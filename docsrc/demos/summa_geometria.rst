Tractatus Geometriae (Part II, 1494)
====================================

Constructs and proves Heron's ``(13, 14, 15)`` triangle on *Summa* Part II, fol. 9v
(:math:`s=21`, :math:`\sqrt{6\cdot 7\cdot 8\cdot 21}=\sqrt{7056}=84`, cathetus :math:`12`,
inradius :math:`4`), proportional triangle divisions from *Distinctio IV: De divisione superficierum*,
and the exact metrics of the five Platonic and dependent Archimedean solids inscribed in a sphere
of diameter :math:`D=6` (*Distinctio VIII: De corporibus regularibus*, fols. 68v–70r).
