=====
about
=====

``geometor.pacioli`` is part of the **GEOMETOR** initiative by **phi ARCHITECT**, dedicated to the digital reconstruction and geometric analysis of Fra Luca Pacioli's treatises.

Our edition of *De Divina Proportione* synthesizes the 1509 Venice *editio princeps* printed by Paganino Paganini (featuring Leonardo da Vinci's sixty polyhedra woodcuts, the twenty-three Roman capital letter plates, and marginal Euclidean diagrams) with Constantin Winterberg's 1889 Vienna critical edition and commentary.
