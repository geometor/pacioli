Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Commensurability in Length (:math:`2:3`)**: Notice the remarkable rational consequence of composing :cite:`Campanus XV.4 <campanus:euclid:XV.4>` (:math:`s_{\text{cube}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}s_{\text{octa}}`) with :cite:`Campanus XV.2 <campanus:euclid:XV.2>` (:math:`s_{\text{tetra}} = \sqrt{2}\,s_{\text{cube}}`): the :math:`\sqrt{2}` factors cancel completely, leaving :math:`s_{\text{tetra}} = \frac{2}{3}s_{\text{octa}}`!
