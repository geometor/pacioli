CHAPTER XIII (English)
======================

.. rubric:: On the Intervals Between One Triglyph and Another.

What we have just said concerning the spacing of columns applies equally to the spacing of the **triglyphs** (*tigrafi*), which are placed near the summit of buildings upon the frieze above the epistyle (architrave):

.. assertion:: Alignment and Spacing of Triglyphs

   1. Every triglyph must be centered **directly over the axis of the column** beneath it (and with corresponding intervals over the intercolumniations), so that the upper order answers visually and structurally to the columns below.
   2. Thus if the intercolumniation (*tetrante*) of the columns is :math:`1\frac{1}{2}`, :math:`2`, or :math:`2\frac{1}{2}` column thicknesses, the spacing of the triglyphs is governed accordingly.
   3. And **in no way does Vitruvius commend an unreinforced stone span of 3 column thicknesses** (*diastyle*), because under so wide an interval stone epistyles crack and break of their own weight, as you will hear immediately in the next chapter!

.. qed::
