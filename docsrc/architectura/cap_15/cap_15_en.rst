CHAPTER XV (English)
====================

.. rubric:: On the Zophorus (Frieze) Above the Epistyle.

.. container:: track-nav

   **English** · :ref:`Italiano (1509) <architectura-cap_15-it>` · :ref:`Deutsch (1889) <architectura-cap_15-de>` · :ref:`Notes <architectura-cap_15-notes>` | :ref:`Conspectus <architectura-index>` · :ref:`← Prev <architectura-cap_14-en>` · :ref:`Next → <architectura-cap_16-en>`

.. folio:: 0079 (f. 32r)
   :initial: 0079.c1.png

Directly upon the top taenia (:math:`h`) of the epistyle rests the **zophorus** (:term:`*zophoro* <zophoro>`, marked :math:`V` in :ref:`Plate 0215 <plates-architectural-en>`), which our modern craftsmen call the **frieze** (:term:`*fregio* <zophoro>`).

.. assertion:: Proportion of the Frieze (*Zophoro*) to the Epistyle (Vitruvius III.5.10)

   1. **When Left Plain Without Carved Ornament (*Schietto senza Ornamenti*)**:
      If the frieze is to be left smooth and uncarved, its height is made **one-fourth less** than the height of the epistyle below it (or in small plain mouldings, a quarter part).
   2. **When Carved with Relief Ornaments (*Con Adornamenti*, :math:`5 : 4` Ratio)**:
      If the frieze is to be carved with **foliage, scrolling vines (*viticci*), animals, or figures** (as the Greek name *zophoros*, "animal-bearing," signifies, and as shown on :ref:`Plate 0215 <plates-architectural-en>` and :ref:`Plate 0185 (Porta Speciosa) <plates-architectural-en>`), it must be made **one-fourth (:math:`\frac{1}{4}`) taller than the epistyle** (*el quarto più largo del suo :term:`epistilio <epistilio>`*)—that is, in the *sesquiquarta* ratio of **5 to 4**:

      .. math::

         \text{Height}_{\text{carved frieze}} = \frac{5}{4}\,\text{Height}_{\text{epistyle}}

      so that if the epistyle is **4** units high, the carved frieze :math:`V` is **5** units high, allowing its carved ornaments to be seen clearly and gracefully both from afar and up close!
