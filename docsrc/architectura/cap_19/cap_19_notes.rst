Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Piero della Francesca's *Legend of the True Cross* at Arezzo and *De Prospectiva Pingendi* at Urbino**: This passage on Folio 0081 (``f. 33r``) is one of the earliest and most authoritative Renaissance tributes to **Piero della Francesca** (c. 1415–1492), listing every major city where Piero worked (**Urbino, Bologna, Ferrara, Rimini, Ancona, Sansepolcro, and Arezzo**), noting his mastery of both **oil and tempera/gouache on wall and panel** (*in muro e taula a oglio e guazzo*), singling out the **Cappella Maggiore of San Francesco in Arezzo** (*la magna capella dela tribuna del'altar grande*) as *"vna dele dignissime opere de Italia e da tutti commendata"*, and citing Piero's manuscript treatise *De Prospectiva Pingendi* in the Ducal Library of Urbino!
