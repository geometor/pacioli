CHAPTER X (English)
===================

**Pyramids**, both **round** (cones) and **lateral** (polygonal pyramids and obelisks/spires), will likewise be easy for you to fashion and proportion from their corresponding columns, seeing that—as our **Euclid** proves demonstratively (:just:`XII.7`, :just:`XII.10`) and as we explained in :ref:`Chapters LXIV–LXVIII <compendio-cap_64-en>`—**every pyramid, whether in weight (*al peso*) or in solid measure (*ala mesura*), is always in every way exactly one-third (:math:`\frac{1}{3}`) of its cylinder or column** having the same base and height!

Their order and perspective figures you have above in this volume (:ref:`Plates XLIII–LIX <plates-polyhedra-en>`) together with all the other regular and dependent bodies, drawn by the hand of our aforesaid fellow countryman **Leonardo da Vinci of Florence** (*per mano del prelibato nostro compatriota Leonardo da Vinci Fiorentino*)—**against whose drawings and figures there was never in truth any man who could raise an objection** (*ali cui disegni e figure mai con verità fo homo li potesse oponere*)!
