Historical & Mathematical Commentary
------------------------------------

- **Vitruvius's Attic Base (Vitruvius III.5.2)**: Pacioli's step-by-step arithmetic for the Attic column base (:ref:`Plate 0213 <plates-architectural-en>`) follows Vitruvius III.5.2–3: total base height :math:`= \frac{1}{2}D`; plinth width :math:`= 1\frac{1}{2}D`; plinth height :math:`= \frac{1}{3}` of base height (:math:`= \frac{1}{6}D`), leaving :math:`\frac{2}{3}` of base height (:math:`= \frac{1}{3}D`), of which :math:`\frac{1}{4}` is the upper torus and the remaining :math:`\frac{3}{4}` is split equally (:math:`\frac{3}{8}` each) between the lower torus and the scotia (*trochilus*) with its fillets.
